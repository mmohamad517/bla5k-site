# ── bla5k.com — unified robots policy (single source of truth) ──────────────
# bla5k is a public directory. Every crawler — search engines and AI search
# engines alike — is welcome to index, reference, and quote our content.
#
# The Content-Signal line is a machine-readable copyright reservation
# (per EU Directive 2019/790, Art. 4):
#   search=yes    → may build a search index & show excerpts
#   ai-input=yes  → may use our content as input for AI answers (RAG/grounding)
#   ai-train=no   → must NOT train or fine-tune models on our content
#   use=reference → may index, excerpt, and link back
#
# Business decision (Aug 2026): we want bla5k visible in AI search engines
# (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, …) while reserving the right
# to opt out of AI training — that opt-out lives in ai-train=no, not in
# blocking the crawlers themselves.
User-agent: *
Allow: /
Content-Signal: search=yes, ai-input=yes, ai-train=no, use=reference
