## What is Stable Diffusion?

Stable Diffusion is an **open-source text-to-image AI model** released by Stability AI in 2022. It was a breakthrough moment: previously, generating images with AI required expensive closed services. Stable Diffusion's weights were released openly, letting anyone run it, modify it, and build on it.

The model works by learning the relationship between text descriptions and images, then generating new images that match your prompt. Its open nature spawned an enormous ecosystem:

- **Thousands of custom models and styles** (LoRA, checkpoints)
- **Tools** for inpainting, outpainting, and upscaling
- **Free platforms** like this one that run it for you

Stable Diffusion Online is one such platform: **the full model, free, in your browser.**

## How to get great results

1. **Be specific** — "a watercolor painting of a lighthouse at sunset, seagulls, warm light" beats "lighthouse."
2. **Add style words** — art styles, lighting, and camera terms steer the result.
3. **Use negative prompts** — exclude what you don't want (blurry, low quality).
4. **Iterate** — generate several versions and refine the best one.
5. **Try models** — different checkpoints give different aesthetics.

Prompt quality is 80% of the result. Describe composition, style, and lighting like you're directing a photographer.
