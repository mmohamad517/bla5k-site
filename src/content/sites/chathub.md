Every AI model answers differently — one writes better copy, another codes better, a third thinks more creatively. **ChatHub** lets you see all of them at once: type one prompt and compare ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, and 20+ models side by side. This guide covers what ChatHub does, how it works, and why comparing models is worth your time.

For more AI tools, explore the [AI Tools directory](/category/ai-tools/) and our [best AI tools in 2026](/guides/best-ai-tools-2026/) roundup.

## What is ChatHub?

ChatHub is a **browser extension that compares AI chatbots side by side**. Instead of opening ChatGPT in one tab, Claude in another, and Gemini in a third, ChatHub lets you send a single prompt to multiple models at once and view all the responses together.

It supports **20+ AI services**, including:

- **ChatGPT** (all models)
- **Claude** (Anthropic)
- **Gemini** (Google)
- **Grok** (xAI)
- And many more

It works as a Chrome/Edge extension, and offers an all-in-one chat mode where you can use all your AI accounts from a single window.

## How ChatHub works

The comparison workflow is elegant:

1. **Install the extension** and connect your AI accounts (ChatGPT, Claude, etc.).
2. **Type one prompt** in the ChatHub panel.
3. **Select the models** you want to compare.
4. **ChatHub sends the prompt to all of them simultaneously.**
5. **Responses appear side by side** for instant comparison.

Because you're using your own accounts, you get the full power of each model — ChatHub just orchestrates them. No single AI is hidden behind a weaker proxy.

## Key features of ChatHub

### Side-by-side model comparison

The core feature: **one prompt, many answers, side by side**. See exactly how models differ in quality, tone, and approach for your specific task.

### 20+ AI services supported

Compare across **ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, and more** — the broadest multi-model comparison available.

### All-in-one chat

Beyond comparison, use **all your AI services from a single window** — no more switching between tabs and accounts.

### Prompt testing and refinement

Perfect for **developers and prompt engineers** iterating on prompts: test variations across models and pick the best-performing version.

### Uses your own accounts

No middleman quota: ChatHub connects to **your existing AI subscriptions**, so you get full model access.

## Who is ChatHub for?

- **Curious users** evaluating which AI to subscribe to.
- **Prompt engineers and developers** refining prompts.
- **Researchers** comparing model answers.
- **Content creators** finding the best AI for each type of content.

It's **not** for casual single-AI users who don't care about comparison, or for users who want image generation or voice chat. If you already know your one favorite model, [HotBot](/tools/hotbot/) or a dedicated chatbot may be simpler.

## How to get started with ChatHub

1. **Install ChatHub** from the Chrome Web Store (or Edge Add-ons).
2. **Connect your accounts** — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and others.
3. **Type a prompt** and select multiple models.
4. **Compare the responses** side by side.
5. **Refine your prompt** based on what you see.

Start with a prompt you actually care about — the comparison is most useful when you're evaluating real work, not test questions.

## The bottom line

ChatHub answers the question everyone eventually asks: **which AI is actually best for my work?** By showing ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and 20+ models answering the same prompt side by side, it turns a subjective debate into an instant visual comparison. If you use multiple AIs — or are deciding which to use — it's an essential tool.

For the rest of your AI stack, combine ChatHub with [Monica](/tools/monica/) or [Sider](/tools/sider-ai/) for browsing assistance, and [NotebookLM](/tools/notebooklm/) for research.
