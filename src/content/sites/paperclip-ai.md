## What is PaperClip?

PaperClip is an **AI-powered document management platform** that transforms how teams work with their files. Instead of folders, filenames, and manual searching, PaperClip understands the content of your documents and lets you query them conversationally.

Core capabilities:

- **Document organization** — upload and manage all your files
- **Natural-language search** — ask questions, get answers
- **Insight extraction** — surface information across documents
- **Workflow support** — built for business and legal teams

It's positioned for document-heavy organizations where the cost of finding information — contracts, policies, research — is real and recurring.

## How PaperClip works

The workflow replaces folder-drilling with conversation:

1. **Upload your documents** — contracts, PDFs, policies, research.
2. **PaperClip indexes** them — AI reads and understands the content.
3. **Ask questions** — "which contracts expire this quarter?"
4. **Get answers** — extracted from your documents with context.
5. **Search naturally** — refine and follow up conversationally.

The shift is fundamental: from "where is the file?" to "what does it say?" — and PaperClip answers the second question directly.

## Key features of PaperClip

### Semantic document search

Search **by meaning, not keywords** — ask questions and get the relevant passages, even when your words don't match the document's exactly.

### Question answering from your files

Get **direct answers extracted from your documents**, with context you can verify — like a research assistant who has read everything.

### Cross-document insights

Compare and synthesize **information across many documents** — find patterns, extract terms, summarize trends.

### Team workflows

Built for **business and legal teams** with document-heavy processes and knowledge management needs.

## Who is PaperClip for?

- **Legal teams** managing contracts and case documents.
- **Business teams** drowning in policies and reports.
- **Knowledge managers** building searchable org knowledge.
- **Researchers** working across large document sets.

It's **not** for simple file storage, personal note-taking, or casual use — those are better served by Drive, Notion, or [NotebookLM](/tools/notebooklm/). It's for teams whose documents are their business.
