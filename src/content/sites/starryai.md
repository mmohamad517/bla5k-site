AI art was made popular on desktops — but most people live on their phones. **Starryai** brings AI art generation to iOS and Android: type a prompt, pick a style, and get a stunning AI-generated artwork in seconds, anywhere. This guide covers what Starryai is, how it works, and who it's for.

## What is Starryai?

Starryai is a **mobile AI art generator app** that turns text prompts into AI-generated artwork. It was one of the early mobile apps to bring text-to-image AI to everyday users, and it remains popular for its accessibility and style variety.

With Starryai you can:

- **Type a prompt** describing your image
- **Choose styles and presets** — from anime to photorealism
- **Generate variations** of your best results
- **Share directly** to social media
- **Create on the go** — no desktop required

It's designed around one idea: making AI art creation as easy as sending a text message.

## How Starryai works

The creation loop takes seconds:

1. **Open the app** and start a new creation.
2. **Type your prompt** — describe the scene, subject, and mood.
3. **(Optional) Pick a style** — preset or describe your own.
4. **Generate** — Starryai creates your artwork.
5. **Refine** — try variations or tweak the prompt.
6. **Save and share** — export or post directly.

The app handles the AI complexity behind the scenes — you just describe and tap.

## Who is Starryai for?

- **Mobile users** who want AI art anywhere.

## How to get started with Starryai

1. **Download the app** from the App Store or Google Play.
2. **Create an account** and claim your free credits.
3. **Type a prompt** — start simple, then add detail.
4. **Try a style preset** — see how it changes the result.
5. **Generate variations** — refine your favorite.
6. **Share your creation** — or save it to your gallery.

Start with a specific, descriptive prompt and one style preset — the combination usually produces impressive first results.
