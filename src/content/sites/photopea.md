## What is PhotoPea?

PhotoPea is a **free, browser-based image editor** that mimics Adobe Photoshop's interface and capabilities. Created by Ivan Kutskir and launched in 2013, it's grown into the most capable free Photoshop alternative available — no installation, no account, no payment.

Its headline capability is **PSD compatibility**: PhotoPea opens, edits, and saves Adobe Photoshop files, preserving layers, masks, and effects. It also supports:

- **XCF files** (GIMP's format)
- **Sketch files** (design tool format)
- Standard formats: PNG, JPG, SVG, WebP, GIF, TIFF
- Layers, masks, blend modes, filters, and adjustment layers

It's essentially a professional layer-based editor running entirely in your browser tab.

## How PhotoPea works

PhotoPea works like a desktop editor, but simpler to start:

1. **Open photopea.com** — nothing to install or sign up for.
2. **Open a file** — PSD, XCF, Sketch, or any standard format, from your device or a URL.
3. **Edit with layers** — the interface mirrors Photoshop: layers panel, tools, filters.
4. **Use the pro features** — masks, blend modes, adjustment layers, smart objects.
5. **Export** — save as PSD, PNG, JPG, WebP, SVG, and more.

Because it runs in the browser, it works on any modern computer — Windows, Mac, Linux, even Chromebooks — with no downloads or license keys.

## Key features of PhotoPea

## Who is PhotoPea for?

- **Anyone who receives PSD files** without owning Photoshop.
- **Students** learning layer-based editing.
- **Freelancers and designers** on a budget.
- **Chromebook and Linux users** who can't run Photoshop.

It's **not** for users who want a simple one-click editor (try [Microsoft Designer](/tools/microsoft-designer/) or [Canva](https://www.canva.com/)), or for professional workflows needing 3D, video, or extensive plugin ecosystems.

## How to get started with PhotoPea

1. **Open photopea.com** in your browser.
2. **Open a PSD or image file** — or start a new document with preset sizes.
3. **Explore the layers panel** — this is where the power lives.
4. **Try the tools** — selection, brush, clone, text, and filters.
5. **Save your work** — export as PSD to keep editing later, or PNG/JPG to share.

If you know Photoshop, you already know PhotoPea — the layout is nearly identical. If you're new, the layers panel is the key concept to learn first.
