## What is Cognito?

Cognito is a **free revision platform for UK students** studying GCSE and A-Level. It combines short, focused video lessons with practice questions and AI-adaptive quizzes — a complete revision cycle built around the UK curriculum.

Core features:

- **Short video lessons** — every topic, explained clearly
- **Exam questions** — practice in exam style
- **AI-powered quizzes** — adaptive testing
- **Curriculum mapping** — aligned to UK exam boards

It's built by educators who understand exactly what UK students need to pass their exams.

## How Cognito works

The revision cycle is complete:

3. **Practice with questions** — exam-style testing.

5. **Track progress** — know when you're ready.

Learn, practice, test, improve — the full cycle in one free platform.
