## What is HomeByMe?

HomeByMe is a free online tool for designing and visualizing home interiors in 3D. You
draw your floor plan in 2D, furnish and decorate it in 3D, then generate photorealistic
4K images of the result — all in your browser, with no training or design background
required.

## What you can do with it

- **Draw real floor plans** — lay out rooms accurately in an intuitive 2D editor.
- **Decorate in 3D** — fill your space with real, branded furniture, rugs, and decor.
- **Render in 4K** — produce ultra-realistic images to judge colors and layouts before you buy.
- **Share and get inspired** — publish designs and browse a community gallery of ideas.

## Why it belongs in this directory

Whether you're planning a renovation, staging a room to sell, or just dreaming, HomeByMe
takes the guesswork out of home design. It's genuinely free and approachable enough for
complete beginners, yet powerful enough to produce convincing visuals — so you can see a
room before spending a cent on furniture.

> **Tip:** Get your room dimensions right in the 2D floor plan first — everything you place in 3D depends on that accurate base.
