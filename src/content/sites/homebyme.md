Redesigning a room or planning a renovation usually means guessing. Will that sofa fit? Does this paint color work with the flooring? HomeByMe removes the guesswork by letting you design, furnish, and visualize your home in realistic 3D — entirely free, in your browser, with no design background required. It is one of the most approachable ways to see a space before you spend a cent on it.

## What is HomeByMe?

HomeByMe is a free online tool for designing and visualizing home interiors in 3D. You draw your floor plan in 2D, furnish and decorate it in 3D, then generate photorealistic images of the result — all in your browser. It bridges the gap between imagining a space and seeing it, giving everyday people the kind of visualization power that used to require professional software.

The workflow is designed to feel natural: lay out your rooms accurately, drop in furniture and decor, adjust colors and materials, and then render the scene to see how it all comes together under realistic lighting.

## Who develops HomeByMe

HomeByMe is developed by Dassault Systèmes, a major software company renowned for its professional 3D design, engineering, and simulation tools used across industries. HomeByMe channels that deep 3D expertise into a consumer-friendly product, which is a large part of why its visualizations look so convincing.

## How HomeByMe works

The process follows three intuitive stages:

1. **Draw your floor plan** in the 2D editor, laying out rooms, walls, doors, and windows to real dimensions.
2. **Furnish and decorate in 3D**, filling the space with furniture, rugs, lighting, and decor from a catalog of real, often branded items.
3. **Render in high resolution**, generating photorealistic images that reveal how colors, materials, and layouts actually look.

Because the 3D space is built on an accurate 2D base, everything you place reflects true proportions — so the final visualization is genuinely useful for real decisions.

## Key features

### Accurate 2D floor planning
Lay out rooms precisely in an intuitive editor, establishing the true dimensions everything else depends on.

### 3D furnishing with real products
Decorate your space with real, branded furniture and decor, so your design reflects items you could actually buy.

### Photorealistic rendering
Produce ultra-realistic images — including high-resolution renders — to judge colors, lighting, and layouts before committing.

### Community and inspiration
Publish your designs and browse a gallery of others' projects to spark ideas and see what is possible.

### Browser-based and saved online
Everything runs in the browser with projects stored online, so you can pick up where you left off from any device.

## What you can do with it

- **Plan a renovation** and test layouts before hiring anyone.
- **Arrange furniture virtually** to confirm it fits and flows before buying.
- **Stage a room for sale** to help buyers picture the space.
- **Test paint, materials, and decor** choices risk-free.
- **Experiment with interior design** purely for the joy of it.

## Who is it for?

HomeByMe suits homeowners, renters, and anyone planning a space: people mid-renovation, first-time decorators, sellers staging a property, and design enthusiasts who simply enjoy dreaming up rooms. It is approachable enough for total beginners yet capable enough to produce visuals convincing enough to guide real purchases.

## Pricing

HomeByMe provides a free plan covering the core experience — floor planning, 3D furnishing, and a number of realistic images. Additional or higher-quality renders and certain advanced features may use credits or a paid upgrade. For casual planning and visualization, the free tier is genuinely capable, which is a major part of its appeal.

## Advantages

- **Genuinely free** for the core design experience.
- **No installation** — runs entirely in the browser.
- **Beginner-friendly** with no CAD knowledge required.
- **Photorealistic output** backed by serious 3D technology.
- **Real, branded furnishings** that reflect purchasable items.

## Limitations

- **Rendering can take time**, and the best-quality renders may be limited or paid.
- **Large or complex projects** can feel demanding in a browser.
- **Catalog constraints** mean not every real-world product is available.
- **A learning curve** exists for getting layouts and materials exactly right.

## Alternatives and comparisons

Alternatives include tools like Planner 5D, Roomstyler, and SketchUp for varying levels of complexity and control. HomeByMe's advantage is the combination of an approachable, free, browser-based experience with the photorealistic rendering quality that comes from Dassault Systèmes' 3D heritage.

## Conclusion

HomeByMe takes the risk and guesswork out of home design. By letting you build an accurate floor plan, furnish it in 3D with real products, and render photorealistic images — all free and in your browser — it lets you truly see a room before spending money on it. For anyone planning a renovation, a move, or simply a refresh, it is a genuinely useful and delightful tool.

> **Tip:** Get your room dimensions right in the 2D floor plan first — everything you place in 3D depends on that accurate base.
