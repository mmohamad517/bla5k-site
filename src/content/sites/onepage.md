## What is Onepage?

Onepage is a **one-page website builder** that lets small businesses and individuals create professional websites without coding. Its approach is simple: instead of a complex drag-and-drop canvas, Onepage asks questions about your business and generates a complete single-page site.

The core idea:

- **One-page format** — everything on a single scrolling page
- **Questionnaire-based setup** — describe your business, get a site
- **No code** — design, content, and layout handled for you
- **Simplified pricing** — often one-time payment instead of subscriptions

It's built for people who need "a website" — clean, presentable, and live — without the complexity of full website platforms.

## How Onepage works

The setup is unusually fast:

1. **Answer a questionnaire** — what's your business, services, contact info.
2. **Pick a design** — choose from templates that suit your industry.
3. **Review the generated site** — content and sections auto-populated.
4. **Customize** — tweak text, colors, and images.
5. **Publish** — your one-page site goes live with a custom domain option.

The questionnaire-driven flow is what sets Onepage apart — most of the site builds itself from your answers.
