Selling online is brutally visual: the product photo decides the sale. But not everyone has a studio, and that's the gap **Photoroom** fills. It's an AI photo editor designed specifically for ecommerce sellers — remove a background, drop a product onto a clean studio backdrop, and produce consistent, market-ready photos from your phone. No lights, no backdrop paper, no Photoshop degree.

This guide covers what Photoroom is, how it works, its pricing, and who gets the most from it.

## What is Photoroom?

Photoroom is an **AI photo editing app built for ecommerce**. Its flagship feature is background removal — but it goes far beyond: AI-generated backgrounds, product scene creation, batch editing, and tools that keep your entire catalog visually consistent.

It started as "background remover for sellers" and grew into a complete product-photography toolkit that replaced studio work for millions of small businesses.

## How Photoroom works

1. **Photograph your product** — even with a phone, on any background.
2. **Remove the background** — one tap with AI precision (including tricky edges like hair or transparent objects).
3. **Choose a background** — clean white, a studio style, a template, or an AI-generated scene from a prompt.
4. **Batch and export** — process many photos at once and export at marketplace-ready sizes.

The core promise: **from phone photo to listing-ready in under a minute.**

## Key features

### Background removal
Fast, accurate AI cutouts — the app's foundation.

### AI backgrounds and scenes
Generate or choose backdrops that match your brand.

### Batch editing
Apply consistent edits across entire product catalogs.

### Marketplace templates
Sizes and formats matched to Amazon, Etsy, eBay, and Shopify.

### Brand kits (Pro)
Consistent styles and logos across all your listings.

### Mobile-first
Full power in the iOS and Android apps.

## Who is Photoroom for?

- **Ecommerce sellers** on Amazon, Etsy, eBay, and Shopify.
- **Small businesses** without a photography budget.
- **Marketplace resellers** processing large catalogs.
- **Marketers** needing clean product visuals for ads and social.

## Pricing

Photoroom is **freemium**. The free tier includes background removal and basic tools with export limits and watermarks on some features. **Pro** and **Business** plans remove limits and add batch processing, brand kits, AI generation, and team features. Confirm the current free-tier limits at photoroom.com.

## Advantages

- **Professional results without a studio.**
- **Fast and batch-friendly** — built for catalog volumes.
- **Mobile workflow** — shoot with your phone, edit on the spot.
- **Consistency tools** — uniform photos across your store.
- **Real free tier** to evaluate quality.

## Limitations and honest considerations

- **Focus is products** — advanced people-retouching and creative editing aren't its strength.
- **Subscription for serious use** — catalog sellers will need Pro.
- **AI backgrounds can look generic** if overused — blend with brand style.
- **Check resolution limits** for very large formats.
