## What is Vectorizer AI?

Vectorizer AI is an **AI-powered tool that converts raster images to SVG vectors**. Raster images (PNG, JPG) are made of pixels and degrade when scaled; vector graphics use mathematical paths and stay sharp at any size.

The tool handles the conversion automatically:

- **Upload a raster image** — PNG, JPG, and other formats
- **AI traces the shapes** — clean, accurate vector paths
- **Download an SVG** — scalable and editable

It replaces the manual tracing process that designers used to do by hand in software like Adobe Illustrator.
