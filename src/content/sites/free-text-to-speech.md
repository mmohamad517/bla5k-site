## What is Free Text-to-Speech?

Free Text-to-Speech (freetts.com) is a **browser-based tool that converts written text into spoken audio**. You paste your text, choose a voice, and download the generated speech as an audio file — no software, no payment, no installation.

It's part of the modern wave of online TTS tools that leverage neural speech synthesis — the same technology behind voice assistants — to produce natural-sounding narration from plain text.

Typical uses:

- **Video narration** — voiceover for YouTube and social videos
- **Podcast audio** — spoken versions of written content
- **E-learning** — narration for courses and tutorials
- **Accessibility** — listening to text instead of reading
- **Language learning** — hearing correct pronunciation

## How it works

The process takes under a minute:

1. **Open freetts.com** in your browser.
2. **Paste your text** into the input box.
3. **Pick a voice** — choose from the available voices and adjust speed if needed.
4. **Generate** — the tool synthesizes the speech.
5. **Download** — save the audio file for your project.

Everything happens in the browser, so there's nothing to install and no learning curve.
