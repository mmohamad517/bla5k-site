The web is full of long articles, foreign-language pages, and confusing docs — and switching to a separate AI tab breaks your flow. **Sider** fixes that with an AI sidebar that lives in your browser: highlight any text and summarize, translate, explain, or rewrite it instantly, with 30+ models behind it. This guide covers what Sider is, how it works, and whether it's worth adding to your browser.

## What is Sider?

Sider is an **AI-powered browser extension** that adds a smart sidebar to your browser, giving you instant access to 30+ AI models wherever you browse. It launched in 2022 and quickly became one of the most popular AI extensions on the Chrome Web Store.

Instead of opening a separate AI chat tab, Sider works **in context**: you're reading a page, highlight text, and the AI responds right beside it. Its core tools include:

- **Summarize** — long articles, videos, and PDFs
- **Translate** — any highlighted text, with model choices
- **Explain** — complex passages in simple words
- **Rewrite** — improve tone, clarity, or length
- **Ask anything** — a full chatbot in the sidebar

It aggregates models from ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, and more, so you get variety without juggling multiple apps.

## How Sider works

The workflow is built around your existing browsing:

1. **Install the extension** and pin the sidebar.
2. **Highlight any text** on any web page.
3. **Choose an action** — summarize, translate, explain, rewrite.
4. **Sider responds instantly** in the sidebar, right beside the text.
5. **Or open the chat** — ask follow-ups without leaving the page.

Because it lives in the browser, Sider removes the friction of copying text, opening ChatGPT, pasting, and switching back. Highlight → command → done.

## Key features of Sider

### Instant text actions

Summarize, translate, explain, and rewrite **any highlighted text** without leaving the page — the core workflow that saves the most time.

### 30+ models in one place

Access **ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, and more** from a single sidebar, with the ability to pick models or compare answers.

### Whole-page understanding

Sider summarizes **entire pages, PDFs, and YouTube videos**, not just selections — perfect for research and digesting long content.

### Sidebar chat

A full **AI chat in the sidebar** that keeps your browsing context, so you can ask questions about what you're viewing without switching tabs.

### Multilingual support

Strong **translation and cross-language assistance**, useful for reading foreign-language sites and working across languages.

## Who is Sider for?

- **Students and researchers** digesting lots of reading.
- **Professionals** dealing with long reports and documents.
- **Multilingual users** translating content constantly.
- **Content consumers** who want AI in their browsing flow.

It's **not** for Firefox/Safari users (Chrome/Edge only), or for heavy daily use on the free tier (quotas apply). For deep one-on-one conversations, a full chatbot like [DeepSeek](/tools/deepseek/) or [Qwen Chat](/tools/qwen-chat/) is a better fit.

## How to get started with Sider

1. **Install Sider** from the Chrome Web Store.
2. **Pin the sidebar** icon for one-click access.
3. **Highlight text** on any page and try summarize or translate.
4. **Configure your favorite model** in settings.
5. **Try page summarization** — great for long articles and videos.
6. **Upgrade** only if your daily quota feels limiting.

Start with the free tier and the summarize command — once you feel the flow, the rest of the features follow naturally.
