Netflix invented streaming as we know it — and in 2026 it's still the biggest name in the game, with a massive library of original series, blockbuster movies, live events, and even mobile games. This guide covers current pricing, what you get, and honestly whether it's worth it compared to free and other paid services.

## What is Netflix?

Netflix is the **world's largest streaming subscription service**. For a monthly fee, you get unlimited access to a library of licensed movies and shows plus an unmatched catalog of **Netflix Originals** — series, films, documentaries, and stand-up specials produced by Netflix that you can't watch anywhere else.

Beyond on-demand content, Netflix now includes **live events** (sports entertainment, specials, comedy), **mobile games**, and expanding interactive experiences — all inside one subscription.

## How Netflix works

1. **Pick a plan** — from $8.99/month with ads to $26.99/month in 4K.
2. **Create profiles** — separate watch histories for family members.
3. **Browse and stream** — unlimited movies and series on demand.
4. **Watch anywhere** — phone, tablet, TV, laptop, with downloads offline.
5. **Enjoy extras** — live events, games, and interactive content included.

## Key features of Netflix

### The biggest original library

**Netflix Originals** are the reason to subscribe: high-budget series, blockbuster films, documentaries, and stand-up specials exclusive to the platform.

### Unlimited streaming

**Watch as much as you want** on a flat fee — no per-title costs, no rental fees, no ads on mid-tier and premium plans.

### Live events included

**Live streaming at no extra cost** — sports entertainment, live specials, and comedy events built into the subscription.

### Mobile games

An expanding library of **exclusive mobile games** included free in the iOS and Android apps.

### Downloads and profiles

**Offline downloads** plus **individual profiles** with personalized recommendations for each family member.

## Who is Netflix for?

- **Heavy streamers** — hours of content a week.
- **Series bingers** — Netflix originals you can't get elsewhere.
- **Families** — profiles, parental controls, and broad genres.
- **Live-event fans** — sports entertainment and specials included.

It's **not** for light viewers who'd be fine with free services, or for budget-conscious users — the cheapest plan still costs more than nothing.

## Why Netflix is 100% legal

Netflix is a **publicly traded company** and the most established streaming service in the world. Every title — licensed movies and shows plus Netflix Originals — is distributed under formal agreements with studios, and its own productions are made by Netflix itself. There's nothing pirated or unlicensed anywhere on the platform.

This matters because "watch movies online" searches are full of sketchy sites. Netflix is the opposite: a regulated, mainstream company whose entire catalog is rights-cleared, which is why it's safe to recommend without qualification.

## How to get started with Netflix

1. **Choose a plan** — start with Standard with Ads to test value.
2. **Create your profile** — personal recommendations from day one.
3. **Watch an original** — try a flagship series to feel the difference.
4. **Download for offline** — on mobile or tablet before travel.
5. **Upgrade later** — to 4K or ad-free if the ads bother you.

## The bottom line

Netflix in 2026 is **the subscription for people who watch a lot** — the largest original library, live events, and mobile games justify the fee for heavy users. Light viewers should start with free services like Tubi, and movie lovers who only want new releases can rent on Fandango instead.
