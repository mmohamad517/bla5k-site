## What is Dubbing AI?

Dubbing AI is a **voice-cloning dubbing platform** that translates videos into 30+ languages with the original speaker's voice preserved. Instead of robotic generic voices, the dubbing sounds like the actual person speaking another language.

Core features:

- **Voice cloning** — your voice in every language
- **30+ language support** — global reach
- **Video translation** — spoken content translated
- **Creator focus** — built for YouTube, courses, and marketing

For creators, it converts a single video into a global asset.

## How Dubbing AI works

The pipeline is automatic:

1. **Upload your video**.
2. **Choose the target language(s)**.
3. **AI clones your voice** — from the original audio.
4. **AI translates and re-voices** — in your voice, the new language.
5. **Download** — the dubbed video, ready to publish.

The voice clone is the differentiator — dubbed videos sound personal, not robotic.
