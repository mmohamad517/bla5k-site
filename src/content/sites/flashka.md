## What is Flashka?

Flashka is an **AI flashcard generator** that turns text into study decks instantly. It addresses the most common study failure: knowing flashcards work, but not making them.

Core promise:

- **Paste text** — notes, chapters, articles
- **Get flashcards** — generated in seconds
- **Study ready** — decks covering key concepts
- **Zero card-making effort** — AI does the work

It's built for students who want the benefits of flashcards without the hours of card creation.

## How Flashka works

The workflow is faster than making one card by hand:

1. **Paste your text** — notes, a chapter, or an article.
2. **Flashka analyzes** — AI identifies key concepts and facts.
3. **Cards are generated** — Q&A flashcards covering the material.
4. **Review and study** — flip through your ready deck.
5. **Export or repeat** — study your deck or generate more.

The AI handles the analysis and phrasing — you handle the actual studying.
