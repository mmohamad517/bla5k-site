The old story: building an app means months of learning, or paying a developer. **Bolt.new** rewrote that story: type what you want, and it builds a real, working full-stack app in your browser — instantly, deployable, and endlessly editable by chat. It's one of the most impressive AI "build anything" tools to emerge, and it's changing who gets to call themselves an app builder.

This guide covers what Bolt.new is, how it works, its pricing, and its honest limits.

## What is Bolt.new?

Bolt.new is an **AI-powered app builder** by StackBlitz. You describe an application in natural language, and Bolt generates the complete codebase — frontend, backend, database schema, styling — runs it in a browser-based environment, and lets you refine it through conversation.

It's different from a chatbot that prints code: Bolt **executes** the code in your browser, so you can see the app work, interact with it, and deploy it — all without leaving the browser.

## How Bolt.new works

1. **Describe your app** — "a to-do app with categories and dark mode" or "a landing page for my podcast."
2. **Bolt generates the app** — complete, runnable code.
3. **See it live** — the app runs in an integrated browser preview.
4. **Refine by chatting** — "make the header sticky," "add a login," "change the colors."
5. **Deploy** — push it to a live URL in one click.

The conversation is the IDE: you steer, it builds, you watch.

## Key features

### Full-stack generation
Real apps with frontend, backend, and data — not just static mockups.

### Live browser preview
See the app running as it's built, and interact with it.

### Chat-driven editing
Iterate by describing changes in plain language.

### Deployment built in
From prompt to live URL without touching a server.

### Modern stacks
Generates professional code (React, Node, etc.) you can export and keep.

### WebContainer technology
The entire dev environment runs in the browser — no installs.

## Who is Bolt.new for?

- **Founders and non-developers** who want to validate ideas with real apps.
- **Developers** who want to skip boilerplate and prototype fast.
- **Designers** turning concepts into working products.
- **Learners** who want to see how apps are built by example.

## Pricing

Bolt.new is **freemium**: a free tier with limited daily tokens for building and testing, and paid plans (Pro and higher) with more tokens, faster builds, private projects, and custom domains. Token limits matter for large apps, so check bolt.new for current pricing.

## Advantages

- **Prompt to working app in minutes** — astonishing speed.
- **No environment setup** — everything runs in the browser.
- **Deployable** — not just a demo; real URLs.
- **Iterative** — chat-driven refinement feels natural.
- **Exports real code** — nothing locks you in.

## Limitations and honest considerations

- **Not for massive systems** — complex production architecture still needs engineers.
- **Token limits** — big apps and heavy iteration consume tokens fast.
- **Quality varies** — great for prototypes and MVPs; polish and scale need human work.
- **Browser dependency** — heavy projects can strain the in-browser environment.

Bolt.new is the most dramatic illustration yet of what AI means for software: describe an app, get an app. It won't replace engineering for serious products, but for prototypes, MVPs, and "I wish this existed" moments, it's revolutionary.
