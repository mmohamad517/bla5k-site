## What is MyEdit?

MyEdit is an **online collection of AI audio and photo editing tools**, built to be free, fast, and installation-free. Instead of juggling desktop software for every task, you open MyEdit, upload a file, and apply an AI tool.

Its main areas:

- **Audio tools** — noise removal, audio enhancement, voice tools
- **Photo tools** — background removal, enhancement, object removal
- **AI utilities** — upscaling and format conversion

The design philosophy is accessibility: professional-quality AI editing without professional software or learning curves.

## How MyEdit works

The workflow is the same for every tool:

1. **Open myedit.online** and choose a tool.
2. **Upload your file** — audio or image.
3. **Apply the AI tool** — one click for most operations.
4. **Preview the result** — compare before and after.
5. **Download** — save the cleaned or enhanced file.

Every tool is a focused, single-purpose workflow — no timeline, no layers, no complexity.
