For companies that need video — training, onboarding, marketing — the old options were brutal: studios, actors, cameras, reshoots, and a budget that hurt. **Synthesia** removed the entire film set. Type a script, choose an AI avatar, and get a professional video where a realistic presenter speaks your words in your language. No camera. No actors. No studio day.

This guide covers what Synthesia is, who it serves, its pricing, and where it fits (and doesn't).

## What is Synthesia?

Synthesia is an **AI video platform for avatar-presented videos**. It combines:

- **AI avatars** — realistic digital presenters (hundreds built-in, plus custom avatars).
- **Text-to-speech** — natural voices across 140+ languages and accents.
- **A script-based studio** — edit video by editing text, not by reshooting.

It's the category leader for corporate AI video, used by thousands of companies for training, onboarding, product demos, and internal communications.

## How Synthesia works

1. **Write a script** in the studio's text editor.
2. **Choose an avatar** — from the library or a custom avatar of your own.
3. **Pick a voice and language** — or use auto-translation for multilingual output.
4. **Add slides and media** — screen recordings, images, and branding.
5. **Generate** — Synthesia renders the video, usually in minutes.

The genius is that *the script is the video*. When content changes, you edit text and regenerate — no reshoots ever.

## Pricing

Synthesia is **paid**: a free plan lets you create a small number of demo videos, and paid plans (Starter, Creator, Enterprise) provide monthly video minutes, more avatars, custom avatars, and team features. Video allowances are the key metric — heavy producers should compare plans carefully. Check the current pricing at synthesia.io.

## Advantages

- **No filming required** — script to video in minutes.
- **Multilingual at scale** — 140+ languages without dubbing.
- **Easily updatable** — edit the script, not the footage.
- **Professional quality** — polished corporate-standard output.
- **Team workflows** — built for organizations, not just individuals.

## Limitations and honest considerations

- **Cost** — paid product; minutes-based pricing suits planned volume.
- **Not cinematic** — it's a presenter avatar, not artistic video generation.
- **Avatar realism varies** — very close inspection still reveals it's AI.
- **Tone limits** — it suits training and explainers better than emotional, human storytelling.
