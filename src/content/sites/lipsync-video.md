## What is LipSync.video?

LipSync.video is an **AI lip sync tool** that aligns a video's lip movements to any given audio track. It's the visual component of modern AI dubbing — making speakers appear to say words they never originally spoke.

Core capabilities:

- **Audio-to-lips matching** — lips follow new audio
- **Dubbing support** — translated videos look natural
- **Character animation** — sync characters to voice lines
- **Automatic processing** — no manual animation

For localization, content creation, and animation, it removes the visual tell that a video has been dubbed.

## How LipSync.video works

The pipeline is automatic:

1. **Upload a video** — a speaker or character.
2. **Provide the audio** — a voiceover or translated track.
3. **AI analyzes both** — the face and the speech.
4. **Lips are resynced** — facial movements match the audio.
5. **Download** — the synced video, ready to use.

The AI handles the complex facial animation — you provide the source video and target audio.
