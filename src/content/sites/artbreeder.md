Most AI image tools start from a blank prompt. **Artbreeder** works differently: it *evolves* images. You blend two pictures, nudge a slider, and the AI generates variations — like breeding plants, but for faces, characters, and landscapes. It's a unique creative process that feels less like typing a prompt and more like playing with genetics on a canvas.

This guide covers what Artbreeder is, how its breeding process works, its pricing, and who it suits best.

## What is Artbreeder?

Artbreeder is a **generative art platform** built around the idea of blending and evolving images. Instead of only text prompts, you work with images as "genes": combine portraits, adjust attributes (age, mood, expression, style), and the AI creates new images that inherit traits from both parents.

The result is a creative playground — especially beloved by artists for **character design**, producing unique faces that don't exist anywhere else, and for exploring visual concepts in minutes.

## How Artbreeder works

1. **Choose a starting image** — from your library, the community, or a template.
2. **Blend** — cross it with another image to inherit both sets of traits.
3. **Adjust sliders** — age, gender, mood, lighting, style, and more.
4. **Generate variations** — the AI produces new images from your settings.
5. **Iterate** — feed promising results back in to evolve further.

The feedback loop is the magic: each result becomes a new starting point, so you can wander an infinite landscape of visual possibilities.

## Pricing

Artbreeder is **freemium**: a free tier with monthly creation limits, and subscription tiers that raise limits, add higher resolutions, more sliders, and commercial usage rights. Because the free tier is limited, serious artists typically subscribe. Confirm current plans at artbreeder.com.

## Advantages

- **Creative workflow** — iterative and explorative, not just prompt-and-download.

## Limitations and honest considerations

- **Not photorealistic for everything** — faces are strong; general scenes vary.
- **Less direct control** than traditional tools or text models.
- **Learning curve** — the breeding process takes some getting used to.
- **Free tier is tight** — meaningful work needs a subscription.
