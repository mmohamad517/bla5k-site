## What is AutoDraw?

AutoDraw is a **free web tool by Google** that uses machine learning to turn rough sketches into clean, professional drawings. It's part of Google's A.I. Experiments series — tools that showcase what AI can do in playful, accessible ways.

How it works:

- **You sketch** a rough doodle
- **AI recognizes** what you're drawing in real time
- **It suggests** professional illustrations
- **You pick one** and it replaces your doodle

The result: anyone can produce clean graphics with zero art skills.

## How AutoDraw works

The workflow is playful and instant:

1. **Open autodraw.com** — no account, no install.
2. **Start sketching** with your mouse or finger.
3. **Watch suggestions appear** — AutoDraw guesses what you're drawing.
4. **Pick a suggestion** — your doodle becomes a clean drawing.
5. **Add color and text** — finish your graphic.

The AI's suggestions appear at the top of the screen as you draw, making the process feel like magic.
