The math is brutal: a 40-minute podcast might contain 10 viral moments, but editing them into shorts takes hours. **Opus Clip** automated that entire job. Upload a long video, and its AI finds the highlights, cuts them into short clips, adds captions and hooks — and hands you a stack of posts ready for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts. It became one of the fastest-growing creator tools by turning repurposing from a chore into a pipeline.

This guide covers what Opus Clip is, how it works, its pricing, and its honest limits.

## What is Opus Clip?

Opus Clip (now at **opus.pro**) is an **AI repurposing platform** that turns long-form video into short-form clips. It's designed for the reality of modern content: long content is easier to make, but short content gets the views. Opus automates the bridge between them.

Its core loop: upload → AI finds highlights → clips are cut, captioned, and hook-optimized → you post them across platforms.

## How Opus Clip works

1. **Upload a long video** — podcast, webinar, YouTube video, meeting.
2. **AI analyzes** — it detects engagement signals: energy shifts, strong statements, jokes, questions.
3. **Clips are generated** — each with a compelling opening hook, precise timing, and styled captions.
4. **Review and post** — edit titles, pick your best clips, and export for each platform's format.

You're not editing — you're *curating*. The machine does the cutting; you choose what goes out.

## Key features

### Automatic highlight detection
AI finds the moments most likely to perform.

### Hook optimization
Each clip opens with the strongest moment — the key to short-form retention.

### Auto-captions
Accurate, styled captions that make clips watchable on mute.

### Platform-ready exports
Sizes and formats tuned for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts.

### Virality scoring
Clips are ranked by predicted performance so you can post the best first.

### Bulk processing
Turn entire content libraries into clips on paid plans.

## Pricing

Opus Clip is **freemium**: a free plan includes a limited number of monthly video hours, and paid plans (Standard, Pro, and Business tiers) raise limits, add bulk processing, custom branding, and team features. Hours, not files, are the unit that matters — compare plans on opus.pro.

## Advantages

- **Massive time savings** — hours of editing collapse to minutes of curation.
- **Captions built in** — the #1 short-form engagement factor, handled automatically.
- **Hook-first design** — clips engineered for retention.
- **Consistent posting pipeline** — turn every long video into weeks of shorts.

## Limitations and honest considerations

- **Quality varies by source** — boring source material yields boring clips; it amplifies, not invents.
- **Review needed** — AI picks can miss context; check clips before posting.
- **Credits are limited** — serious creators need a paid plan.
- **Not an original creator** — it repurposes; it doesn't generate new content.
