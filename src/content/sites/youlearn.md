Videos and PDFs are full of knowledge — but watching or reading once isn't studying. **YouLearn** is the AI assistant that converts any content into active learning: feed it a lecture or document and it builds summaries, key points, and quizzes you can actually study from. This guide covers what YouLearn is and how it works.

For more study tools, explore our [Education directory](/category/learning/) and the [best AI tools in 2026](/guides/best-ai-tools-2026/) roundup.

## What is YouLearn?

YouLearn is an **AI study assistant that turns any content into personalized learning materials**. It's built on a simple insight: the best study materials are the ones derived from *your* content, structured for *how you learn*.

Give YouLearn a YouTube lecture, a PDF, or your notes, and it produces:

- **Summaries** of the key ideas
- **Structured notes** and concepts
- **Practice quizzes** to test understanding
- **Personalized explanations** adapted to you

It transforms passive content consumption — watching a video, reading a PDF — into active, testable learning.

## How YouLearn works

The workflow takes minutes:

1. **Provide content** — paste a YouTube link or upload a document.
2. **YouLearn analyzes** — AI reads and understands the material.
3. **Materials are generated** — summaries, notes, and quizzes.
4. **Study actively** — review, quiz yourself, and fill gaps.
5. **Personalize** — focus on the areas you struggle with.

The content you were going to skim becomes a structured, testable study session.

## Key features of YouLearn

### Video and document learning

Turn **YouTube lectures and PDFs into study materials** — your existing learning content becomes interactive.

### AI-generated summaries and notes

Clear **summaries and structured notes** extracted from your content — no more re-watching or re-reading.

### Practice quizzes

**Generated quizzes** that test your understanding and highlight what you still need to review.

### Personalized study

Materials **adapted to your learning style and gaps** — focused on what you need, not generic content.

### Active learning focus

Built around **active recall and testing**, the study methods proven most effective.

## YouLearn vs. NotebookLM vs. Knowt

| | YouLearn | NotebookLM | Knowt |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| From videos | ✅ Yes | Yes | Notes |
| Quiz generation | ✅ Yes | Study guides | ✅ Yes |
| Personalization | ✅ Strong | Moderate | Moderate |
| Best for | Active learning | Research Q&A | Flashcards |

**YouLearn focuses on active, personalized study** from any content. **NotebookLM** is best for source-grounded research and Q&A. **Knowt** excels at flashcards and spaced repetition. For turning lectures into testable material, YouLearn is the specialist.

## Who is YouLearn for?

- **Students** learning from video lectures and readings.
- **Exam preparers** who want practice questions from their materials.
- **Lifelong learners** turning courses into study sessions.
- **Anyone** who wants to actively learn, not passively consume.

It's **not** for casual content consumption, or users who prefer traditional reading and note-taking without AI structuring. For research-grounded Q&A, [NotebookLM](/tools/notebooklm/) remains the complement.

## How to get started with YouLearn

1. **Create an account** at youlearn.ai.
2. **Paste a video link** or upload a document.
3. **Let YouLearn build** your summaries and materials.
4. **Take the quiz** — see what you retained.
5. **Study the gaps** — review what you missed.

Start with a lecture you've already watched — the generated materials will show you exactly what you missed and what to review.
