## What is Compose?

Compose AI is a Chrome extension that auto‑completes text in any web editor, learning the user’s style to suggest personalized phrases. It runs locally, preserves privacy, and cuts typing time for professionals, students, and creators.

In short, Compose is built around one clear promise: take your input — a prompt, a file, or a task — and return a usable result for AI writing assistant without the manual grind. For plenty of users, that one capability of Compose replaces several steps they used to do by hand.
