Math homework has a new best friend: **Gauth AI** (formerly Gauthmath). Snap a photo of any problem and get an instant, step-by-step solution — from algebra to calculus, physics to chemistry. It's like having a tutor available 24/7 in your pocket. This guide covers how Gauth works, what it's good for, and how to use it responsibly.

## What is Gauth AI?

Gauth AI is a **photo-based homework solver** that gives step-by-step solutions for math and science problems. It began as Gauthmath in 2020 and expanded into a general study app used by millions of students worldwide.

The core experience:

- **Snap a photo** of any problem
- **Get a solution instantly** with every step shown
- **AI + expert tutors** — AI handles most problems; experts take the hard ones
- **Multiple subjects** — math, physics, chemistry, and more

It's designed for the moment every student knows: stuck on a problem, deadline looming, no tutor available. Gauth fills that gap in seconds.

## How Gauth works

The workflow is instant:

1. **Open the app** and take a photo of your problem.
2. **Gauth recognizes** the text and equations.
3. **The AI solves it** — step by step, with explanations.
4. **For hard problems**, it routes to an expert tutor for a detailed solution.
5. **You learn the method** — the steps matter as much as the answer.

The step-by-step format is the key: Gauth doesn't just give answers, it shows *how* the problem is solved, which is what actually helps you learn.

## Key features of Gauth

### Photo problem solving

Snap a photo of handwritten or printed problems and get **instant solutions** — no typing equations by hand.

### Step-by-step explanations

Every solution breaks down into **clear, sequential steps**, so you can follow the reasoning and apply it to similar problems.

### Broad subject coverage

Math from **basic arithmetic to calculus and beyond**, plus physics, chemistry, and other STEM subjects.

### Expert tutor backup

For especially tough problems, Gauth connects you with **human expert tutors** for thorough explanations.

### Works on mobile

Available as an **iOS and Android app**, so help is with you wherever homework happens.

## Who is Gauth for?

- **Students** stuck on homework at any level.
- **Exam prep** — quickly verifying methods and checking work.
- **Parents** helping kids with math they've forgotten.
- **STEM learners** who want to see the method, not just the answer.

It's **not** a replacement for learning the material (you need the steps, not just the result), and it's not for non-STEM subjects where general assistants shine. For learning from scratch, pair it with [Coddy](/tools/coddy/) or [Knowt](/tools/knowt/).

## How to use Gauth responsibly

1. **Try the problem yourself first** — Gauth is for when you're stuck, not the first resort.
2. **Study the steps** — understand each line; don't just copy the answer.
3. **Check your work** — use Gauth to verify your own solution.
4. **Practice the method** — apply what you learned to a similar problem.
5. **Know your school's policy** — AI use rules vary by institution.

Used this way, Gauth is a powerful tutor. Used as a shortcut, it teaches you nothing. The steps are the value — read them.
