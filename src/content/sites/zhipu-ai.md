## What is Zhipu AI?

Zhipu AI is a **Chinese AI company** that develops the GLM (General Language Model) series. Its flagship product is a consumer chatbot at chatglm.cn, plus developer APIs and enterprise models.

Its core promise:

- **Free access** — a generous free tier for everyday use
- **Strong reasoning** — competitive on math, logic, and coding benchmarks
- **Native Chinese fluency** — excellent for Chinese writing and translation
- **Developer APIs** — build apps on top of the GLM models

For anyone curious about the Chinese AI landscape, Zhipu is one of the "big four" AI labs in China alongside the companies behind Qwen, DeepSeek, and Moonshot (Kimi).

## How Zhipu AI works

The GLM chatbot works like other modern assistants:

1. **Open the chat** — at chatglm.cn, no heavy setup.
2. **Ask anything** — questions, code, documents, or writing tasks.
3. **GLM responds** — with reasoning, code, or drafted text.
4. **Refine the conversation** — iterate like any chat assistant.

What makes GLM notable is how much performance you get on the free tier — the gap between GLM's public models and the top Western models has narrowed dramatically.

## Key features of Zhipu AI

### GLM's strong reasoning

GLM models score **competitively on math, logic, and reasoning benchmarks** — the kind of tasks that separate capable assistants from toy chatbots.

### Free everyday assistant

The free tier covers **chatting, writing, and coding** for regular use, with paid plans unlocking higher limits and faster models.

### Native Chinese excellence

GLM is **first-class in Chinese** — writing, translating, and understanding Chinese context better than most Western models, which matters for a huge share of the world's users.

### Developer API

Zhipu offers **APIs for the GLM models**, so businesses can integrate the assistant into their own products without training a model from scratch.

### Multi-model choice

Zhipu releases **multiple model sizes**, from lightweight fast models to flagship reasoning models, letting users pick the right balance of speed and capability.
