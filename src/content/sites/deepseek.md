## What is DeepSeek?

DeepSeek is an **AI research company and chatbot platform based in China** that builds large language models with an unusual focus on efficiency. Its models match or exceed Western rivals on coding, math, and reasoning benchmarks — while costing a small fraction to train and run.

The company offers three ways to use its technology:

1. **A free chatbot** at deepseek.com and a mobile app, with a 1M-token context window.
2. **A paid API** for developers, with some of the lowest prices in the industry.
3. **Open-source model weights**, downloadable and runnable on your own hardware.

Its release created genuine market shock: DeepSeek's efficient training techniques showed that frontier AI didn't require billions in compute, and the stock-market ripple effect was covered worldwide. For everyday users, the practical takeaway is simpler: **a very capable AI assistant that costs almost nothing**.

## How DeepSeek works

DeepSeek's models use a **mixture-of-experts (MoE) architecture** — instead of activating the entire network for every request, they route each token through only the most relevant subset of parameters. That's how a huge model can stay fast and cheap to run.

On top of that, DeepSeek added **reinforcement-learning-driven reasoning**: the model is trained to "think" step by step before answering, which dramatically improves performance on math, logic, and coding problems. You can see this reasoning in action in the chat interface, where the model walks through its thinking before giving a final answer.

This combination — efficient architecture plus explicit reasoning — is why DeepSeek punches far above its price point.

## Key features of DeepSeek

### A 1M token context window

DeepSeek can hold **roughly one million tokens** in a single conversation. That's the full text of a trilogy of novels, a large codebase, or hours of transcripts. You can dump an entire repository in and ask for a coherent analysis — a capability most rivals only offer at enterprise pricing tiers.

### Extremely low API pricing

The API costs around **$0.14 per million input tokens** for the flagship model. To put that in perspective, comparable models from other providers can cost 50–100 times more. For startups and developers processing high volumes, DeepSeek makes AI features economically viable at scale.

### Open-source model weights

Several DeepSeek models are **released openly**, so you can download them and run them locally or on your own infrastructure. This is a huge deal for researchers, privacy-conscious teams, and anyone who wants to avoid per-token charges entirely.

### Strong coding performance

DeepSeek is **exceptionally strong at programming**: writing code, debugging, explaining legacy code, and reasoning about entire architectures. Many developers use it as their daily coding assistant precisely because it rivals more expensive tools on technical work.

### Free web and mobile chat

The simplest way to try it: **just open deepseek.com and chat**. No subscription, no credit card, no usage limits for regular use.
