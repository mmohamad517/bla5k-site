## What is Top3D.ai?

Top3D.ai is an **independent benchmark for AI 3D generators**. Instead of trusting vendor benchmarks or sponsored reviews, it runs identical prompts through every generator, lets real users vote on blind results, and turns those votes into live ELO ratings.

Its core promise:

- **Blind, community-driven voting** — brands hidden until after you vote
- **Live ELO rankings** — updated continuously across four arenas
- **Side-by-side comparison** — pick any two tools and inspect outputs
- **No paid placement** — rankings can't be bought

For anyone choosing between Tripo, Meshy, Hunyuan, or the rest, it answers one question honestly: which one actually produces the better result?

## How the Top3D.ai arena works

The process is simple and rigorous:

1. **One prompt, every tool** — Top3D.ai runs the same image prompt through every generator it tracks, with no per-tool tuning.
2. **Blind voting** — two unlabeled outputs appear side by side; you spin, zoom, and pick the winner.
3. **Brands reveal** — the tool names appear only after you vote.
4. **ELO updates** — each vote shifts both ratings, and the leaderboard refreshes in real time.

After hundreds of thousands of votes, the rankings converge on the community's honest assessment.

## Key features of Top3D.ai

### Four ranking arenas

Top3D.ai scores tools across **texture quality, geometry and topology, low-poly game-ready output, and AI-detected segmentation** — because the best tool for photoreal texture isn't necessarily the best for stylized game assets.

### Live ELO leaderboard

Every vote feeds a **continuous ELO rating**, like chess ratings for 3D generators — so rankings reflect accumulated community judgment rather than one-off tests.

### Named comparison mode

When you already know the contenders, **Compare mode** lets you pick any two tools, choose from a curated prompt library, and inspect outputs side by side with full brand names shown.

### Detailed tool profiles

Each generator has a **community profile** with ratings across seven quality dimensions, pricing, plans, and feature filters — polycount control, 4K texture, rigging, and more.

### Completely free

Voting, browsing, and comparing are **free and non-sponsored** — the site's credibility depends on not taking paid placement.
