**Manus** is one of the most talked-about AI agents of its generation — a general-purpose agent that doesn't just answer questions but completes whole tasks end-to-end. Its tagline, "Hands On AI," captures the idea: Manus is the hands that get things done. In this guide we'll cover what Manus is, what it can do, how it works, pricing, and the important caveats about verifying its current status.

## What is Manus?

Manus is a **general-purpose AI agent** — an autonomous "action engine" that executes multi-step tasks for you. Unlike a chatbot that produces text, Manus performs actual work: it researches, codes, builds apps, designs, analyzes data, and delivers finished results. Its positioning is that it's the "hands for AI to do" — turning instructions into completed outcomes.

It's available as a **web app**, **desktop app**, and **mobile app**, with individual and team plans plus an API for developers.

## Who creates Manus?

Manus is developed by the company behind the domains manus.im and manus.ai. **Important caveat:** as of the time of writing, the official site displays claims that "Manus is now part of Meta — bringing AI to businesses worldwide," with footer copyrights reading "© 2026 Meta" and careers pages linking to metacareers.com. This affiliation could not be independently verified beyond the site's own statements, so it's worth checking the latest official status before relying on it.

## How Manus works

Manus uses an agentic model: you give it a task, and it plans and executes the work through a loop of reasoning, using tools, and checking results. Key aspects:

1. **Describe the task** — in natural language, in the web, desktop, or mobile app.
2. **Manus plans** — it breaks the task into steps.
3. **It executes** — researching, coding, building, designing, or analyzing.
4. **It delivers** — finished work, not just answers.
5. **You supervise** — review and iterate with follow-up instructions.

Its approach is described as "less structure, more intelligence" — a no-code, chat-driven way to get complex work done without micromanaging every step.

## Key features of Manus

- **Autonomous execution** — completes complex multi-step tasks end-to-end.
- **Parallel task runs** — work on multiple things at once.
- **Wide Research** — deep research across the web.
- **Browser operator** — uses a browser to navigate and act on websites.
- **Generators** — AI image, music, and slide generation.
- **Mail Manus** — email management agent.
- **Slack integration** — work from your chat.
- **Shared team workspaces** — pooled credits across your team.
- **Admin dashboard** — manage usage and members.
- **API** — task automation programmatically.

## What can you use Manus for?

- **Research** — deep, multi-source research with delivered reports.
- **Coding and building** — write code, build apps, and create websites.
- **Design** — generate images, slides, and creative assets.
- **Data analysis** — analyze data and produce insights.
- **Automation** — recurring tasks via the API.
- **Team collaboration** — shared workspaces for group work.

## Pricing: what does Manus cost?

Manus offers **individual and team plans**. Key documented mechanics:

- **Team plan** — per-seat/month with pooled credits shared by all members; annual billing saves about 17%.
- **Add-on credits** — buy more when the pool runs low.
- **Flexible billing** — billed seats adjust as members join or leave.

**Note:** exact current prices are rendered client-side and could not be extracted from the official page — check manus.im/pricing for live figures.

## Integrations

- **Manus API** — open.manus.ai with base URL api.manus.ai; create and manage tasks, projects, files, webhooks, skills, and agents.
- **Connectors** — Google Calendar, GitHub, Notion, Slack, and more.
- **Slack** — team collaboration.
- **SSO** — for team plans.

## Who is Manus for?

Manus is aimed at **people who want an agent that completes work, not just conversations**. It's a strong fit for:

- **Knowledge workers** doing research and analysis.
- **Builders and makers** who want apps and sites created for them.
- **Teams** that want shared, managed AI usage.
- **Developers** who want to automate tasks via the API.

## Advantages of Manus

- **End-to-end execution** — delivers finished work, not just answers.
- **Broad capabilities** — research, code, build, design, analyze.
- **Team features** — pooled credits, shared workspaces, admin.
- **Developer API** — automate and integrate.
- **Multi-platform** — web, desktop, mobile, Slack.

## Limitations and honest considerations

- **Ownership status unclear** — the "part of Meta" claim is unverified; check the latest official status.
- **Pricing not transparent** — current prices aren't in the static site.
- **General-purpose trade-offs** — less specialized than domain-specific tools.

## Security and privacy

The official site claims **SOC 2 compliance** and a **Trust Center**, and states that Manus does not train models on your data. It offers SSO for teams. Because the ownership situation is currently ambiguous (the site's Meta claims), verify the company's latest official security and privacy statements before sharing sensitive work.

## Getting started with Manus

1. **Visit manus.im** — sign up for an individual or team plan.
2. **Try a task** — start with something concrete, like a research task.
3. **Iterate** — refine with follow-up instructions.
4. **Go deeper** — try building, coding, or the API.
5. **Scale to a team** — set up a workspace with pooled credits.

## The bottom line

Manus is a capable general-purpose agent that executes real work end-to-end — research, code, building, and analysis. Its team features and developer API make it useful for both individuals and organizations. The caveats are real: its ownership status (the unverified "part of Meta" claim) and non-transparent pricing deserve scrutiny before you commit. If you want an agent that gets things done rather than one that chats, Manus is worth evaluating — with due diligence.
