## What is HeartMuLa?

HeartMuLa is a cloud-based AI music generator that creates full songs with vocals from text prompts or custom lyrics. It uses a 3-billion-parameter model with a hierarchical Transformer architecture and a proprietary ultra-low frame rate codec (HeartCodec at 12.5 Hz) to produce songs up to 6 minutes long.

The platform positions itself as an affordable alternative to larger AI music services, with royalty-free output for commercial use on paid plans.

## Key features

**Full song generation** with natural male and female AI vocals across multiple styles and moods.

**Text-to-song and lyrics-to-song** — describe what you want in plain language or provide custom lyrics for more control.

**Royalty-free output** on paid plans, cleared for use in videos, apps, podcasts, and streaming platforms.

**Multiple styles and moods** supported, from pop and rock to lo-fi, classical, and electronic.

**Fast generation** with instant previews so you can iterate quickly on ideas.

## Pricing

HeartMuLa is one of the more affordable AI music generators: Starter at $4.99/month (about 12 songs) and Pro at $14.99/month (about 41 songs). Free signup credits let you try before committing.

## Who is HeartMuLa for?

HeartMuLa is ideal for content creators, YouTubers, and app developers who need affordable, royalty-free AI music with vocals. For free, unlimited, local generation, [ACE-Step UI](/tools/ace-step-ui/) is the better choice. For the most advanced features and largest community, [Suno](/tools/suno-ai/) and [Udio](/tools/udio/) lead the field.

## Conclusion

HeartMuLa offers a solid combination of quality, affordability, and clear commercial licensing. It's a practical choice for creators who need AI music regularly without paying premium prices, and its royalty-free model removes licensing uncertainty.
