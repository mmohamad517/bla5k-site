## What is HeartMuLa?

HeartMuLa is a cloud-based AI music generator that creates full songs with vocals from text prompts or custom lyrics. It uses a 3-billion-parameter model with a hierarchical Transformer architecture and a proprietary ultra-low frame rate codec (HeartCodec at 12.5 Hz) to produce songs up to 6 minutes long.

The platform — available at [heart-mula.com](https://heart-mula.com/) — positions itself as an affordable alternative to larger services like [Suno](/tools/suno-ai/) and [Udio](/tools/udio/), with royalty-free output for commercial use on paid plans. For creators who produce content regularly and need reliable, licensed background tracks, HeartMuLa offers one of the most cost-effective options in the market.

## How HeartMuLa works

The generation process starts with either a text description or custom lyrics. You describe the genre, mood, vocal style (male or female), and any specific characteristics you want. HeartMuLa's model interprets your input and generates a complete song — vocals, instrumentation, arrangement, and basic production — in a single pass.

The proprietary HeartCodec plays a key role in output quality. Operating at an ultra-low frame rate of 12.5 Hz, it compresses audio into a highly efficient representation that the model can process. This allows the 3-billion-parameter Transformer to generate longer compositions (up to 6 minutes) without the quality degradation that typically comes with extended AI generation.

The platform also supports iterative refinement. If the first generation isn't quite right, you can adjust your prompt, specify what to change, and regenerate with tighter guidance.

## Key features

**Full song generation** with natural male and female AI vocals across multiple styles and moods. The vocal quality is competitive with other consumer-tier generators, producing clear singing that matches the instrumental backing.

**Text-to-song and lyrics-to-song** — describe what you want in plain language or provide custom lyrics for more control. The lyrics mode gives you direct influence over the vocal content while the AI handles melody and arrangement.

**Royalty-free output** on paid plans, cleared for use in videos, apps, podcasts, and streaming platforms. This is a significant advantage over tools where the commercial licensing is ambiguous or requires separate negotiation.

**Multiple styles and moods** supported, from pop and rock to lo-fi, classical, and electronic. The model handles genre-blending well, producing coherent results even when you mix stylistic directions.

**Fast generation** with instant previews so you can iterate quickly on ideas. Generation times are competitive with other cloud-based tools, typically producing a full track in under a minute.

**Extended compositions** up to 6 minutes, longer than many competitors offer at the same price tier.

## Pricing

HeartMuLa is one of the more affordable AI music generators: Starter at $4.99/month (about 12 songs) and Pro at $14.99/month (about 41 songs). Free signup credits let you try before committing. This makes it roughly half the cost of [Suno](/tools/suno-ai/)'s equivalent tier and comparable to [Udio](/tools/udio/)'s Standard plan in terms of cost per song.

## How HeartMuLa compares to alternatives

Against [Suno](/tools/suno-ai/), HeartMuLa is more affordable but has a smaller community and fewer advanced features. Against [Udio](/tools/udio/), HeartMuLa offers simpler pricing but lacks Udio's voice control and style blending capabilities. Against [ACE-Step UI](/tools/ace-step-ui/), HeartMuLa trades unlimited free generation for the convenience of a cloud platform and clearer commercial licensing.

For users who want the lowest possible cost with the clearest commercial rights, HeartMuLa hits a sweet spot. For those who want maximum quality and features, Suno or Udio lead. For free, unlimited, private generation, [ACE-Step UI](/tools/ace-step-ui/) is unmatched.

HeartMuLa is ideal for content creators, YouTubers, podcasters, and app developers who need affordable, royalty-free AI songs with vocals on a regular basis.
