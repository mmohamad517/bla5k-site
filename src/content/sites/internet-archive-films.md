## What is Internet Archive?

The Internet Archive (archive.org) is a **non-profit digital library** preserving internet and cultural history — websites, books, music, software, and films. Its film collection includes **thousands of public-domain movies**: films whose copyrights have expired, making them free for anyone to watch, share, and even download.

It's the opposite of a streaming service: nothing is licensed from studios for a fee. The films are *yours* — part of the public domain, free forever, by law.

## How it works

1. **Open archive.org/details/feature_films**.
2. **Browse the collection** — silent classics, vintage Hollywood, sci-fi, noir, cartoons.
3. **Press play** — films stream directly in your browser.
4. **Or download** — most are free to download in multiple formats.
5. **No account needed** — watch immediately.

## Key features of Internet Archive Films

### Thousands of public-domain films

**Silent classics, vintage Hollywood, early sci-fi and horror, B-movies, noir, cartoons, and international films** — a unique collection of cinema history.

### 100% legal, free forever

Public-domain films are **free by law** — no licensing, no subscriptions, no ads, no expiration. This is the safest film source on the internet.

### No account, no limits

Watch **directly in your browser with no account** — and download most titles freely because they're public domain.

### A living archive

Part of a **non-profit preservation mission** — you're supporting digital history, not just watching movies.

## Who is it for?

- **Film historians and classic-movie fans** — silent and vintage cinema.
- **Students and educators** — public-domain films for teaching.
