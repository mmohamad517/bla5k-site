PRISMAL creates immersive Web3 and tech brand experiences — 3D websites, spatial environments, Webflow development, Unity/Spatial.io integrations and GSAP animations — delivering brand identity, product design, MVPs and interactive demos for founders and product or marketing teams.

In short, Prismal.io is a 3D modeling tool built around one clear promise: take your input — a text prompt, a photo, a sketch, or even a video — and return a usable 3D result without the manual modeling grind. For many users that single capability is enough to replace several steps in their current workflow.

- Runs in the browser with no heavy desktop software required
