Not everyone can pay for premium AI video tools — and **Vidnoz** built its reputation on being the *free* option that's actually usable. Avatar videos, talking photos, and text-to-video generation, with a genuinely generous free tier. It's the tool creators reach for when they need video now and their budget is zero.

This guide covers what Vidnoz offers, its free-versus-paid structure, and its honest limits.

## What is Vidnoz?

Vidnoz is an **AI video platform** with a family of tools:

- **AI Avatar video** — presenter videos from a library of avatars.
- **Talking photo** — animate still photos into speaking videos.
- **Text-to-video** — generate clips from scripts.
- **Video templates** — quick-start formats for social and business content.

Its identity is accessibility: free credits for core tools, a huge template library, and no requirement to learn professional editing.

## How Vidnoz works

1. **Pick a tool** — avatar video, talking photo, or text-to-video.
2. **Provide input** — a script, a photo, or a template choice.
3. **Customize** — avatar, voice, language, background.
4. **Generate and download** — video renders on the platform.

For talking photos specifically: upload a photo, type or paste a script, pick a voice — Vidnoz animates the face with lip-sync matching the speech.

## Pricing

Vidnoz is **freemium**: a free plan with daily credits for core tools, and paid plans (Starter, Business, Enterprise-style tiers) that add more credits, premium avatars, higher resolution, and commercial rights. The free tier is more generous than most competitors — check vidnoz.com for current limits.
