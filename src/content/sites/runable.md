## What is Runable?

Runable is an AI **general agent** — and that word matters. Instead of just answering
questions like a chatbot, it thinks, plans, and executes complete tasks from start to
finish. You describe a goal, and Runable produces the actual deliverable: a slide deck, a
website, a video, a set of images, a spreadsheet, or a researched document.

## What you can do with it

- **Create content** — generate slide decks, websites, videos, images, and audio from a prompt.
- **Design and organize** — build carousels and spreadsheets without switching apps.
- **Research and write** — run AI-powered research and draft documents in one place.
- **Work anywhere** — available on desktop, Mac, iPhone, and Android.

## Why it belongs in this directory

Runable's real pitch is consolidation. It aims to replace a whole stack of separate
subscriptions — a chatbot, a design tool, an image generator, and more — with a single
platform at a fraction of the combined cost. For solo founders and small teams tired of
juggling a dozen tools, that all-in-one promise is a genuinely compelling reason to try it.

> **Tip:** Start with one real deliverable — a pitch deck or a landing page — to see how far a single prompt actually takes you.
