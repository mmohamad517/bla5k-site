Studying effectively isn't about reading notes again and again — it's about **active recall and spaced repetition**. **Knowt** makes that automatic: upload or paste your notes and its AI turns them into flashcards and quizzes, then schedules your reviews for maximum retention. This guide covers what Knowt is, how it works, and why students love it.

## What is Knowt?

Knowt is an **AI-powered study platform** built around flashcards, spaced repetition, and automatic quiz generation. It started as a Quizlet alternative and grew into a full study toolkit used by millions of students.

Its core capabilities:

- **Notes to flashcards** — paste notes, get cards automatically
- **AI quiz generation** — practice questions from your material
- **Spaced repetition** — review scheduling that boosts retention
- **Import from Quizlet** — bring existing sets over for free
- **Study modes** — matching, multiple choice, and more

The pitch is simple: your notes become active study material in seconds, and Knowt handles the science of when to review what.

## How Knowt works

The study loop is effortless:

1. **Add your material** — paste notes, upload a doc, or import a Quizlet set.
2. **Knowt generates study content** — flashcards and practice questions from your notes.
3. **Study actively** — flip cards, answer quizzes, play matching games.
4. **Spaced repetition kicks in** — Knowt schedules reviews at optimal intervals.
5. **Track progress** — see what you've mastered and what needs review.

Everything builds toward one goal: turning passive notes into active recall that actually sticks.

## Key features of Knowt

### AI notes → flashcards

Paste or upload your notes and Knowt's AI **generates flashcards and practice questions automatically** — eliminating the tedious card-building phase entirely.

### Spaced repetition built in

Knowt schedules reviews **at the moment you're about to forget**, dramatically improving long-term retention compared to cramming or re-reading.

### AI practice quizzes

Turn any material into **multiple-choice and short-answer quizzes**, with explanations for why answers are right or wrong.

### Quizlet import

Bring your **existing Quizlet sets** to Knowt and study them free — a big reason it's become Quizlet's most popular free alternative.

### Multiple study modes

Flashcards, matching, quiz, and learn modes keep studying **varied and engaging** instead of monotonous.
