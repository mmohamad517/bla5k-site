## What is Plex?

Plex is a **media platform in two parts**:

1. **A personal media server** — organize the movies, shows, music, and photos you own into a polished library, watchable on any device.
2. **A free streaming service** — ad-supported movies, shows, and live TV channels built into the same app.

The magic is that both live together: your personal collection and legal free streaming in one interface, on every screen you own.

## How Plex works

1. **Install Plex Media Server** on a computer, NAS, or mini-PC you own.
2. **Point it at your media files** — it organizes them automatically with posters and metadata.
3. **Install the Plex app** on your TV, phone, or laptop.
4. **Watch your library** anywhere — or browse Plex's free ad-supported section.
5. **No files? No problem** — you can still use Plex purely for free streaming.

The setup is a one-time thing; after that, everything just works.

## Key features of Plex

### Personal media library

Organize your **own movies, shows, music, and photos** into a beautiful, metadata-rich library — posters, ratings, descriptions, automatically.

### Free ad-supported streaming

A built-in **free movie and show library** plus **hundreds of free live TV channels**, fully licensed and legal, no subscription.

### Watch anywhere

Your library and free streaming on **any device** — TV, phone, tablet, laptop, game console, anywhere with your account.

### Flexible hosting

Run the server on a **PC, NAS, or mini-PC** — your files stay yours, on your hardware.

## Why Plex is 100% legal

Plex is a **legitimate, mainstream media platform** with a large user base. Its free streaming section is fully licensed — the movies and live channels come from content partners under proper agreements. Its media-server feature simply plays files from your own devices, which is the same thing VLC or your TV's USB port does; the legality depends on the files you own, exactly as with any media player.

There's nothing sketchy about Plex: it's a publicly used app from a well-established company, available in official app stores on every platform, with its free content distributed through licensed partnerships.

## Who is Plex for?

- **Media collectors** — people with their own movie/music libraries.
- **Home-server users** — NAS and mini-PC owners.
- **Free streamers** — who also want ad-supported movies and TV.
- **Families** — one app for everything, on every TV.

It's **not** for pure cord-cutters who want zero setup — if you only want free streaming, Tubi or Pluto TV is simpler.
