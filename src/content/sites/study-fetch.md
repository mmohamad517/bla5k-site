Lectures are where learning happens — and where it's lost, since few students rewatch them. **Study Fetch** is the AI companion that turns your lectures and notes into active study materials: flashcards, practice tests, and study guides generated from your own content, with an AI tutor on call. This guide covers what Study Fetch is and how it works.

For more study tools, explore our [Education directory](/category/learning/) and the [best AI tools in 2026](/guides/best-ai-tools-2026/) roundup.

## What is Study Fetch?

Study Fetch is an **AI study companion** that converts your course content into everything you need to study effectively. Instead of re-reading notes or rewatching lectures, you upload your material and Study Fetch builds the resources:

- **Flashcards** — from lectures and notes
- **Practice tests** — questions generated from your content
- **Study guides** — structured summaries
- **AI tutor** — explanations and quizzing on demand

It's built around a simple idea: your actual course content should drive your studying — not generic flashcards from strangers.

## How Study Fetch works

The workflow is upload → learn:

1. **Upload your content** — lectures, slides, PDFs, notes.
2. **Study Fetch analyzes** — AI extracts the key concepts.
3. **Materials are generated** — flashcards, tests, and guides.
4. **Study actively** — review cards and take practice tests.
5. **Ask the AI tutor** — get explanations when you're stuck.

Your study materials come from your course — so you're learning what will actually be tested.

## Key features of Study Fetch

### Lecture-to-flashcards

Turn **recorded lectures into flashcards** — no more rewatching hours of video.

### Practice tests from your content

**Generated practice tests** from your notes and lectures — exam prep built from your actual material.

### Study guides

Structured **summaries and study guides** that organize your content into reviewable sections.

### AI tutor

An **AI tutor that explains concepts and quizzes you** — on-demand help tied to your course material.

### Multiple input formats

Upload **lectures, PDFs, slides, and notes** — flexible inputs, consistent study output.

## Who is Study Fetch for?

- **University students** with recorded lectures.
- **Students** who learn best by active testing.
- **Exam preparers** who want practice from their own material.
- **Lifelong learners** taking structured courses.

It's **not** for casual learners without course content to upload, or for users who prefer reading to active quizzing. For lecture-based courses, it's a strong fit.

## How to get started with Study Fetch

1. **Create an account** at studyfetch.com.
2. **Upload a lecture or notes** — your most important course.
3. **Generate flashcards** and review them.
4. **Take a practice test** — see what you've retained.
5. **Use the AI tutor** — ask about the concepts you missed.

Start with one lecture you actually need to master — the transformation from passive recording to active study material is immediate.
