## What is Hoopla?

Hoopla is a **digital media borrowing service funded by public libraries**. If your library subscribes, you use your library card to borrow movies, TV shows, music, audiobooks, ebooks, comics, and magazines — instantly, with no holds or waitlists.

Unlike ad-supported services, Hoopla is **completely ad-free**: your library covers the cost, and you get clean, unlimited-in-spirit borrowing within your library's monthly credit allowance.

## How Hoopla works

1. **Open hoopladigital.com** (or the app).
2. **Select your library** and sign in with your card.
3. **Browse the catalog** — movies, music, audiobooks, ebooks, comics.
4. **Borrow instantly** — no holds, no waiting lists.
5. **Enjoy** — ad-free, with no late fees.

The key difference from other library services: **instant availability**. If it's in the catalog, you borrow it right now.

## Why Hoopla is 100% legal

It's worth being explicit about this: Hoopla is a **fully licensed library service**. Every movie, album, and book it offers is acquired through formal licensing agreements with publishers and studios — nothing is pirated, uploaded by users, or hosted without permission. Libraries pay Hoopla for the service, and Hoopla pays rightsholders. This is the same legal model as your physical library lending you a DVD, just digital.

This matters because "free streaming" is a category full of piracy traps. Hoopla is the opposite: it's an official, contract-based library platform used by public libraries across North America, with no user uploads and no unlicensed content.
