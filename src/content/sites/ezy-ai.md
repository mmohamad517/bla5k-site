In short, EZY.ai is built around one clear promise: take your brand and your website, and return a practical plan for how AI models describe and recommend you. For many, that single strength of EZY.ai is the reason they adopt it: it turns AI visibility from guesswork into a workflow.

- A WordPress plugin that automates optimization across your pages
