## What is The Roku Channel?

The Roku Channel is Roku's **free, ad-supported streaming service**. It offers a library of licensed movies and TV shows plus **free live channels** — news, entertainment, and niche programming — all funded by ads instead of subscriptions.

It launched as a bonus for Roku device owners, then expanded to the web and other platforms. Today you can open it in any browser and start watching in seconds — no account, no card.

## How it works

1. **Open therokuchannel.roku.com** in any browser.
2. **Browse movies, shows, and live channels.**
3. **Press play** — streaming starts immediately.
4. **Watch with short ads.**
5. **Create a free account** (optional) — resume watching across devices.

## Why The Roku Channel is 100% legal

It's worth being explicit: The Roku Channel is operated by **Roku, a major publicly traded company**, and every title and channel it streams is licensed from studios and networks under formal agreements. The service is funded by advertising — the same legitimate AVOD model used by Tubi and Pluto TV — and nothing on it is pirated or user-uploaded.

That's the reassurance that matters in the "free streaming" space: a big, accountable company, licensed content, and no gray-area hosting. The Roku Channel is as legitimate as mainstream streaming gets.
