## What is Google Chrome AI?

Google Chrome integrates AI features—Gemini-powered context-aware assistance and AI search via the Omnibox—plus Google Lens, password manager, Safe Browsing, tab grouping, performance and privacy controls, offline Workspace access, and extensions across desktop and mobile. In short, Google Chrome AI is built around one clear promise: take your input — a prompt, a file, or a task — and return a usable result for AI assistance without the manual grind. Many users discover that this feature of Google Chrome AI alone covers what previously took multiple tools.
