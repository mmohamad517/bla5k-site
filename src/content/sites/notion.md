## What is Notion?

Notion is an all-in-one workspace that blends notes, documents, wikis, and databases
into a single flexible tool. Instead of juggling separate apps, you build exactly the
system you need — from a simple to-do list to a full company knowledge base.

## What makes it powerful

- **Building blocks** — every page is made of blocks you can rearrange freely.
- **Databases** — turn lists into filterable tables, boards, and calendars.
- **Templates** — start fast with thousands of ready-made setups.
- **Collaboration** — share pages with a team or the whole web.

## Who is it for?

Students organizing notes, freelancers tracking clients, and teams running entire
companies all use Notion. Its flexibility is its superpower — and its learning curve
is gentle enough to start today.

> **Tip:** Duplicate a community template first, then customize it to your workflow.
