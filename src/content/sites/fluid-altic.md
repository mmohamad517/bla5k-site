## What is Fluid?

FluidVoice is an open-source macOS voice-to-text dictation app running local speech models and an optional on-device post-processor (Fluid-1) for context-aware formatting and capitalization. It offers system-wide hotkey input, multi-engine, multilingual transcription, and local-first privacy.
