For most of the past few years, AI music generation has been a two-horse race between Suno and everything else. Udio has firmly established itself as the strongest challenger — and in several important ways, it's pulling ahead.

## What is Udio?

Udio is a web-based AI music generator that creates original songs from text prompts. You describe a style, mood, or musical idea — optionally with your own lyrics — and Udio generates a complete track with vocals, instrumentation, and structure. It runs entirely in the cloud, so there's nothing to install.

What sets Udio apart from other AI music generators is its combination of creative control features and commercial licensing. While most AI music platforms operate in a legal gray area, Udio has signed licensing agreements with major labels including Universal Music Group, Warner Music, Merlin, and Kobalt.

## Key features

**Voice control** lets you extract the vocal character from any Udio-generated song, save it as a reusable voice profile, and apply it to new generations. You can even blend two vocal references together, giving your music a consistent sonic identity across tracks.

**Style blending** allows you to mix two sonic style references rather than being limited to a single genre description. This produces more nuanced, original-sounding output.

**Audio uploads** let you feed your own audio file as a style reference (you must own the rights), giving the model a concrete sonic target rather than relying on text descriptions alone.

**Lyrics editing** works before, during, and after generation. You can write lyrics upfront, let the AI auto-generate them from a prompt, or edit them after a song is created.

## Pricing

Udio offers three tiers: Free (100 credits/month), Standard ($10/month, 2,400 credits), and Pro ($30/month, 6,000 credits). At the Standard tier, that's roughly 1,200 songs per month — significantly more than competing services at the same price point. Student pricing is available on the Pro plan.

## Who is Udio for?

Udio is best for content creators, musicians, and producers who want commercially safe AI music with strong creative control — and who prefer the convenience of a cloud-based tool over local installation. If you need free, unlimited, local generation, [ACE-Step UI](/tools/ace-step-ui/) is the better choice. If you want the most established platform, [Suno](/tools/suno-ai/) is the other leading option.

## Limitations

The free tier is limited to 100 credits per month, which is enough to explore but not to rely on. Downloads are currently paused pending the launch of Udio's co-licensed UMG platform. And like all cloud services, your prompts and generated audio are processed on Udio's servers.

## Conclusion

Udio has carved out a strong position in the AI music space by combining competitive generation quality with the strongest licensing story in the industry. For creators who need commercially safe AI music without the hassle of local setup, it's one of the best options available in 2026.
