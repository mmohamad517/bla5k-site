**Voiceflow** is an enterprise conversational-AI platform used to build everything from simple website chatbots to production voice agents on phone lines. In this guide we'll cover what Voiceflow is, who builds it, how it works, its features, pricing, and how it compares to alternatives.

## What is Voiceflow?

Voiceflow is an **enterprise platform to design, test, deploy, and monitor AI chat and voice agents** across any channel and language. It's a visual, no-code environment where teams build conversational experiences:

- **Chat agents** — website, web chat, mobile, and messaging channels.
- **Voice agents** — phone and telephony with voice interactions.
- **Agentic playbooks** — AI agents with workflows and guardrails.

Its strength is a visual builder that lets non-engineers design conversation flows while supporting developers through APIs, SDKs, and MCP tools.

## Who creates Voiceflow?

Voiceflow is built by **Voiceflow, Inc.**, led by CEO Braden Ream. It's an established commercial product reporting **10,000+ live agents in production**, 4,000+ customers, and 200,000+ users, and it was a G2 2026 Best Software Award winner.

## How Voiceflow works

Voiceflow's workflow covers the full agent lifecycle:

1. **Design** — build the conversation flow on a visual canvas, with agentic playbooks, deterministic workflows, and guardrails.
2. **Connect knowledge** — bring in your knowledge base for RAG-powered answers.
3. **Choose models** — GPT, Claude, Gemini, Llama, Grok, or bring your own.
4. **Test** — LLM-powered evaluations, transcripts, and analytics.
5. **Deploy** — web chat, voice widget, phone/telephony, mobile via the Conversations API.
6. **Monitor** — analytics, transcripts, and continuous improvement across dev/staging/prod environments.

The visual-first approach means conversation designers and developers can collaborate on the same agents.

## Key features of Voiceflow

- **Visual agent builder** — design flows on a canvas.
- **Agentic playbooks** — AI agents with workflows and guardrails.
- **Knowledge base** — connect your content for accurate answers.
- **Omnichannel deploy** — web chat, voice widget, phone, mobile.
- **Model-agnostic** — GPT, Claude, Gemini, Llama, Grok, or BYO.
- **LLM-powered evaluations** — test and improve agent quality.
- **Transcripts and analytics** — understand agent performance.
- **Environments** — dev/staging/prod workflows.
- **REST API, SDKs, MCP tools** — developer control.
- **Fast voice latency** — the company claims ~500ms voice latency.
- **Scale** — claims support for 300k messages per minute.
