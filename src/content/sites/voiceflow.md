Customer support is the most common place businesses deploy AI — and most of them shouldn't be writing code to do it. **Voiceflow** is the no-code platform where teams design, test, and deploy AI agents on a visual canvas: chatbots for your site, voice assistants for your phones, all grounded in your knowledge base. This guide covers what Voiceflow is, how it works, and who it's for.

## What is Voiceflow?

Voiceflow is a **visual, no-code platform for building AI agents** — primarily customer-facing chatbots and voice assistants. It grew out of the voice-assistant design community (Alexa and Google Assistant skills) and evolved into a general platform for production AI agents.

With Voiceflow, teams can:

- **Design conversations** on a drag-and-drop canvas
- **Connect knowledge bases** for grounded, accurate answers
- **Integrate LLMs and AI steps** for flexible responses
- **Test agents** live before launch
- **Deploy** to websites, apps, WhatsApp, and phone systems
- **Monitor performance** with analytics

It's used by thousands of companies — from startups to enterprises — to automate customer support, lead qualification, and internal help desks.

## How Voiceflow works

The workflow is designer-friendly:

1. **Open the visual canvas** and create a flow.
2. **Drag blocks** — greetings, questions, conditions, responses, handoffs.
3. **Connect your knowledge base** — FAQ docs, product info, policies.
4. **Add AI steps** — LLM-powered response generation and intent detection.
5. **Test it** — chat with your agent in the built-in simulator.
6. **Deploy** — publish to your website widget, app, or phone line.
7. **Analyze** — track containment rate, handoffs, and satisfaction.

Because it's visual, the conversation designer — not an engineer — controls the experience, while developers can still extend with APIs.

## Key features of Voiceflow

### Visual conversation canvas

Design flows with **drag-and-drop blocks** — branching, conditions, and variables without code. Non-developers own the experience.

### Knowledge-base grounding

Connect **documents and FAQs** so the agent answers from your real information, with LLM-powered retrieval for natural responses.

### Multi-channel deployment

Deploy the same agent to **web widgets, mobile apps, WhatsApp, Messenger, and voice/IVR phone systems**.

### Live testing and analytics

Simulate conversations before launch, then **monitor containment, handoffs, and customer satisfaction** after.

### Team collaboration

Enterprise features for **shared workspaces, versioning, and permissions** — design agents as a team, not a solo project.
