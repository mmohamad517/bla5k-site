## What is Nothing To Watch?

We've all been there: twenty minutes of scrolling through streaming apps, still with
nothing playing. **Nothing To Watch** is a playful movie-discovery tool built to fix
exactly that. Instead of endless menus, it surfaces films for you to explore and helps you
land on something to actually watch tonight.

## What makes it useful

- **Discovery over searching** — stumble onto films you'd never have typed into a search box.
- **Fast and focused** — designed to end decision paralysis, not add to it.
- **Powered by open movie data** — it draws on TMDB's public movie dataset, so the info is broad and current.
- **Best on desktop** — the experience is built for a larger screen.

## Why it belongs in this directory

Nothing To Watch is a small, clever tool that solves a very real everyday problem. It
doesn't stream anything itself — it's purely about **discovery**, pointing you toward your
next film so you can go watch it wherever you already subscribe.

> **Tip:** Open it on a laptop or desktop for the full experience, then queue up your pick on your usual streaming service.
