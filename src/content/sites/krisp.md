It's the AI noise cancellation tool that removes background sound from your microphone in real time, so cafés, home construction, and barking dogs simply disappear from your calls. It became a staple of remote work almost overnight.

This guide covers what Krisp is, how it works, its pricing, and its limits.

## What is Krisp?

Krisp is an **AI-powered audio processing tool** that removes background noise, echo, and other voices from your microphone in real time. It works at the system level: you select Krisp as your microphone in any app, and it cleans the audio before it's transmitted — so the person on the other end hears only you.

It also cleans **recorded audio**: podcasts, interviews, voice memos, and videos can be processed to remove noise after the fact.

## How Krisp works

1. **Install Krisp** on Windows or macOS.
2. **Select it as your microphone** in Zoom, Teams, Meet, or any app.
3. **Speak normally** — Krisp separates your voice from background sound in real time.
4. **For recordings**, drop audio files into Krisp to clean them up.

The key architectural point: processing happens **on your device**, so your raw audio isn't uploaded for analysis — just the clean output goes to the call.

Krisp is **freemium**: a free tier with a weekly allowance of noise-cleaned minutes (great for light users), and **Pro** and **Business** plans with unlimited minutes and advanced features like call transcription. Check krisp.ai for current free limits.

## Limitations and honest considerations

- **Minute limits on free tier** — heavy callers need Pro.
- **Not perfect on extreme noise** — very loud or overlapping sound can still leak.
- **Occasional voice artifacts** — aggressive filtering can slightly alter voice tone.
- **System overhead** — a small resource cost on older machines.
