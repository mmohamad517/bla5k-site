## What is Uchat?

UChat AI Agent creates no‑code chatbots for Web, WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, Telegram, and SMS via a drag‑and‑drop builder. It supports GPT, Claude, Gemini, and real‑time function calls, plus a unified CRM, analytics, and integrations with Shopify, HubSpot, Zapier, Stripe, and Calendly. In short, Uchat is built around one clear promise: take your input — a prompt, a file, or a task — and return a usable result for conversational AI without the manual grind.
