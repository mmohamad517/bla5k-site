**LabEx** is the answer to "I learn best by doing." Instead of watching videos, you work through real hands-on labs in live, in-browser environments — a Linux terminal, a DevOps stack, a cybersecurity range — with an AI assistant to help when you're stuck. In this guide we'll cover what LabEx is, who builds it, how it works, pricing, and who it's for.

## What is LabEx?

LabEx is a **hands-on, interactive learning platform** for Linux, DevOps, and Cybersecurity, with additional tracks in Programming and Data Science. Its defining feature: **"no videos, 100% hands-on."** Every course is a series of labs where you work in a real environment — spinning up actual servers, running real commands, and completing real tasks.

It offers **6,000+ hands-on labs**, organized into structured "Skill Trees," plus a free Linux Journey track. It's the successor to the beloved **linuxjourney.com** project.

## Who creates LabEx?

LabEx is made by **LabEx Technology Limited** (copyright "© 2017-2026"). The company has been operating for 9+ years and reports **6,000+ hands-on labs** and **900,000+ active learners** (over 1 million total learners). Its open-source lab content lives in the labex-labs GitHub organization.

## How LabEx works

LabEx's model is learning by doing:

1. **Pick a skill tree** — structured paths for Linux, DevOps, security, and more.
2. **Launch a lab** — a real environment (with virtual machines) opens in your browser.
3. **Do the work** — follow the steps, run real commands, complete tasks.
4. **Get AI help** — Labby, the AI assistant, debugs and explains.
5. **Track progress** — 2,000+ challenge solutions and 400+ projects as you advance.
6. **Earn certificates** — complete paths and prove your skills.

There's no setup and no local install — everything runs in the browser, with virtual machines for the hands-on work.

## Pricing: what does LabEx cost?

LabEx's pricing is simple and transparent:

- **Free** — hands-on labs with 3 virtual machines/day.
- **Pro** — $99/year (~$0.27/day): unlimited VMs, all 6,000+ labs, 2,000+ challenge solutions, 400+ projects, certificates, and full Labby AI access.
- **Pro 2 Years** — $149 one-time (25% off).

The free tier is genuinely usable — the paid tier removes the limits and unlocks everything.
