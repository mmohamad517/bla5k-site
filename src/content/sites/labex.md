## What is LabEx?

LabEx is a **hands-on coding platform** that provides real, working environments for learning. Each lab gives you a live Linux terminal in your browser and a task — you solve it, verify it, and move on, building genuine skills.

Covered areas include:

- **Python and programming** — real coding practice
- **Docker and Kubernetes** — container skills
- **Linux administration** — command-line mastery
- **Databases** — SQL and more

It's the "learn by doing" philosophy applied to technical education, with environments that actually work.

## How LabEx works

The lab loop is active and immediate:

1. **Pick a lab or path** — structured learning journeys.
2. **Open the environment** — a real Linux terminal in your browser.
3. **Read the task** — clear, step-by-step objectives.
4. **Do the work** — type commands, write code, solve problems.
5. **Verify** — check your solution and advance.

No setup, no installation — the environment is ready the moment you open the lab.
