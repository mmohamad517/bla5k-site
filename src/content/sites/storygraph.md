## What is The StoryGraph?

The StoryGraph is a **reading tracking and book recommendation platform** built around personalization. It analyzes the books you read and rate to understand your taste — then recommends books that genuinely match, based on mood, pace, length, and genre.

Core features:

- **Reading tracking** — log what you read
- **Personalized recommendations** — taste-based discovery
- **Mood and pace matching** — beyond just genre
- **Reading statistics** — understand your habits

It emerged as the reader-friendly alternative to Goodreads, prioritizing personalization over social networking.

## How The StoryGraph works

The recommendation loop improves with every book:

1. **Add books you've read** — rate them.
2. **StoryGraph builds your profile** — taste, moods, and pacing.
3. **Recommendations appear** — matched to your preferences.
4. **Filter and explore** — by mood, pace, length, genre.
5. **Track and refine** — each rating sharpens the match.

The more you read and rate, the better it knows you.
