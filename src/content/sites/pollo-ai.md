The AI video world has a problem: the best results come from different models for different prompts — Veo for realism, Kling for motion, Runway for style — and subscribing to all of them is expensive and exhausting. **Pollo AI** built the answer: one platform, many models. Pick whichever frontier video model you want, generate in a single clean interface, and compare results side by side without a wallet full of subscriptions.

This guide covers what Pollo AI is, how it works, its pricing, and who it's for.

## What is Pollo AI?

Pollo AI is a **multi-model AI video generation platform**. Instead of being locked to one model, it aggregates the leading video generators — Veo, Kling, Runway, Luma, and others — into one interface. You choose a model, write your prompt, and generate.

For creators, that means one account, one credit system, and the freedom to match each prompt to the model that does it best.

## How Pollo AI works

1. **Pick a model** — browse the available video generators and choose one.
2. **Write your prompt** — or upload a reference image for image-to-video.
3. **Generate** — the video renders on Pollo's platform.
4. **Compare and refine** — try the same prompt on another model, keep the winner.

The multi-model catalog is the differentiator: model choice becomes part of your creative workflow instead of a subscription decision.

## Key features

### Multiple frontier models
Veo, Kling, Runway, Luma, and more under one roof.

### Text-to-video and image-to-video
Generate from prompts or animate still images.

### Side-by-side comparison
Test the same prompt across models and pick the best.

### Simple interface
One clean workflow instead of learning each model's platform.

### Social-ready export
Download in formats suited to TikTok, Reels, and Shorts.

## Who is Pollo AI for?

- **Creators exploring AI video** without subscribing everywhere.
- **Marketers** who want the best model per use case.
- **Beginners** looking for the easiest multi-model entry point.
- **Anyone comparing models** before committing to one.

## Pricing

Pollo AI is **freemium**: free daily credits let you test generation, and paid plans add more credits, higher resolution, watermark-free exports, and priority speed. Because model access affects pricing, check pollo.ai for the current plans and which models each includes.

## Advantages

- **One subscription, many models** — real cost savings for multi-model users.
- **Model choice** — match each prompt to the best generator.
- **Easy for beginners** — no per-platform learning curve.
- **Fast iteration** — compare outputs quickly.

## Limitations and honest considerations

- **Credits can go fast** — video generation is expensive; heavy use costs.
- **Not a full editor** — it generates; polish happens elsewhere.
- **Model catalog changes** — availability shifts as new models launch.
- **Indirect access** — you're using models through a middleman, with the quirks that implies.

## The bottom line

Pollo AI solves a genuine friction point: which AI video model should you use, and do you really need five subscriptions to find out? By putting the best models in one interface, it lets you experiment cheaply and use the right tool per prompt. If AI video is part of your content plan, it's one of the smartest ways to explore the space.

> **Tip:** Run your most important prompt on two or three models and keep the best — model choice often changes results more than prompt tweaks do.
