## What is Pluto TV?

Pluto TV is a **free, ad-supported streaming service owned by Paramount Global**. Its signature feature is **live TV**: hundreds of channels that play continuously, organized into a program guide you can browse and flip through — the internet-era version of cable, minus the bill.

Alongside live channels, Pluto TV offers **on-demand movies and shows**, covering everything from classic cinema and studio films to reality, news, comedy, kids, and sports highlights.

## How Pluto TV works

1. **Open pluto.tv** — no account required to start.
2. **Browse the live guide** — hundreds of channels playing right now.
3. **Tune in** — click a channel and watch like regular TV.
4. **Or pick on-demand** — movies and shows to watch anytime.
5. **Create a free account** (optional) — favorites and resume-watching.

The live guide is the heart of it — an always-on TV experience you can drop into at any moment, which is a different feel from on-demand-first services.

## Key features of Pluto TV

### Hundreds of live channels

**Always-on live TV** across every genre — news, movies, comedy, sports highlights, reality, kids, and niche channels — organized in a browsable program guide.

### Owned by Paramount

Backed by **Paramount Global**, one of the world's biggest media companies — every channel and title is fully licensed. 100% legal.

### Free with no account

**No subscription, no credit card, no account needed** to start watching on most platforms.

### On-demand movies too

Beyond live TV, a solid **on-demand library** of movies and shows from Paramount's catalog and studio partners.

### Works everywhere

Available on **web, mobile, and every major streaming device** — Roku, Fire TV, Apple TV, Chromecast, smart TVs, consoles.

## Pluto TV vs. Tubi vs. Kanopy

| | Pluto TV | Tubi | Kanopy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Signature | Live channels | On-demand library | Criterion-style films |
| Cost | Free + ads | Free + ads | Free via library card |
| Owner | Paramount | Fox | Library-funded |
| Ads | Yes | Yes | None |

**Pluto TV** is the live-TV king. **Tubi** has the bigger on-demand catalog. **Kanopy** offers a curated, ad-free film experience for library-card holders. All three are completely legal.

## Who is Pluto TV for?

- **Cord-cutters** — live TV without cable.
- **Channel-flippers** — people who miss browsing a guide.
- **News and sports fans** — 24/7 channels for headlines and highlights.
- **Budget viewers** — free entertainment, no subscription.

It's **not** for people who need the newest first-run movies or an ad-free experience. If you want a deep on-demand library instead of live channels, Tubi is the better fit.

1. **Open pluto.tv** on any device.

4. **Try on-demand** — browse the movie and show library.
5. **Create a free account** — save favorites and resume anywhere.
