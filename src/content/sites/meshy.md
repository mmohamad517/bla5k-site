## What is Meshy AI?

Meshy AI is an AI 3D model generator that creates production-ready assets from text or images. It provides AI texturing, auto-rigging, and exports with plugins for major game engines and 3D software.

In short, Meshy AI is a 3D modeling tool built around one clear promise: take your input — a text prompt, a photo, a sketch, or even a video — and return a usable 3D result without the manual modeling grind. That one feature of Meshy AI often eliminates several smaller steps from the usual workflow.

## Key features

- Auto-rigging so characters are animation-ready without manual setup
- Multi-format export (FBX, GLB, OBJ, STL, USDZ, BLEND) for game engines and DCC tools
- **Clean, watertight mesh output** — is slicer- and engine-ready
- Text-to-3D generation from a simple prompt
- Image-to-3D conversion from a single photo or reference image
