## What is GlobalGPT?

GlobalGPT is an **all-in-one AI platform** that aggregates a huge range of AI capabilities behind one interface. Instead of subscribing to ChatGPT, Claude, an image tool, and a video tool separately, you use GlobalGPT's single workspace:

- **Chat models** — ChatGPT (GPT-5), Claude, Gemini, Grok, and more

It's built for people and teams who are tired of app fragmentation: creators, agencies, and power users who switch between many AI tools daily.

## How GlobalGPT works

The workflow is designed around consolidation:

1. **Open GlobalGPT** — one workspace for everything.
2. **Pick your model** — chat, image, or video, from 100+ options.
3. **Use credits** — each action draws from your balance.
4. **Generate** — text, images, and video in one place.
5. **Compare and iterate** — switch models freely without switching apps.

The credit model is the key differentiator: you're not locked into per-tool subscriptions, and you can route each task to the best model without thinking about separate accounts.
