Learning platforms come in two kinds: passive (watch videos) and active (practice and get feedback). **Atlas** is firmly the second — an AI-powered platform with interactive courses and real-time feedback that adapts to your level. This guide covers what Atlas is and how its approach differs.

## What is Atlas?

Atlas is an **AI-powered learning platform** built around interactive courses and real-time feedback. Instead of watching lectures, you learn by doing — answering, practicing, and getting instant AI feedback that guides you.

Core features:

- **Interactive courses** — learn by doing
- **Real-time feedback** — instant correction and guidance
- **Adaptive difficulty** — content adjusts to your level
- **Personalized paths** — a study journey built for you

It's designed for learners who absorb more by practicing than by watching.

## How Atlas works

The learning loop is feedback-driven:

1. **Start an interactive course**.
2. **Answer and practice** — engage with the material actively.
3. **Get instant feedback** — the AI corrects and explains.
4. **The AI adapts** — difficulty adjusts to your performance.
5. **Progress** — your path personalizes as you learn.

Every interaction teaches — the platform learns about you while you learn the material.

## Key features of Atlas

### Interactive courses

**Learn by doing** — engaging exercises rather than passive video.

### Real-time AI feedback

**Instant correction and explanation** — understand mistakes immediately.

### Adaptive difficulty

Content **adjusts to your level** — challenging but not overwhelming.

### Personalized study paths

The AI builds a **path tailored to your progress**.

### Self-paced learning

**Learn on your schedule** — the platform adapts to you.

## Who is Atlas for?

- **Self-paced learners** who prefer active practice.
- **Students** who want feedback as they learn.
- **Lifelong learners** building new skills.
- **Anyone** frustrated by passive video courses.

It's **not** for learners who prefer traditional video lectures, offline study, or non-interactive formats. For active, feedback-driven learning, it's a strong modern option.

## How to get started with Atlas

1. **Create an account** at atlas.org.
2. **Pick a course** — start with something you want to master.
3. **Complete the interactive lessons** — practice actively.
4. **Use the feedback** — learn from every answer.
