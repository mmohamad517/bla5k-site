Homework gets hard, and textbooks don't always explain things well. **Course Hero** is one of the largest student platforms for fixing that: millions of study guides, class notes, and textbook solutions, plus 24/7 AI tutoring. This guide covers what Course Hero is, how it works, and how to use it effectively — and ethically.

## What is Course Hero?

Course Hero is an **online learning platform** where students access study materials — class notes, study guides, past exams, and textbook solutions — contributed by a community of millions. Founded in 2008, it has grown into one of the largest educational resource libraries on the internet.

Its main offerings:

- **Study documents** — notes, guides, and exams from real courses
- **Textbook solutions** — step-by-step answers for popular textbooks
- **AI tutor** — 24/7 step-by-step help with homework questions
- **Expert answers** — solutions from subject-matter experts
- **Flashcards and practice** — test yourself before exams

It's a mix of user-generated content and AI assistance, designed to help students understand material rather than memorize it.

## How Course Hero works

The workflow fits naturally around studying:

1. **Search your course or textbook** — Course Hero matches against millions of documents.
2. **Browse relevant materials** — study guides, notes, and solved problems for your exact class.
3. **Unlock documents** — via subscription or by uploading your own study materials.
4. **Ask the AI tutor** — stuck on a problem? Get a step-by-step explanation.
5. **Practice and prepare** — use flashcards and practice questions before exams.

The value comes from specificity: other students in *your* course have often already uploaded notes and study guides, so the material is tailored to what you're actually studying.

## Key features of Course Hero

### Millions of study documents

A massive library of **class notes, study guides, and past exams** from real courses — searchable by course, school, and textbook.

### Textbook solutions

Step-by-step solutions for **thousands of popular textbooks**, letting you check your work and understand the method, not just the answer.

### Flashcard and practice tools

Turn your material into **flashcards and practice questions** to prepare for exams effectively.

## Who is Course Hero for?

- **College students** in lecture-based courses with exams.
- **High school students** taking AP and advanced classes.
- **Students struggling with textbook homework**.
- **Exam-prep focused learners** who want practice material.

It's **not** for non-academic self-learners, professional certification study, or anyone unwilling to contribute documents to the community model. For general learning outside academia, platforms like [Knowt](/tools/knowt/) or free courses are better fits.

## How to use Course Hero effectively

1. **Search for your exact course** — the more specific, the better the matches.
2. **Use textbook solutions to check work** — understand the method, don't copy.
3. **Study guides before exams** — review what others found important.
4. **Ask the AI tutor when stuck** — get the next step, not the answer to paste.
5. **Upload your own notes** — earn unlocks and help the community.

The most effective users treat Course Hero as a study partner: use it to understand, verify, and practice — then write your own answers.

Course Hero puts **the collective study notes of millions of students** at your fingertips — plus AI tutoring that explains anything, anytime. Used properly, it's one of the most powerful study aids available for exam-heavy courses. The key is using it to understand, not to copy.
