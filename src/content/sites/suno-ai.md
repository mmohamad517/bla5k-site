## How Suno works

The creative loop takes seconds:

1. **Describe the song** — "energetic synth-pop anthem about chasing dreams, female vocal."
2. **(Optional) Add your own lyrics** — or let Suno write them.
3. **Choose a style** — pick a genre, or describe a custom sound in the style field.
4. **Generate** — Suno produces a complete song, usually in under a minute.
5. **Refine** — regenerate variations, extend the track, or tweak your prompt until it's right.
6. **Download** — export the finished track as audio.

Under the hood, Suno uses a large model trained on music to compose melody, harmony, rhythm, lyrics, and vocal performance together — which is why the results sound like complete productions rather than simple loops.
