## What is StockCake?

StockCake is a **free stock photo platform built entirely on AI-generated images**. Instead of relying on human photographers, StockCake generates its library with AI — which means unlimited supply, consistent availability, and no per-download limits.

Its pitch is simple:

- **Free** — no subscription for standard downloads
- **Unlimited** — download as many as you need
- **No attribution** — use images without credit requirements
- **High quality** — curated, photorealistic AI images

For bloggers, small businesses, and creators who need a constant stream of visuals, it's a practical alternative to paid stock sites.

## How StockCake works

The workflow is simpler than traditional stock sites:

1. **Search StockCake** — browse by subject, style, or keyword.
2. **Preview images** — filter by orientation, style, and more.
3. **Download** — unlimited, with no credit system.
4. **Use it** — no attribution required in most cases.

Because the library is AI-generated, the availability is effectively unlimited — you're far less likely to see the "this image is licensed" wall that paid sites use.
