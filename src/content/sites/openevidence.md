## What is OpenEvidence?

OpenEvidence is an **AI-powered medical evidence platform** that provides clinicians with research-backed answers to clinical questions. It's a purpose-built tool for evidence-based medicine — grounded in peer-reviewed literature and clinical guidelines.

Core features:

- **Clinical question answering** — from research and guidelines
- **Evidence citations** — every answer backed by sources
- **Clinician-focused** — designed for professional workflows
- **Free for professionals** — in many regions

It addresses a real problem: the impossibility of keeping up with the medical literature, even for the most dedicated clinician.

## How OpenEvidence works

The workflow fits clinical practice:

1. **Ask a clinical question** — natural language, medical context.
2. **OpenEvidence searches** the evidence base.
3. **Get a grounded answer** — from peer-reviewed sources.
4. **Review citations** — verify the supporting research.
5. **Apply with judgment** — clinical reasoning stays with you.

The evidence grounding is the key difference — answers aren't guesses, they're summaries of the research with sources you can check.

## Key features of OpenEvidence

### Evidence-grounded answers

## Who is OpenEvidence for?

- **Physicians** answering clinical questions.
- **Nurses and advanced practitioners** supporting decisions.
- **Medical students** learning evidence-based medicine.
- **Researchers** checking the literature.

It's **not** for patients seeking diagnoses, general health questions, or non-medical use. For clinicians, it's a decision-support tool.
