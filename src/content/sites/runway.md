If you've seen AI-generated video that looks genuinely cinematic — sweeping drone shots, impossible scenes, seamless character motion — there's a good chance **Runway** made it. Runway is one of the defining platforms of the AI video boom: an AI research lab that turned video generation from a demo into a professional tool used by studios, filmmakers, and millions of creators.

This guide covers what Runway is, what it can do, its pricing, and its honest limits.

## What is Runway?

Runway is an **AI video generation and editing platform**. Its headline tools:

- **Text-to-Video** — describe a scene, get a video clip.
- **Image-to-Video** — animate a still image with motion.
- **AI editing tools** — remove objects, key out green screens, extend clips, track motion, and more.

Runway is both a consumer-friendly platform (browser-based, credit-based pricing) and a serious professional tool — its models have been used in feature films, music videos, and major ad campaigns.

## How Runway works

1. **Pick a mode** — generate from text, animate an image, or open an editor tool.
2. **Describe or upload** — a prompt, a reference image, or existing footage.
3. **Generate** — Runway renders a clip (seconds to minutes of video, depending on plan).
4. **Refine and edit** — use AI tools to extend, restyle, or composite the result.

The workflow is built for iteration: generate quickly, keep what works, and polish with its editing suite.

## Key features

### Text-to-Video
Frontier models that turn prompts into realistic, cinematic clips.

### Image-to-Video
Bring still images to life with controlled motion.

### AI editing tools
Object removal, background replacement, green-screen keying, motion tracking, video extension, and upscaling.

### Character and world consistency
Newer models keep characters and scenes coherent across multiple shots — critical for storytelling.

### Web-based workflow
Everything runs in the browser, with team and API options for professionals.

## Who is Runway for?

- **Filmmakers and video creators** prototyping and producing shots.
- **Marketers** generating on-brand social video quickly.
- **Agencies and studios** doing VFX, previz, and concept work.
- **Anyone curious** about AI video generation.

## Pricing

Runway is **freemium**: a free plan with a few one-time credits to test the platform, and paid plans (Standard, Pro, Enterprise) that provide monthly credits, higher resolutions, watermark removal, and advanced tools. Credits refresh monthly and can be limiting for heavy use — check the current tiers at runwayml.com.

## Advantages

- **Frontier video models** — among the most capable in the world.
- **Complete toolkit** — generation plus real editing tools.
- **Professional adoption** — trusted by studios, not just hobbyists.
- **Iterative workflow** — browser-based, fast to test ideas.
- **Constantly improving** — frequent model and feature releases.

## Limitations and honest considerations

- **Costs add up** — video generation burns credits fast; heavy use is expensive.
- **Still imperfect** — AI video can produce artifacts, especially with faces and complex physics.
- **Limited duration** — clips are short; long-form still needs traditional editing.
- **Prompt skill matters** — great output requires learning how to write effective video prompts.

## Alternatives and comparisons

Runway competes with **Pika**, **Kling**, **Luma**, and Google's **Veo** ([see our Veo 3 page](/tools/veo-3/)). Each has strengths; Runway's edge is its combination of generation **and** professional editing tools in one platform. For full traditional editing, pair it with a pro editor like Premiere Pro. ## The bottom line

Runway is where AI video generation became professional. Whether you're a filmmaker exploring shots or a marketer who needs video yesterday, its combination of frontier models and editing tools makes it the platform to beat. Start on the free credits, learn prompt craft with one short clip, and scale up when the results start paying for themselves.
