## What is ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is a conversational AI assistant built by OpenAI that can understand
natural language and respond with human-like text. Since its launch it has become
one of the fastest-growing products in internet history, used by students,
writers, developers, and businesses to draft, brainstorm, explain, and automate.

## What can you use it for?

- **Writing & editing** — draft emails, articles, and social posts, then refine the tone.
- **Learning** — ask for explanations of any topic at any level of depth.
- **Coding** — generate, debug, and document code across dozens of languages.
- **Brainstorming** — turn a blank page into ideas, outlines, and plans in seconds.

## Why it belongs in this directory

ChatGPT is often the first stop for anyone exploring what modern AI can do. It is
free to start, works right in the browser, and pairs well with the other AI tools
in this guide. If you are new to AI, this is the doorway.
