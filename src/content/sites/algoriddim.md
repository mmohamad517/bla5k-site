## What is Algoriddim DJ?

Algoriddim DJ is the company behind **djay**, a line of AI-powered DJ applications for mobile and desktop. Its apps bring professional mixing to everyday devices, with intelligent features that automate the hard parts of DJing.

Core capabilities:

- **AI beat matching** — tracks synced automatically
- **Smart transitions** — smooth, musical blends
- **Multi-platform** — iOS, macOS, Android, Windows
- **Controller support** — hardware integration

It's designed for a simple truth: DJing is fun, and AI removes the barrier to entry.

## How Algoriddim works

The mixing workflow is AI-assisted:

1. **Load your music** — from your library or streaming services.
2. **Pick two tracks** — Algoriddim analyzes their beats and keys.
3. **Sync and mix** — AI beat-matches and suggests transitions.
4. **Perform** — add effects, loops, and blends.
5. **Record or stream** — share your mix.

The AI handles the technical foundation — beats, keys, and timing — so you focus on the creative choices.
