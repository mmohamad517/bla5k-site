## What is Microsoft Designer?

Microsoft Designer is **Microsoft's AI-powered design and image generation tool**. Launched in 2023 and integrated across Microsoft 365 and Copilot, it combines text-to-image generation (powered by OpenAI's DALL-E models) with templates and editing tools — all in a simple web app.

The idea is simple: **describe what you want, and Designer makes it**. Instead of starting from a blank canvas, you start with a prompt, and Designer produces a finished-looking graphic you can tweak, resize, and download.

It works for:

- **Social media posts** — pre-sized templates for every platform
- **AI image generation** — from text prompts, with editing
- **Marketing graphics** — flyers, banners, promo images
- **Everyday visuals** — invitations, thumbnails, presentations

## How Microsoft Designer works

The workflow is beginner-friendly by design:

1. **Open designer.microsoft.com** (or launch it from Copilot/Office).
2. **Describe what you want** — "a vibrant flyer for a summer yoga class, calming colors."
3. **Choose a style or template** — or let AI suggest layouts.
4. **Generate** — Designer creates images and complete designs from your text.
5. **Edit and customize** — change text, swap images, use AI fill and background removal.
6. **Download** — export in the right size for your platform or print.

Because it's AI-first, most of the heavy lifting — composition, color, imagery — happens automatically, and you just polish the result.

## Key features of Microsoft Designer

### Text-to-image generation

The core feature: **type a description, get an image**. Powered by DALL-E-class models, it produces high-quality, varied images for content, marketing, and personal projects — with a generous free credit allowance.

### AI-powered design suggestions

Beyond images, Designer suggests **complete layouts for your content** — given text or an image, it proposes polished design options you can pick and refine.

### Templates for every platform

Designer includes **hundreds of templates sized for social platforms** and common formats, so your output fits Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, and more without manual resizing.

### Editing tools with AI

**Generative fill, background removal, and smart crop** let you edit images naturally — select an area, describe the change, and AI applies it.

### Microsoft 365 and Copilot integration

Designer's AI flows into **Word, PowerPoint, and Copilot** — generate images and designs once, and use them across your documents and presentations.

## Who is Microsoft Designer for?

- **Small business owners** making marketing posts without a designer.
- **Non-designers** who need quick, professional-looking graphics.
- **Social media managers** producing daily content at speed.
- **Microsoft 365 users** who want AI design inside their existing tools.

It's **not** for professional print designers needing precise control, advanced vector work, or fine-grained creative direction. For those, Canva Pro, Adobe tools, or [Figma](/tools/figma/) are the right choices.

## How to get started with Microsoft Designer

1. **Open designer.microsoft.com** and sign in with a free Microsoft account.
2. **Start with a template** — pick a format (Instagram post, flyer, logo).
3. **Or describe your idea** — type what you want in the prompt box and generate.
4. **Customize** — change text and colors, regenerate images until they're right.
5. **Use AI editing** — background removal and generative fill for polish.
6. **Download** — export in the right size, or drop it into Word/PowerPoint.

Start with a template to learn the flow, then experiment with pure text-to-image prompts — that's where the magic lives.
