## What is TapNow AI?

TapNow AI is an **AI avatar video generator** that creates presenter-style videos from text. Instead of filming a person, you choose an AI avatar that speaks your script with natural lip sync and presentation.

Core capabilities:

- **Text-to-avatar video** — scripts become presenter videos
- **Talking-head format** — the engaging presenter style
- **Avatar variety** — presenters for different brands
- **No filming** — no camera, actor, or studio

For marketing and training content, it turns "we need a video" into a 10-minute task.

## How TapNow works

The workflow is script → video:

1. **Write your script** — the presenter's words.
2. **Choose an avatar** — style and language.
3. **Configure** — scene, branding, and settings.
4. **Generate** — the avatar video is created.
5. **Download** — ready for your campaign.

The AI handles the performance — lip sync, delivery, and presentation — so your script becomes a polished video.
