## What is Bylo.ai?

Bylo.ai is a **free, fast AI image generator** that converts text descriptions into images. It's positioned as one of the easiest ways to try AI image generation — no account, no installation, just type and generate.

Core promise:

- **Text-to-image** — describe, get an image
- **Free and fast** — images in seconds
- **No signup** — low-friction entry
- **Easy interface** — designed for everyone

It's the tool to reach for when you need a quick visual from a text idea.

## How Bylo works

The workflow is minimal:

1. **Open bylo.ai** — no account needed.
2. **Type your prompt** — describe the image you want.
3. **Generate** — Bylo creates images from your text.
4. **Review and refine** — regenerate or adjust your prompt.
5. **Download** — save your image.

The simplicity is the point — from idea to image in under a minute.

## How to get the best results from Bylo

1. **Be descriptive** — "a red fox in a snowy forest, golden hour" beats "fox."
2. **Mention style** — painting, photo, 3D render, illustration.
3. **Specify composition** — lighting, angles, and details matter.
4. **Iterate** — regenerate and refine your prompt.
5. **Keep prompts simple** — too many ideas confuse the result.

The prompt is the whole game — describe the image like you're explaining it to someone who can draw anything.
