Most teams now run more than one AI assistant, and the challenge has shifted from finding a model to orchestrating several of them into one workflow. 11ai addresses that directly. This guide explains what it does, who it is for, what it costs, and where it fits among the alternatives.

## What is 11ai?

11 ai is a voice assistant using ElevenLabs Agents that enables voice-driven task management, customer research, ticket updates, and team messaging via integrations with Perplexity, Linear, and Slack, supporting private MCP servers and fast voice cloning across 5,000+ voices.

In short, 11ai is an AI agent platform built around one clear promise: let you build, run, and manage AI-driven workflows without stitching everything together by hand. For teams already using AI in production, that orchestration layer is often the difference between a demo and a deployed system.

## Key features

- **Multi-agent orchestration** — coordinate several AI agents or models in a single workflow instead of switching between tools.
- **Developer-friendly** — APIs, SDKs, and deployment options designed to slot into an existing stack.
- **Automation of repetitive work** — offload planning, research, messaging, or task-tracking steps to the platform.
- **Production focus** — built to run real workloads, not just prototypes.

11ai fits developers, product teams, and operators who want to put AI to work in a structured way — building agents, automating processes, or running experiments. It is less aimed at someone looking for a single chatbot to chat with; it is a platform for building with AI.
