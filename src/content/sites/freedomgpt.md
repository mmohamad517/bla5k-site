Privacy is the one thing cloud AI can't offer — every chat you send to ChatGPT or Claude passes through a company's servers. **FreedomGPT** was built to change that: open-source AI that runs entirely on your device, with hundreds of models, zero tracking, and no data collection. This guide covers what FreedomGPT is, how it works, and who it's for.

## What is FreedomGPT?

FreedomGPT is an **open-source AI assistant platform designed for local, private execution**. Instead of sending your questions to a cloud service, it runs language models directly on your own computer — Windows, Mac, or Linux.

Its positioning is simple and uncompromising:

- **Local execution** — models run on your device
- **Zero tracking** — no analytics, no telemetry
- **No accounts** — nothing to sign up for
- **400+ models** — including uncensored and open variants

The project emerged from the movement of developers and privacy advocates who wanted the power of LLMs without the surveillance and content-control of cloud platforms.

## How FreedomGPT works

The architecture is fundamentally different from cloud AI:

1. **Download FreedomGPT** — a desktop app for your OS.
2. **Choose a model** — pick from the library based on your hardware.
3. **Run locally** — the model loads and runs on your device.
4. **Chat privately** — conversations happen entirely on your machine.
5. **No cloud involved** — nothing is sent anywhere.

Because everything is local, there's no subscription, no usage limits, and no data leaving your device — the privacy guarantees are structural, not policy promises.

## Key features of FreedomGPT

### 100% local execution

Models run **on your device** — your conversations never touch the internet, making privacy structural rather than policy-based.

### 400+ open-source models

A library of **hundreds of models** across sizes and styles, including uncensored variants, giving you choice that cloud services don't offer.

### Cross-platform desktop app

Available for **Windows, Mac, and Linux** — a native desktop experience for local AI.

### Zero tracking and no accounts

**No telemetry, no analytics, no signup** — designed to collect nothing about you.

### Uncensored model access

Access to **models without heavy safety filtering** — useful for research, creative work, and topics that filtered models refuse.

## Who is FreedomGPT for?

- **Privacy-conscious users** who refuse cloud AI.
- **Researchers** exploring uncensored and open models.
- **Developers** who want local, self-hosted AI.
- **Anyone** who wants AI with zero data collection.

It's **not** for users who want cloud-level speed on weak hardware, casual users who won't download software, or anyone with limited GPU memory wanting large models. For cloud convenience, [ChatGPT](/tools/chatgpt/) or [DeepSeek](/tools/deepseek/) remain strong.

## How to get started with FreedomGPT

1. **Download the app** for your operating system.
2. **Browse the model library** and pick one that fits your hardware.
3. **Install the model** — it downloads and loads locally.
4. **Start chatting** — completely private, on your device.
5. **Try different models** — compare uncensored and standard variants.

Start with a small model to confirm your hardware can run it, then scale up to larger, more capable options.
