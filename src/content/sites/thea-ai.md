## What is Thea?

Thea is an **AI learning assistant built around adaptation**. Instead of a fixed curriculum, it assesses your knowledge, identifies gaps, and creates a personalized learning path that focuses on what you actually need.

Core features:

- **Knowledge assessment** — figures out what you know
- **Gap identification** — finds what you're missing
- **Personalized paths** — tailored learning journeys
- **Adaptive teaching** — adjusts to your progress

It's learning that treats you as an individual — the way a good human tutor would.

## How Thea works

The adaptive loop is continuous:

1. **Thea assesses you** — through interaction and questions.
2. **Gaps are identified** — weak areas are mapped.
3. **A path is built** — personalized to your needs.
4. **You learn** — with content adapted to your level.
5. **Thea adjusts** — as you improve, it recalibrates.

Every session refines the model of what you need — efficient learning without wasted repetition.
