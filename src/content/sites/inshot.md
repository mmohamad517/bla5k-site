The best social media videos aren't always made on expensive desktop software — many of the most viral TikToks and Reels are cut on a phone in minutes. **InShot** is the tool that makes that possible: a mobile video editor with trimming, music, filters, text, and platform-perfect export, all in an app anyone can learn in an afternoon. ## What is InShot?

InShot is a **popular mobile video and photo editor** available on iOS and Android, designed for quick, social-ready content. Since its launch in 2013, it's become one of the most downloaded video apps in the world — especially popular with TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts creators.

It covers the essentials:

- **Video editing** — trim, split, merge, speed control
- **Music and sound** — library tracks plus your own audio
- **Text and stickers** — captions, titles, and graphics
- **Filters and effects** — colors, transitions, and styling
- **Photo editing** — basic adjustments, backgrounds, and collages

Its strength is simplicity: the interface is clean, the workflow is fast, and everything is optimized for mobile.

## How InShot works

Editing a social video in InShot takes minutes:

1. **Import your clips** — from your camera roll.
2. **Trim and arrange** — cut the boring parts, split clips, reorder.
3. **Add music and text** — pick a track, write captions.
4. **Apply filters and transitions** — match your style.
5. **Adjust speed** — speed up or slow down sections for effect.
6. **Export** — choose your platform's size and quality, then post.

Every step is visual and touch-friendly, so there's no timeline anxiety — you just drag, tap, and preview.

## Key features of InShot

### Social-first export sizes

Export **vertical 9:16 for TikTok and Reels, square 1:1 for Instagram, or widescreen 16:9** — with quality options that balance file size and clarity.

### Music and sound library

A built-in library of **licensed music and sound effects**, plus support for your own audio files, with trimming and volume controls so your soundtrack fits your edits.

### Text and sticker overlays

Add **captions, titles, and animated stickers** — essential for the text-on-screen style that dominates social video.

### Filters and transitions

Color filters, effects, and **transitions between clips** to give your videos a polished, consistent look without complex grading.

### Speed control

**Speed up or slow down** any clip — a staple technique for dynamic short-form content.

## Who is InShot for?

- **Social media creators** making TikToks, Reels, and Shorts.
- **Small businesses** editing product and promo videos on the go.
- **Beginners** who find desktop editors intimidating.
- **Anyone** who needs a quick, good-looking video from their phone.

It's **not** for professional multi-track production, long-form documentaries, or advanced color grading — those need desktop tools like DaVinci Resolve or Premiere Pro.

## How to get started with InShot

1. **Download InShot** from the App Store or Google Play.
2. **Import a clip** and explore the trim tool first.
3. **Add music** from the library and adjust the volume.
4. **Add a caption or title** — text is essential for social video.
5. **Apply a filter** for a consistent look.
6. **Export** in the right size for your platform and post it.

Start by editing a single clip end to end — trim, music, text, export — and you'll have the whole workflow mastered.
