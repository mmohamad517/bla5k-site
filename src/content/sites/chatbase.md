Every business gets the same questions on repeat: "What's your pricing? Do you ship here? How do returns work?" **Chatbase** exists to answer them automatically — with a chatbot trained on *your* actual information, not generic AI guesses. Upload your docs, embed a widget, and suddenly you have a 24/7 support agent that only talks about your business, built without a single line of code.

This guide covers what Chatbase is, how it works, its pricing, and its honest limits.

## What is Chatbase?

Chatbase is a **no-code platform for building custom AI chatbots**. You feed it your content — PDFs, text files, or your website — and it creates a chatbot that answers questions exclusively from that material. It's the "ChatGPT trained on your business" idea, packaged for non-developers.

The differentiator is **grounding**: the bot cites your data instead of free-associating, which makes it dramatically more useful for customer support and internal Q&A.

## How Chatbase works

1. **Create a chatbot** and name it.
2. **Add your data** — upload documents or connect a website URL.
3. **Chatbase indexes it** — building a searchable knowledge base.
4. **Customize** — personality, branding, and behavior.
5. **Embed or share** — a chat widget for your site, a share link, or API integration.

The setup takes minutes, and the result is a chatbot that knows your business.

## Pricing

Chatbase is **freemium**: a free plan with limited messages and data, and paid plans (with tiers scaling messages, chatbots, and data size) for real usage. Message volume is the metric that matters — compare plans on chatbase.co.

## Advantages

- **No code required** — minutes from signup to embedded bot.
- **Grounded answers** — cites your data, reducing wrong answers.
- **24/7 support** — answers customers while you sleep.
- **Easy to update** — refresh data as your business changes.
- **Free tier** to test with real content.

## Limitations and honest considerations

- **Message costs** — high traffic means a subscription.
- **Only knows your data** — useful for support, but not general knowledge.
- **Quality depends on your content** — messy docs produce messy answers.
- **Not full AI engineering** — power users will hit customization limits.
