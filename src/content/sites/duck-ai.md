If privacy matters more than features, this guide explains exactly what Duck.ai offers.

## What is Duck.ai?

Duck.ai is **DuckDuckGo's private AI chat service**. Where most AI platforms collect your data, Duck.ai routes your prompts through DuckDuckGo's anonymous proxy so AI providers — and DuckDuckGo itself — can't identify you or build a profile.

Launched in 2024 as DuckDuckGo's answer to the AI privacy problem, it offers:

- **Anonymous chat** with Claude, GPT-4, Llama, and more
- **No signup** for basic anonymous use
- **Encrypted voice chat** on premium plans
- **No data collection** for ad targeting or model training

It's not the most feature-rich AI tool — it's the most **private** one, and for a growing number of users, that trade-off is exactly right.

## How Duck.ai works

The privacy architecture is the key feature:

1. **You open duck.ai** — no account needed for basic use.
2. **You pick a model** — Claude, GPT-4, Llama, or others.
3. **You type or speak** your prompt.
4. **DuckDuckGo's proxy anonymizes it** — your IP and identity are stripped.
5. **The AI provider answers** without knowing who you are.
6. **Your conversation isn't stored** or used to profile you.

The result: the capabilities of frontier AI, minus the surveillance. If you've avoided AI chatbots for privacy reasons, Duck.ai is designed for exactly you.

## Key features of Duck.ai

### Anonymous by design

Every prompt is routed through **DuckDuckGo's proxy**, so AI providers never see your IP address or identifying info, and no profile is built from your questions.

### No signup for basic use

Start chatting immediately — **no account, no email, no phone number**. For occasional private questions, there's zero barrier.

### Multiple top models

Access **Claude, GPT-4, and Llama** with the freedom to choose — privacy doesn't mean settling for a weaker model.

### Encrypted voice chat (premium)

Speak to AI with **end-to-end encrypted audio** — the premium tier's standout feature for discussing sensitive topics by voice without exposure.

### DuckDuckGo trust

Backed by a company whose **entire business model is privacy**, not a platform that monetizes your data.
