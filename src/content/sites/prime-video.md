If you already pay for Amazon Prime, you're also paying for **Prime Video** — Amazon's streaming service with blockbuster originals, exclusive live sports, and a free ad-supported section built in. This guide covers what you get, what it costs, and how it stacks up against Netflix and Disney+.

For free alternatives, see our [best free legal movie streaming sites](/guides/best-free-legal-movie-streaming-2026/) guide.

## What is Prime Video?

Prime Video is **Amazon's streaming service, included with Amazon Prime**. It offers:

- **Exclusive originals** — Fallout, The Boys, Reacher, The Rings of Power
- **Live sports** — Thursday Night Football, NASCAR, WNBA, and more
- **Free ad-supported content** — the former Freevee catalog, built in
- **Movies to rent or buy** — the full Amazon digital store

It's arguably the best-value streaming bundle: if you're already a Prime member, Prime Video is a substantial bonus you're already paying for.

## How Prime Video works

1. **Sign in with your Amazon account** — included with Prime.
2. **Browse** — originals, live sports, free ad-supported content, rentals.
3. **Watch included titles** — no extra charge with Prime.
4. **Rent or buy** — for movies outside the included catalog.
5. **Add channels** — optional subscriptions like Max or Paramount+ through Prime.

## Key features of Prime Video

### Included with Prime

**$0 extra for Prime members** — one of the best free-with-membership streaming bundles in the industry.

### Exclusive live sports

**Thursday Night Football, NASCAR, WNBA, and global sports packages** — the strongest sports lineup among the big streaming services.

### Blockbuster originals

High-budget hits like **Fallout, The Boys, Reacher, and The Rings of Power** — exclusive to Prime Video.

### Free ad-supported content

The **former Freevee catalog is now built in** — thousands of free ad-supported movies and shows, accessible without extra cost.

### X-Ray integration

**IMDb X-Ray** — actor bios, soundtrack info, and trivia in real time while you watch.

## Who is Prime Video for?

- **Amazon Prime members** — it's already paid for.
- **Sports fans** — Thursday Night Football, NASCAR, WNBA.
- **Blockbuster viewers** — Amazon's big-budget originals.
- **Bargain streamers** — free ad-supported content built in.

It's **not** for non-Prime users who want a single simple streaming app, or for those who hate ads (Ultra costs extra to remove them).

## Why Prime Video is 100% legal

Prime Video is operated by **Amazon** — one of the largest publicly traded companies in the world. All of its content is licensed from studios and networks or produced by Amazon itself, and the free ad-supported section carries the same licensed content that used to run under the Freevee brand.

As with any major service, there's nothing pirated or unlicensed on the platform: it's official distribution from a mainstream company, which makes it completely safe to use and recommend.

## How to get started with Prime Video

1. **Sign in with your Amazon account** — included with Prime.
2. **Watch a flagship original** — try Fallout or The Boys.
3. **Check the free section** — ad-supported movies, no extra cost.
4. **Try live sports** — Thursday Night Football during the season.
5. **Add Ultra** — only if the ads bother you ($4.99/month).

## The bottom line

Prime Video is **the best streaming bonus in the business**: included with Prime, with exclusive live sports, blockbuster originals, and a free ad-supported section. If you're already an Amazon member, it's essentially free content you're not using yet.
