## What is Looka?

Looka is an **AI-powered logo maker and brand design platform**. Formerly known as Logojoy, it helps non-designers create professional-looking logos and complete brand identities quickly and affordably.

The workflow is designed to be effortless:

- Answer a few questions about your business and style
- Get dozens of AI-generated logo concepts instantly
- Customize icons, fonts, colors, and layouts
- Purchase your logo or a full brand kit at a one-time price

Since its launch, Looka has helped millions of businesses create identities, from solo freelancers to funded startups. It's the fastest credible alternative to both $1,000+ agencies and generic logo mills.

## How Looka works

Creating your logo takes about five minutes:

1. **Enter your business name** — and optional tagline.
2. **Pick your industry** — Looka adjusts its suggestions to fit.
3. **Choose styles and colors** — favorite looks, color palettes, and vibes.
4. **Browse concepts** — Looka generates a grid of logo ideas from your choices.
5. **Edit your favorite** — change the icon, font, colors, and layout live.
6. **Buy and download** — your logo in all needed formats, or a full brand kit.

The AI handles the heavy lifting — generating coherent icon+typography+color combinations — while you make the taste decisions. That's the sweet spot: AI speed, human judgment.
