## What is Midjourney?

Midjourney is an AI image generator known for its distinctive, painterly, and often
breathtaking results. You describe a scene in words and it produces original artwork
in seconds — no design skills required.

## What people create with it

- **Concept art** for games, films, and stories.
- **Marketing visuals** and social media imagery.
- **Mood boards** and design inspiration.

## Why it belongs in this directory

Midjourney represents the creative edge of the AI era. Paired with the writing and
video tools elsewhere in this guide, it lets a single person produce work that once
required a whole studio.

> **Tip:** Add style keywords like “cinematic” or “watercolor” to steer the look.
