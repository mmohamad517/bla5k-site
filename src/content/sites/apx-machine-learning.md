**ApX Machine Learning** is a free hub for the LLM engineering crowd — practical tools that answer "what runs, and what does it cost," plus genuinely deep, open-access AI/ML education. In this guide we'll cover what ApX is, who builds it, its tools and courses, pricing, and honest caveats.

## What is ApX Machine Learning?

ApX Machine Learning is a **free hub of LLM engineering tools and education**. Its tagline — "Know what runs. Know what it costs." — captures the mission: give engineers the numbers to make decisions about models, hardware, and budgets. It combines:

- **Tools** — a VRAM calculator, GPU cost comparison, model directory, and benchmarks.
- **Education** — in-depth, open-access AI/ML courses and a masterclass.
- **Community** — a blog and engineering content.

## Who creates ApX?

ApX Machine Learning is an **independent project by a single, unnamed founder/engineer**. Per its official About page: "I originally built ApX Machine Learning as a set of AutoML tools for my own personal use…" Traffic surged at the DeepSeek V3 launch (December 2024), turning it from a personal resource into a professional hub. **Reliability note:** it's genuine and transparent, but solo-maintained and young, with no company entity — validate important decisions independently.

## How ApX works

ApX's tools are designed to be practical:

1. **Use the VRAM calculator** — pick a model (inference or fine-tuning) and see VRAM requirements plus GPU rent/buy cost estimates.
2. **Compare GPUs** — rent-vs-buy cost comparison across hardware.
3. **Browse the model directory** — specs, benchmarks, hardware needs, and licensing.
4. **Check rankings** — LLM performance leaderboards and trends.
5. **Learn** — free courses from "How to Build a Large Language Model" (~80 hours) to local-LLM primers.

## Key features of ApX

- **VRAM calculator** — inference and fine-tuning, with GPU rent/buy costs.
- **GPU cost comparison** — rent vs. buy economics.
- **LLM model directory** — spec, benchmark, hardware, and licensing data.
- **LLM performance rankings** — leaderboards and trends.
- **Free courses** — How to Build an LLM (~80 hrs), Getting Started with Local LLMs, PyTorch, RL, Time Series, Quantization.
- **Engineering blog** — practical LLM content.
- **Calculator API** — programmatic access (paid).

## What can you use ApX for?

- **Hardware decisions** — how much VRAM does this model need?
- **Cost planning** — is it cheaper to rent or buy a GPU?
- **Model selection** — compare specs, benchmarks, and licensing.
- **Learning** — free, in-depth AI/ML courses.
- **Tool building** — via the Calculator API.

## Pricing: what does ApX cost?

ApX has a clear freemium model:

- **Basic ($0/month)** — free forever: standard calculator, public hardware library, ad-supported.
- **Pro ($19/month)** — advanced calculator features, unlimited saved configs, concurrent/batch modeling, Calculator API, exportable cost reports, ad-free.
- **Pro+ ($59/month)** — everything in Pro, higher API limits, priority support.
- **One-time pass** — also available.

The courses are **free** regardless of tier.

## Integrations

- **Calculator API** — programmatic access (Pro+).
- **GitHub org (apxml)** — hosts open projects like `kerb`, an LLM app toolkit.

## Who is ApX for?

ApX is aimed at **LLM engineers and learners**. It's a strong fit for:

- **Engineers** running or deploying models who need VRAM and cost numbers.
- **Students** wanting free, deep AI education.
- **Self-hosters** comparing local-model economics.
- **Researchers** tracking model benchmarks and trends.

## Advantages of ApX

- **Free tools** — the core calculator and library are free forever.
- **Free education** — genuinely deep, open-access courses.
- **Practical numbers** — VRAM and cost answers for real decisions.
- **Comprehensive** — directory, benchmarks, trends, and calculator together.
- **Transparent** — clear authorship and methodology.

## Limitations and honest considerations

- **Solo-maintained** — young project, limited track record.
- **No company entity** — no corporate accountability layer.
- **Data freshness** — benchmarks depend on timely updates; check dates.

## Security and privacy

ApX is a standard website with HTTPS; it's not a data-processing platform, so the main concern is normal browsing privacy. As a solo project, it hasn't been independently audited. Use it as a reference tool, and verify critical numbers against model and hardware vendors' official specs.

## Getting started with ApX

1. **Visit apxml.com** — the tools are immediately accessible.
2. **Run a calculation** — check VRAM and cost for a model you care about.
3. **Browse the directory** — compare models and hardware.
4. **Take a course** — start with a free track that fits your level.

## The bottom line

ApX Machine Learning is a valuable free resource for the LLM engineering community — practical calculators and benchmarks that answer real "what runs and what it costs" questions, plus genuinely deep free courses. Its honest caveat is scale: it's a solo-maintained project without a company entity. For engineers and learners who want practical numbers and free education, ApX is well worth using.
