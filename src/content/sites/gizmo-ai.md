The best way to study is active recall — but making flashcards is the part everyone skips. **Gizmo** is the AI learning platform that automates it: feed it your content and get flashcards and quizzes instantly, with spaced repetition built in. This guide covers what Gizmo is and how it works.

## What is Gizmo?

Gizmo is an **AI-powered learning platform** that turns any study content into active learning materials — flashcards and quizzes, generated automatically, with spaced repetition for retention.

Core features:

- **Automatic flashcard generation** — from your content
- **AI quiz creation** — test yourself on key concepts
- **Spaced repetition** — optimal review scheduling
- **Study analytics** — track your progress

It's built for the reality that active recall works — and that manual card creation is the barrier to using it.

## How Gizmo works

The study loop is automated:

1. **Provide your content** — notes, documents, or text.
2. **Gizmo analyzes** it — identifies key concepts.
3. **Flashcards and quizzes are generated** — automatically.
4. **Study actively** — review cards and take quizzes.
5. **Spaced repetition schedules** — your reviews at optimal times.

Your material becomes an active study system without manual preparation.
