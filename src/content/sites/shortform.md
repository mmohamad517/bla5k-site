## What is Shortform?

Shortform is a **premium book summary and guide service** focused on non-fiction. Each guide is much more than a condensed version — it's a structured deep-dive that extracts the book's full value:

- **Key concepts** explained clearly
- **Frameworks and models** laid out
- **Examples** from the book and beyond
- **Actionable exercises** to apply the ideas
- **AI-powered study tools** for exploration and retention

Founded in 2018, Shortform positioned itself as the "summaries with depth" alternative to services that offer only quick overviews. It's used by busy professionals, students, and lifelong learners who want the ideas — and the application — without the full reading time.

## How Shortform works

The workflow is built for learning:

1. **Pick a book** — browse by topic or search the library.
2. **Read the guide** — a structured deep-dive, not a skim.
3. **Learn the frameworks** — key ideas with examples and context.
4. **Apply the exercises** — guided actions to put ideas into practice.
5. **Use AI study tools** — explore concepts conversationally and quiz yourself.

Each guide is written to stand alone, so you get the book's real substance — arguments, evidence, and practical methods — in the time it takes to read a long article.
