**Coddy** is a coding-learning platform that makes picking up programming feel like a game — interactive lessons, streaks, XP, leaderboards, and an AI tutor that's there when you're stuck. In this guide we'll cover what Coddy is, who builds it, how it works, pricing, and who it's for.

## What is Coddy?

Coddy is a **free, gamified, AI-powered platform for learning to code interactively**. It runs in the browser (web) and on mobile (iOS/Android), and teaches **15+ programming languages** through short, hands-on lessons with an in-browser compiler and playground. Its hook is motivation: coding streaks, XP, and global leaderboards turn learning into a game you keep coming back to.

The built-in **AI Assistant** acts as a personal tutor, explaining concepts and debugging your code when you get stuck.

## Who creates Coddy?

Coddy was founded in **2022** by a team with big-company backgrounds: Nethanel Bar (Co-founder & CEO, ex-Intel), Kevin Spektor (Co-founder & CTO), and Barak Glanz (Co-founder & CMO, ex-Meta). The company reports **4.5M+ users**, 4M+ learners, and 180+ countries, with high App Store and Google Play ratings (4.9 and 4.8).

## How Coddy works

Coddy's model is designed to keep you learning:

1. **Pick a language or course** — from 15+ languages and many topics.
2. **Do interactive lessons** — code directly in the browser with instant feedback.
3. **Build streaks and XP** — daily consistency is rewarded and tracked.
4. **Climb leaderboards** — compare progress with the global community.
5. **Get AI help** — the AI Assistant explains and debugs when you're stuck.
6. **Earn certificates** — verifiable course certificates with public URLs and unique IDs.

The interactive, hands-on approach means you're writing real code from lesson one, not just watching videos.

## Pricing: what does Coddy cost?

Coddy is **free to start** — you can begin, earn certificates, and use the AI assistant without paying. Optional paid tiers (**Plus, Pro, and Lifetime**) remove daily energy-based limits, unlock advanced AI, and add premium challenges. Exact current prices are not published in the static site — check coddy.tech for current figures.

## Who is Coddy for?

Coddy is aimed at **people who want a fun, motivating way to learn coding**. It's a strong fit for:

- **Complete beginners** who want interactive, forgiving lessons.
- **Self-taught learners** who need motivation and structure.

If you prefer deep, video-based university courses, other platforms may fit better — Coddy's strength is engagement and hands-on practice.
