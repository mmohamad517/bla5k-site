## What is Clipfly?

Clipfly is a **free AI video platform** combining text-to-video generation with AI editing tools. It's built for creators who want video capabilities without a professional editor's complexity.

Core features:

- **Text-to-video** — generate videos from prompts
- **Video enhancement** — improve quality of existing footage
- **AI editing tools** — background removal and more
- **Browser-based** — no installation

It's an accessible entry point into AI video creation — from generating fresh content to polishing existing clips.

## How Clipfly works

The workflow depends on the tool:

- **Generate** — type a prompt, choose settings, get a video.
- **Enhance** — upload footage, apply AI upscaling and quality tools.
- **Edit** — use AI tools like background removal on clips.

Each tool is focused and AI-driven — no timeline, no keyframes, no learning curve.
