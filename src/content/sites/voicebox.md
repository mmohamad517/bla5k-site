## What is VoiceBox?

VoiceBox is an **AI voiceover platform** for creating professional voice recordings from text. It's built for creators and businesses who need quality narration without a microphone, studio, or voice actor.

Core features:

- **Text-to-voiceover** — scripts become audio
- **Professional voices** — natural, polished narration
- **Multiple use cases** — videos, podcasts, commercials
- **Creator workflow** — fast, iterative production

It sits above simple TTS tools: professional quality, licensing options, and workflows designed for real content production.

## How VoiceBox works

The workflow is script → voiceover:

1. **Paste your script** — narration, ad copy, or podcast text.
2. **Choose a voice** — style and language.
3. **Adjust delivery** — pace, tone, emphasis.
4. **Generate** — VoiceBox produces the audio.
5. **Download** — your voiceover track, ready to use.

The result is studio-quality audio you can sync with video or publish directly.
