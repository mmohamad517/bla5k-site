If you live in mainland China, ChatGPT Plus has one stubborn problem that has nothing to do with the AI: **you cannot pay for it.** OpenAI's checkout rejects mainland cards, so the subscription button is effectively decorative. PayForChat is one of the services that grew up in that gap — a middleman that takes WeChat Pay and turns it into a Plus subscription on your existing account.

This page is about whether that trade is worth making, not just how it works. It is a real service solving a real problem, and it also asks you to hand a stranger temporary access to your account. Both of those things are true at once.

## What PayForChat actually is

It is a **payment bridge**, not an AI product. You already have a ChatGPT account; PayForChat pays OpenAI so that account becomes Plus, Pro, or gains Codex access. Nothing about the AI changes — you get the same ChatGPT everyone else gets.

That distinction matters because it sets what you should judge it on. Not model quality or features, but three things: whether it asks for credentials it should not need, whether it refunds you when a recharge fails, and whether anyone answers when something goes wrong.

## How the recharge works

1. **Pick a plan** — Plus, Pro, or Codex, for a stated number of months.
2. **Hand over a session token** — copied out of your browser while logged into ChatGPT. Not your password.
3. **Pay with WeChat Pay** (Stripe is offered as an alternative).
4. **Wait a minute or two** while the upgrade is applied to your account.
5. **Revoke the token** — this step is yours, and skipping it is the main mistake people make.

The session token is the part worth understanding properly. It is a temporary credential that proves "this browser is logged in as you." Whoever holds it can act as you inside ChatGPT — **including reading your conversation history** — for as long as it stays valid. It is genuinely better than surrendering a password, because it expires and you can kill it on demand. It is not the same as safe.

The moment your upgrade shows up, go to **Settings → Security → Log out of all devices**. That invalidates every existing session, the service's copy included. Do it immediately, not tomorrow.

## What it costs you beyond the price

Money is the easy part; the real cost is exposure, and it comes in three forms.

**Access to your history.** For the window your token is live, your chats are readable. If you have pasted work documents, client data, or anything personal into ChatGPT, that is what is on the table.

**Account standing.** Paying through a third party is not a route OpenAI endorses. Accounts upgraded this way can be flagged, especially when one payment source is spread across many accounts. No reseller can promise you otherwise, and any that does is overselling.

**No recourse.** If the upgrade fails, your leverage is the service's own refund policy. There is no chargeback path through OpenAI, because OpenAI never took your money.

None of this makes the service illegitimate. It makes it a service you should use with a **throwaway-grade account** rather than the one holding your working life.

## How it compares to the other routes

| Route | Cost | Control | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Virtual overseas card | Card fees + subscription | You own the subscription | More setup; card issuer can shut down |
| Recharge service (PayForChat and similar) | Small premium | Service pays on your behalf | Token exposure, account flagging |
| A friend's overseas card | Face value | You own the subscription | You owe a friend a favour |
| Shared / resold account | Cheapest | None — not your account | Can disappear overnight, zero recourse |

If a virtual card is realistic for you, it is the better long-term answer: the subscription sits in your name, and no one else ever touches your account. Recharge services win on speed and on not needing to pass a card issuer's checks. Shared accounts are cheap for a reason.

## Who it fits

Someone in mainland China who wants Plus for a month or two, has no overseas card, and is willing to use a ChatGPT account that is not tied to anything sensitive. In that specific situation it solves a problem nothing else solves as quickly.

It does not fit anyone who can pay OpenAI directly. If you have a workable card, use it — you will pay less and keep the account entirely yours.

## The honest verdict

PayForChat addresses a genuine barrier, and its approach — session token rather than password, refund on failure, published guides — is the more responsible end of a category that also contains outright scams. That is worth saying plainly.

It is still a third party standing between you and your account. Judge it the way you would judge any such service: use an account you could walk away from, revoke the token the instant the upgrade lands, and keep proof of payment until you have confirmed it worked.

> **Before you start:** log out of ChatGPT everywhere and log back in fresh, so the token you hand over is tied to a session containing nothing you mind sharing.
