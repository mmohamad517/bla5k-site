## What is Remini?

Remini is an **AI photo enhancement app** that uses deep learning to transform blurry, low-resolution, or old photos into sharp, clear images. It began as a viral mobile app and now also offers web and API versions.

Its three headline capabilities:

- **Restore** — bring old or damaged photos back to life
- **Enhance** — sharpen blurry images and improve quality
- **Upscale** — increase resolution for printing or zooming

Under the hood, Remini uses AI models trained on enormous photo datasets to understand what a high-quality version of any image should look like — then reconstructs the missing detail.

## How Remini works

The workflow is almost too simple:

1. **Upload a photo** — from your gallery or a scan.
2. **Choose a mode** — enhance, restore, or upscale.
3. **Wait a few seconds** — Remini processes the image.
4. **Compare** — swipe between the before and after.
5. **Save** — download the enhanced version.

The AI analyzes the image, identifies faces and key details, and reconstructs sharp versions of them. The result is often startlingly good — especially for faces, where the model has been specifically trained.
