Most companies do not need another chatbot — they need AI that actually understands their own data and processes. ## What is EFIMOVE?

EFIMOVE is a French company that helps companies transition to operational AI — AI audits, training, and its ARKYCE RAG solution that gives teams instant access to the most relevant company data. In short, EFIMOVE is built around one clear promise: take your company — its documents, its processes, its readiness — and return a practical AI roadmap grounded in your own data. For many, that single strength of EFIMOVE is the reason they engage: it is a partner-led transition rather than a black-box product.

## Key features

- AI audit and diagnosis of data quality, processes, and AI readiness
- ARKYCE: a RAG-based solution that retrieves answers from company documents
- Training and co-construction so teams own the solution after handover
- A listening-first engagement model built around each company's constraints

## What you can do with it

- **Audit before you build** — understand data quality and AI readiness before spending.
- **Ground answers in your data** — ARKYCE retrieves the most relevant information from your own documents instead of hallucinating from nowhere.
- **Train your team** — co-built projects include the knowledge transfer needed to operate the solution.
- **Scale from one use case** — start with a concrete, high-impact process and expand from there.

## Who is it for?

EFIMOVE fits SMEs and mid-size companies starting their AI transition who prefer audited, co-built projects over self-serve tools. EFIMOVE helps most in settings where data is scattered, processes are undocumented, or teams lack AI experience. Start with an audit or a scoped pilot — for most companies that is the right entry point.
