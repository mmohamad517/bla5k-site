## What is Kanopy?

Kanopy is a **free, ad-free streaming service funded by public libraries and universities**. Instead of paying with your money or your attention, you pay with your library card — your library covers the cost, and you watch unlimited-quality cinema with no commercials.

Its catalog is Kanopy's defining feature: **award-winning independent films, documentaries, classics, world cinema, and Criterion titles** — the kind of movies that win Oscars and festival prizes, curated with care rather than churned for volume.

## How Kanopy works

1. **Open kanopy.com**.
2. **Select your library or university** — thousands participate worldwide.
3. **Sign in with your library card** (or university login).
4. **Browse the curated catalog** — films, documentaries, classics.
5. **Press play** — ad-free, on any device.

That's it. No credit card, no subscription, no ads — just your library card opening a film library.

## Key features of Kanopy

### Free with your library card

**Zero cost to you** — participating libraries and universities fund access. You only need a card from one of thousands of partner institutions.

### Completely ad-free

No commercials, no banners, no interruptions — **a truly ad-free streaming experience**, rare among free services.

### Curated, award-winning catalog

Specializes in **quality cinema**: independent films, documentaries (PBS, Ken Burns), classics, world cinema, and Criterion titles — curated for depth rather than volume.

### Educational and cultural mission

Built for **libraries, universities, and learners** — with features like classroom-friendly titles and film study tools.

### Works on your devices

Stream on **web, iOS, Android, and major smart TV platforms** with a library or university login.

## Who is Kanopy for?

- **Film lovers** — award-winning and classic cinema.
- **Documentary fans** — PBS, Ken Burns, and festival docs.
- **Students and educators** — research-friendly film access.
- **Library-card holders** — anyone with access to a participating library.

It's **not** for people chasing blockbusters and mainstream hits, and you need a participating library or university to use it.

## How to get started with Kanopy

1. **Check your library** — visit kanopy.com and see if yours participates (thousands do).
2. **Sign in with your card** — select your institution and authenticate.
3. **Browse the curated catalog** — start with a documentary or classic.
4. **Watch ad-free** — on web, mobile, or your TV.
5. **No card yet?** — ask your local library to join; it's free for them too.
