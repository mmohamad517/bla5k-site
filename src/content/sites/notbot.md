**Notbot** is a WhatsApp-focused automation platform — chatbots, broadcast marketing, CRM, and a shared inbox, all built on the official WhatsApp Business API. In this guide we'll cover what Notbot is, its features, who it's for, and an honest reliability assessment.

## What is Notbot?

Notbot is a **WhatsApp Business API platform** that combines several tools into one:

- **Chatbot builder** — drag-and-drop WhatsApp chatbots.
- **Broadcast marketing** — WhatsApp broadcast campaigns.
- **CRM** — customer segments, fields, and campaign management.
- **Team inbox** — a multi-agent unified inbox for support.

It's aimed mainly at **SMB marketing and support teams in India and South Asia**, providing an all-in-one way to do business on WhatsApp.

## Who creates Notbot?

Notbot is a service on the domain notbot.in, with contact info@notbot.in and a demo contact named in its materials. It claims to be "Trusted by 1000+ clients." **Reliability note:** formal corporate identity beyond the website is not disclosed, there are no published security certifications, and the "1000+ clients" claim is self-reported. The product and site are real, but verifiable information about the company is limited.

## How Notbot works

Notbot sits on the **official WhatsApp Business API**, providing a no-code layer on top:

1. **Build a chatbot** — drag-and-drop flows with interactive lists, CTA buttons, and webhook triggers.
2. **Run campaigns** — broadcast to contacts (up to 10,000+ messages/day), with template approval in ~10 minutes.
3. **Manage customers** — CRM segments, custom fields, contact import.
4. **Handle support** — a multi-agent inbox with the 24-hour reply window and pre-approved templates.
5. **Automate** — custom webhooks, data stores, workflow counters, and 20+ app integrations.

## Limitations and honest considerations
