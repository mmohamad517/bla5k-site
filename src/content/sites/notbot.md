## What is Notbot?

Notbot is a **WhatsApp chatbot builder** that lets businesses automate customer conversations without code. It connects to WhatsApp's business messaging infrastructure and gives you a visual builder to design conversational flows powered by AI.

Core capabilities:

- **AI conversations** — answer customer questions automatically
- **Order and lead flows** — structured conversations that convert
- **Broadcasts** — send updates to customers at scale
- **Human handoff** — route complex cases to real agents
- **24/7 availability** — the bot never sleeps

It's built for the reality that in many markets, WhatsApp *is* the customer channel — and automating it is how businesses scale support.

## How Notbot works

The workflow is no-code:

1. **Connect your WhatsApp** — through the Business API integration.
2. **Design conversations** — use the visual builder for flows.
3. **Add AI** — let the bot handle open-ended questions.
4. **Configure handoffs** — decide when humans take over.
5. **Launch** — the bot starts handling conversations.
6. **Monitor and improve** — review conversations and refine flows.

Businesses define the rules and the AI handles the volume, with clear paths to escalate when customers need a person.
