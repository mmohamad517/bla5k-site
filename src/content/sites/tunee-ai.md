Learning songs by ear is the best skill a musician can develop — but fast solos and buried instruments make it brutal. **Tunee AI** is the practice tool that fixes it: slow songs down without changing pitch, isolate any instrument, and learn by ear like never before. This guide covers what Tunee does for musicians.

## What is Tunee AI?

Tunee AI is an **AI music practice tool** built for musicians learning songs. It uses audio AI to make ear-training practical:

- **Pitch-preserving slowdown** — learn fast passages at your pace
- **Instrument isolation** — focus on one part of the mix
- **Practice aids** — loop, transcribe, and learn effectively

It's the modern replacement for the old "slow the record down" trick — dramatically better and easier.

## How Tunee works

The workflow is simple for musicians:

1. **Load a song** — upload or connect your music.
2. **Choose a tool** — slow down, isolate, or loop.
3. **Adjust** — set speed, select an instrument stem.
4. **Practice** — play along and learn the part.
5. **Repeat** — refine until you've got it.

The AI handles the heavy audio processing — you focus on playing.

## Key features of Tunee

### Pitch-preserving slowdown

Slow songs **without changing pitch** — learn fast passages at a comfortable tempo.

### Instrument isolation

**Separate and isolate vocals, guitar, drums, and bass** using AI audio separation.

### Learning by ear

Practice the way professional musicians learn — **by ear, with modern tools**.

### Looping and practice aids

**Loop tricky sections** and use practice tools to master passages.

### Musician-focused design

Built **for players**, not producers — practice-first workflows.

- **Guitarists, bassists, and drummers** learning songs.
- **Vocalists** learning parts from recordings.
- **Music students** developing ear-training skills.
- **Hobby musicians** who learn by playing along.

It's **not** for producers seeking studio tools or non-musicians. For learning existing songs, it's purpose-built.

## How to get started with Tunee

1. **Create an account** at tunee.ai.
2. **Load a song** you're learning.
3. **Try the slowdown** — reduce speed on a hard section.
