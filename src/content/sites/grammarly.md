You've probably seen the green logo in your browser toolbar, in Gmail, or in Google Docs. **Grammarly** is the writing assistant that became a default part of the internet — the quiet proofreader that catches your typos before anyone else does. What started as a grammar checker has grown into a full AI writing partner that fixes mistakes, adjusts tone, and even drafts text for you.

This guide covers what Grammarly is, how it works, what's free versus paid, and where it's genuinely valuable.

## What is Grammarly?

Grammarly is an **AI writing assistant** that checks your writing in real time — grammar, spelling, punctuation, clarity, engagement, and tone — and offers one-click fixes. Its superpower is **ubiquity**: it works as a browser extension, desktop app, and mobile keyboard, and integrates into Google Docs, Microsoft Word, email clients, and countless web forms.

You don't go to Grammarly; Grammarly comes to you. Whatever you're typing, it's watching — which is exactly why hundreds of millions of people use it.

## How Grammarly works

Grammarly's suggestions fall into a spectrum:

1. **Basic corrections** — typos, grammar, spelling, punctuation (free tier).
2. **Advanced suggestions** — clarity rewrites, tone adjustment, formality, conciseness (premium).
3. **Generative AI** — drafting, rewriting, and expanding text from your instructions (premium).

Each suggestion comes with a brief explanation — *why* the change is better — which makes Grammarly a quiet teacher as well as a corrector. The more you see its reasoning, the better your own writing gets.

## Key features

### Everywhere you type
Browser extension, desktop app, mobile keyboards, Google Docs, Word, email, and more.

### Real-time grammar and spelling
Instant underlines and one-click fixes as you type.

### Tone and clarity
Detect how your message *feels* and rewrite for clarity, formality, or confidence.

### AI writing tools
Draft, rewrite, expand, and summarize text with prompts — beyond just fixing.

### Plagiarism detection (premium)
Check text against web sources and academic databases.

### Style and personal dictionary
Enforce your preferred style and teach it your vocabulary.

## Who is Grammarly for?

- **Professionals** writing emails, reports, and messages all day.
- **Students** polishing essays and papers.
- **Non-native English speakers** who want confident, natural writing.
- **Anyone** who's ever hit send and immediately spotted a typo.

## Pricing

Grammarly is **freemium**. The free tier covers essential grammar, spelling, and punctuation — genuinely useful on its own. **Premium** (paid) adds advanced clarity and tone suggestions, conciseness, formality, plagiarism checking, and generative AI writing features. **Business** and **Enterprise** plans add team style guides, admin controls, and stronger security. Check current plans at grammarly.com.

## Advantages

- **Works everywhere** — no other tool is this present across apps.
- **Explains its fixes** — you learn while you write.
- **Tone awareness** — catches the *feeling* of a message, not just the grammar.
- **Genuinely useful free tier.**
- **Constantly improving** — increasingly capable AI features.

## Limitations and honest considerations

- **Suggestions aren't always right** — grammar engines can be overly literal; your judgment still wins.
- **Privacy considerations** — the extension processes your text through Grammarly's servers; review its policies for sensitive writing.
- **Premium costs** — the best features are behind a subscription.
- **Language coverage** — strongest in English; other languages are more limited.

## Alternatives and comparisons

Grammarly competes with **ProWritingAid** (deep editorial analysis), **Hemingway Editor** (simplicity and readability), and **LanguageTool** (open-source, more languages). Grammarly wins on ubiquity and tone; ProWritingAid wins on deep reporting for serious writers. For paraphrasers, see [QuillBot](/tools/quillbot/). ## The bottom line

Grammarly is the writing assistant that made "check my spelling everywhere" a solved problem. Its real value compounds: not just fewer typos, but better sentences and clearer messages, everywhere you type. Start with the free tier — the moment it catches a mistake you almost sent, you'll understand the appeal.

> **Tip:** Pay attention to the "tone" notes on important emails — adjusting perceived tone is where Grammarly prevents real-world misunderstandings.
