## What is MoltBook?

MoltBook is an **AI-powered notebook for structured thinking**. Where traditional notes apps store your text, MoltBook helps you *think* — using AI to organize ideas, surface connections, and build structure from your content.

Core features:

- **AI organization** — notes grouped by topic automatically
- **Connection finding** — related ideas surfaced
- **Structure suggestions** — coherent layouts proposed
- **Knowledge building** — scattered notes become knowledge

It's designed for people whose notes are the raw material of their thinking — researchers, writers, and knowledge workers.

The AI does the organizing labor — you do the actual thinking.
