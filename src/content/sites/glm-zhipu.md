China's AI labs have produced more than one household name. **GLM** — built by Zhipu AI — is a frontier model family that excels at the two things developers care about most: **coding and agentic workflows**. Free at chat.z.ai, with open-source variants you can self-host, GLM is a serious contender for your AI stack. This guide covers what it is, what it's good at, and who it's for.

## What is GLM?

GLM (**General Language Model**) is Zhipu AI's flagship model family. Zhipu is one of China's most prominent AI companies, and GLM has become a major player in both the consumer chatbot space (at **chat.z.ai**) and the open-source community, where developers run GLM models on their own hardware.

GLM's identity is built around two strengths:

- **Agentic engineering** — long-horizon, multi-step tasks like system design, multi-file refactoring, and full-stack app generation ("describe it, get the app")
- **An open ecosystem** — open-source variants, free tiers, and API access at competitive prices

On the platform you get:

- A **powerful conversational model** for chat and analysis
- **Strong coding** — architecture design, refactoring, app generation
- **Autonomous agents** — multi-step workflows executed end to end
- **Flexible model tiers** — fast Turbo/Flash variants to heavyweight frontier models
- **Open-source releases** for self-hosting

## How GLM works

GLM works like a modern AI assistant, but its design leans into doing work rather than just answering:

1. **Open chat.z.ai** — free to start, no subscription required.
2. **Describe a task** — a question, a coding job, or a full project.
3. **Let it run** — GLM plans, executes multiple steps, and delivers results.
4. **Use it for coding** — architecture, refactoring, and app generation are its specialty.
5. **For developers** — access open-source weights or the API.

The standout workflow is **vibe coding**: describe an app in natural language, and GLM designs the system and generates the code — file by file.

## Key features of GLM

### Vibe coding and system design

GLM is **optimized for building software from a description** — database schemas, API designs, full-stack app generation, and multi-file refactoring. It's a favorite for rapid prototyping.

### Agentic long-horizon tasks

GLM can execute **autonomous multi-step workflows** — managing complex environments, completing tasks across many steps, and integrating with agent frameworks.

### Open-source availability

Zhipu releases **open-source GLM variants**, so developers can self-host the models — full privacy, no API dependency, complete control.

### Free consumer tier

**chat.z.ai is free** for regular use, with paid coding plans and API tiers for power users and teams.

### Flexible model ecosystem

From **fast Turbo/Flash models** to heavyweight frontier models, GLM covers the spectrum — pick the tier that fits speed and quality needs.

## Who is GLM for?

- **Developers** — vibe coding, architecture, and refactoring.
- **Agent builders** — multi-step autonomous workflows.
- **Students and researchers** — free capable chat.
- **Self-hosters** — open-source models on your own hardware.

It's **not** for organizations with strict data-residency rules (servers are in China) or for users who prefer a Western AI ecosystem with its tooling and integrations.
