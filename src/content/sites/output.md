## What is Output?

Output is a **music technology company** creating professional software instruments and effects for producers and composers. Founded in 2013, it's known for cinematic, genre-defying sounds used across film, pop, and electronic music.

Core products:

- **Cinematic instruments** — strings, percussion, textures
- **Creative effects** — signature processing tools
- **Production suites** — like Arcade, Exhale, and Signal
- **Sound libraries** — professional-grade content

Its tools are built around one idea: give producers sounds that sound *finished*, so the music speaks.

## How Output works

Output's products work inside your DAW:

1. **Install a plugin** — in Ableton, Logic, Pro Tools, etc.
2. **Browse the sound library** — curated, professional content.
3. **Play and perform** — instruments respond expressively.
4. **Shape the sound** — effects and controls for customization.
5. **Make music** — the sounds are production-ready.

The quality is the point — Output's sounds are designed to sit in a professional mix immediately.
