Not every legal streaming option is a subscription. **Fandango at Home** — the service formerly known as Vudu — lets you **rent or buy the newest movies digitally** with no monthly fee, while also offering thousands of free ad-supported titles. ## What is Fandango at Home?

Fandango at Home is the **digital movie store formerly known as Vudu**, now owned by Fandango (a joint venture of NBCUniversal and Warner Bros. Discovery). It combines:

- **Rent or buy** — the newest theatrical releases, available digitally weeks before subscription streaming
- **A free section** — thousands of ad-supported movies and shows, no subscription needed
- **Digital ownership** — movies you buy stay in your library, portable via Movies Anywhere

The model is transactional: you pay per title instead of a monthly fee. That makes it the best way to watch a specific new movie without committing to a subscription.

## How Fandango works

1. **Open athome.fandango.com** — no subscription, create a free account.
2. **Browse the free section** — thousands of ad-supported titles, watch immediately.
3. **Or rent a new release** — typically $0.99-$5.99 for a 48-hour rental.
4. **Or buy to own** — $4.99-$24.99, added permanently to your library.
5. **Watch anywhere** — web, mobile, and most smart TVs, in up to 4K.

The free section works like Tubi; the store works like a digital Blockbuster.

## Key features of Fandango

### Rent new releases early

**New theatrical movies available digitally** often weeks before subscription streaming — the headline feature for movie lovers who can't wait.

### A real free section

**Thousands of ad-supported movies and shows** at no cost, no subscription — Fandango has a legit free tier most people don't know about.

### Buy-to-own library

**Movies you purchase stay in your library permanently** and port to other services via Movies Anywhere.

### 4K quality

**Up to 4K UHD with Dolby Vision and Atmos** on supported titles — premium quality for a per-title price.

### No subscription

**Pay only for what you watch** — no monthly commitment, no canceling, no ads on purchases.

## Who is Fandango for?

- **Movie lovers** who want new releases without subscribing.
- **Digital collectors** building a permanent movie library.
- **Cord-cutters** who rent occasionally instead of paying monthly.
- **Anyone** who wants a free section plus premium options in one place.

It's **not** for people who want unlimited browsing on a flat fee — that's Netflix or Disney+. If you only want free streaming, Tubi has a much bigger free catalog.

## Why Fandango is 100% legal

Fandango at Home (formerly Vudu) is owned and operated by **Fandango Media**, which is jointly owned by **Warner Bros. Discovery** and **Comcast** — two of the world's largest media companies. Every title on the platform — whether from the free ad-supported section or paid rent/buy — is fully licensed and paid for through legitimate distribution agreements with movie studios.

There is nothing pirated or user-uploaded anywhere on the platform. The free section works exactly like Tubi: studios license specific titles for ad-supported viewing, and you watch them in an official app. Rentals and purchases are the same legally protected transactions you'd make at any digital storefront. It's a completely legitimate service with no copyright or legal concerns.
