## What is Figma?

Figma is a browser-based design tool for building user interfaces, prototypes, and
design systems. Its defining feature is real-time collaboration — multiple people
can work on the same file at once, the way they would in a shared document.

## Why designers love it

- **Works anywhere** — no installs; open a link and start designing.
- **Real-time collaboration** — teammates, developers, and clients in one canvas.
- **Powerful prototyping** — turn static screens into clickable flows.
- **Huge community** — thousands of free templates, plugins, and UI kits.

## Who is it for?

From solo creators making their first mockup to product teams shipping apps used by
millions, Figma scales to fit. It has become the default tool taught in design
courses and used across the tech industry.

> **Tip:** Explore the Community tab for free files you can duplicate and learn from.
