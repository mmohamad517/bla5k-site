## How TTSMaker works

The workflow is simple:

1. **Paste your text** into the converter.
2. **Choose a language and voice**.
3. **Adjust settings** — speed, pitch as needed.
4. **Generate** — TTSMaker synthesizes the audio.
5. **Download** — save the audio file.

The language selection is where TTSMaker stands out — few free tools offer its coverage.
