## What is Facewow?

Facewow is an **AI face animation tool** that brings still photos of people to life. Its most famous use case is animating old family photos — turning a vintage portrait into a moving, blinking, smiling video.

Core capabilities:

- **Face animation** — realistic facial movements from a still photo
- **Photo restoration animation** — old photos come alive
- **Nostalgia focus** — designed for family and historical photos
- **Lifelike motion** — natural blinking, smiling, and head movement

It became widely popular through emotional videos of animated ancestors — and it works on modern photos too.

## How Facewow works

The workflow is simple:

1. **Upload a photo** — ideally a clear, front-facing portrait.
2. **AI maps the face** — detects features and expressions.
3. **Motion is generated** — realistic facial animation.
4. **Preview** — watch the photo come to life.
5. **Download** — save the animation as a video.

The AI understands facial structure well enough to generate believable, natural movement from a single still image.

## How to use Facewow responsibly

1. **Use your own photos** — or ones you have permission to use.
2. **Get consent** — especially for living people.
3. **Don't mislead** — clearly share as AI animation.
4. **Respect the subject** — avoid using it to mock or distort.
5. **Check terms** — for commercial or public use.
