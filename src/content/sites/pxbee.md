Most photo edits are the same few tasks — brighten, clean up, remove something, retouch. **PxBee** packages them into one-click AI tools: enhance, retouch, and transform images in your browser without learning a complex editor. This guide covers what PxBee does and how to use it.

## What is PxBee?

PxBee is an **AI photo editor with smart, one-click tools**. Instead of a full editor with layers and masks, it offers focused AI operations that handle the most common edits automatically.

Core tools:

- **AI enhancement** — brightness, sharpness, and quality improvements
- **Object removal** — erase unwanted elements
- **Background tools** — removal and changes
- **Retouching** — clean up and polish photos

It's built for people who want good results without editing skills — the AI does the heavy lifting.

## How PxBee works

The workflow is one-click per edit:

1. **Upload a photo**.
2. **Choose a tool** — enhance, remove object, change background.
3. **AI processes it** — the edit is applied automatically.
4. **Preview and adjust** — see the result.
5. **Download** — save your edited image.

Each tool is a focused operation — no timelines, no layers, no learning curve.
