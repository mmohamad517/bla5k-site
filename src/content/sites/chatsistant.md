## What is Chatsistant?

Chatsistant is an AI chatbot platform that uses a generation framework, combining GPT‑4, Gemini, and Claude. It ingests PDFs, spreadsheets, websites, and YouTube transcripts, vectorizes them, and lets agents answer queries while triggering APIs and integrating with Zapier and Make.
