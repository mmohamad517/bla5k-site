## What is MuleRun?

MuleRun is an **AI agent platform for web research and data extraction**. Instead of a chatbot that talks about doing research, MuleRun provides agents that actually do it — browsing websites, extracting information, and delivering structured data.

Core capabilities:

- **Data extraction** — collect pricing, features, contacts, and more
- **Web research** — multi-site research at scale
- **Structured output** — deliver data in spreadsheets and organized formats
- **Task automation** — replace hours of manual browsing

It's built for businesses and researchers who repeatedly need the same kinds of data — and are tired of gathering it by hand.

## How MuleRun works

The workflow replaces manual research with a task description:

1. **Describe the task** — "find pricing for 50 competitors," "collect contact info for 100 companies."
2. **Set your criteria** — what data you need and any filters.
3. **MuleRun agents work** — they browse websites and extract the data.
4. **Get structured results** — a spreadsheet or organized output.
5. **Review and use** — spot-check and put the data to work.

The agent does the boring part — visiting sites, finding information, and organizing it — while you define the task and use the results.
