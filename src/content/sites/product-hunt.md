## What is Product Hunt?

Product Hunt is a community where makers share the products they just launched and
the community votes on what’s good. Every day brings a fresh, ranked feed of new
apps, tools, and websites — making it the best place to catch what’s next.

## How it works

- **Daily leaderboard** — the day’s launches, ranked by community upvotes.
- **Maker stories** — founders explain what they built and why.
- **Discussion** — real feedback from early adopters in the comments.

## Why it belongs in this directory

If you want to stay ahead of the curve, Product Hunt is essential. Many of the tools
featured elsewhere in this guide first appeared here. Bookmark it and check in daily
to spot tomorrow’s must-have tool before everyone else.
