Finding music for your videos is a nightmare: copyright claims, licensing fees, and hearing the same track in everyone else's content. **Mubert** offers a different route — AI generates *original* royalty-free music matched to your mood, so your video gets a unique soundtrack without the legal minefield. It's become a go-to for creators, streamers, and app developers.

This guide covers what Mubert is, how it works, its pricing, and its honest limits.

## What is Mubert?

Mubert is an **AI music generation platform** focused on royalty-free, instrumental music. You select a mood, genre, and duration, and Mubert creates a track — or generates music live, in real time, matching your inputs.

Its audience: anyone who needs background music — video creators, streamers, podcasters, fitness instructors, app and game developers — and wants it fast, unique, and legally clean.

## How Mubert works

1. **Choose a style** — mood, genre, and energy level.
2. **Set the length** — or generate endlessly in live mode.
3. **Generate** — Mubert's AI composes a unique track from its sound database.
4. **Download and license** — export with rights matched to your plan.

The AI arranges real sound samples into new compositions, so each track is original — not a looped library file everyone else uses.

## Who is Mubert for?

## Pricing

Mubert is **freemium**: a free tier for personal projects and limited generation (with attribution or limitations), and paid plans — including one-time track purchases and subscriptions — with full commercial licensing, unlimited generation, and higher-quality exports. Check mubert.com for the current structure.

## Advantages

- **Royalty-free by design** — built for creators' legal peace of mind.
- **Original music every time** — no generic library tracks.
- **Mood matching** — music that fits the content's energy.
- **Live generation** — perfect for streams.
- **API** for apps and games.

## Limitations and honest considerations

- **Instrumental only** — no vocals or lyric songs.
- **Electronic-leaning** — strongest in electronic and ambient styles, not orchestral or acoustic realism.
- **Free tier limits** — commercial use needs paid plans.
- **Not a songwriting tool** — it's background music, not hit singles.
