There's one streaming service the whole family agrees on: **Disney+** — the exclusive home of Marvel, Star Wars, Pixar, Disney classics, and National Geographic, now bundling with Hulu for serious value. This guide covers pricing, what you get, and how it compares to the other big subscriptions.

## What is Disney+?

Disney+ is **The Walt Disney Company's streaming service** — the exclusive digital home of its biggest franchises:

- **Disney animation** — the classics and new releases
- **Pixar** — every film from Toy Story to the latest
- **Marvel** — the complete Marvel Cinematic Universe
- **Star Wars** — every film and series
- **National Geographic** — documentaries and nature content

With the **Hulu bundle**, subscribers also get Hulu's library of series, movies, and originals in the same subscription.

## How Disney+ works

1. **Choose a plan** — standalone or the Disney+ and Hulu bundle.
2. **Create profiles** — with parental controls for kids.
3. **Browse by franchise** — Marvel, Star Wars, Pixar, Disney, Nat Geo.
4. **Stream on up to 4 screens** — 4K on premium plans.
5. **Download offline** — for travel and road trips.

## Key features of Disney+

### Exclusive franchises

**Marvel, Star Wars, Pixar, Disney, and National Geographic** — the only place to stream these complete catalogs.

### Hulu bundle value

**Both services for $12.99/month with ads** — the bundle is significantly cheaper than buying Disney+ and Hulu separately.

### Family-first parental controls

**Profile locking and content-rating restrictions** — the best parental controls of any major service.

### 4K with Dolby

**Premium plan streams in 4K UHD with Dolby Vision and Atmos** on supported titles.

### Downloads everywhere

**Offline downloads** on mobile and tablet — perfect for kids on road trips.

## Who is Disney+ for?

- **Families with kids** — the safest, most kid-friendly catalog.
- **Marvel and Star Wars fans** — every title in one place.
- **Pixar and Disney animation lovers** — the complete library.
- **Hulu subscribers** — the bundle adds Disney+ cheaply.

It's **not** for viewers who want varied adult drama on standalone plans (the catalog skews family until you add Hulu), or for budget users who'd be fine with free services.

## Why Disney+ is 100% legal

Disney+ is operated by **The Walt Disney Company** — one of the world's largest and most established entertainment corporations.
