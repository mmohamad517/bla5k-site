The fastest way to use AI isn't opening a separate tab — it's having it **where you already are**. **Monica** is a Chrome extension that brings GPT-5, Claude, and Gemini to any webpage, with instant explain, rewrite, translate, and summarize on highlighted text, plus image generation. This guide covers what Monica does, how it compares to similar tools, and whether it belongs in your browser.

For more AI productivity tools, explore our [best AI tools in 2026](/guides/best-ai-tools-2026/) roundup and the [AI Tools directory](/category/ai-tools/).

## What is Monica?

Monica is an **all-in-one AI assistant as a browser extension**. It launched in 2022 and grew into one of the most popular AI extensions on the Chrome Web Store, with millions of users. Its promise: the power of the best AI models, available anywhere on the web, with zero context switching.

At its core, Monica provides:

- **Text assistance** — explain, rewrite, summarize, translate any selection
- **Sidebar chat** — a full AI conversation without leaving your page
- **Image generation** — create images from text prompts
- **Multi-model access** — GPT, Claude, Gemini, and more
- **AI writing tools** — email drafts, social posts, and content ideas

It's designed for people who want AI woven into their daily browsing and writing, rather than a separate destination.

## How Monica works

The workflow is frictionless:

1. **Install the extension** from the Chrome Web Store.
2. **Highlight any text** on a page — an email, an article, a message.
3. **Pick an action** — explain, rewrite, translate, or summarize.
4. **Monica responds instantly** in a popup beside your selection.
5. **Or open the assistant** — chat, generate images, or get writing help.

You never leave the page you're on. Highlight → action → done, with the AI's answer right where you're looking.

## Key features of Monica

### Instant text actions

Explain, rewrite, translate, and summarize **any highlighted text** in one click — the daily-driver feature for writers and readers.

### Sidebar chat with multi-model access

A full **AI chat sidebar** with GPT-5, Claude, and Gemini behind it, so you can ask deep questions or draft content without switching tabs.

### Image generation built in

Generate **images from text** with Monica's own image model — handy for quick visuals without a separate tool.

### AI writing and email tools

Templates and one-click generation for **emails, social posts, and content**, plus a grammar and style assistant for polishing your own writing.

### A generous free tier

Around **40 free queries per day**, which is one of the more generous free allowances among AI extensions.

## Monica vs. Sider vs. other extensions

| | Monica | Sider | ChatGPT extension |

| Text actions on selection | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | Limited |

**Monica** is the strongest choice when writing and image generation matter. **Sider** offers more models and page-level summaries. Both are excellent; your pick depends on whether you lean writing (Monica) or reading/research (Sider).

## Who is Monica for?

- **Writers and content creators** who edit text constantly.
- **Researchers** digesting web content.
- **Multilingual users** translating on the fly.
- **Professionals** who want AI help without leaving their work.

It's **not** for Firefox/Safari users (Chrome/Edge only), or for users who burn through 40 queries a day on the free tier. For heavy conversational use, a dedicated chatbot like [DeepSeek](/tools/deepseek/) is a better fit.

## How to get started with Monica

1. **Install Monica** from the Chrome Web Store.
2. **Highlight some text** on any page and try explain or rewrite.
3. **Open the sidebar** to chat or generate an image.
4. **Customize your default model** in settings.
5. **Use the writing templates** for emails and social posts.
6. **Upgrade** only if you hit the free daily quota regularly.

Start with the text actions — they're the fastest demonstration of value. Then explore image generation and chat as you get comfortable.
