Digitizing a real object into 3D used to mean expensive scanners and specialized skills. **3DPresso** replaces both with something you already own: your phone camera. Film an object for a minute, upload the clip, and the AI reconstructs it as a usable 3D model. This guide covers how it works and who it's for.

## What is 3DPresso?

3DPresso is an **AI-powered 3D modeling service that generates 3D models from video**. Instead of typing a text prompt, you capture a real object on video and let the AI turn the footage into a digital asset.

Its core promise:

- **Phone-first capture** — no scanner, no rig, just video
- **Real objects, real detail** — texture and shape from actual footage
- **Editable output** — rotate, refine, and export the model
- **No 3D skills required** — the AI does the reconstruction

For product teams and creators, it closes the gap between the physical world and digital assets.

## How 3DPresso works

The process is built around a simple capture step:

1. **Film the object** — a 1–2 minute video, circling slowly with even lighting.
2. **Upload the clip** — the AI processes the footage.
3. **The model is reconstructed** — shape and texture built from the video frames.
4. **Edit and export** — refine the result and export for your use case.

Everything from reconstruction to export happens on the platform — no photogrammetry software, no manual mesh work.

## Key features of 3DPresso

### Video-to-3D reconstruction

The core trick is **using video instead of a single image** — dozens of angles give the AI the coverage to reconstruct accurate shape and surface detail.

### Mobile-friendly capture

Any **phone camera** works. There's no special hardware, which makes it practical for product teams, designers, and creators on the go.

### Editable 3D assets

The output isn't a dead-end render — it's an **editable 3D model** you can rotate, refine, and export for your pipeline.

### Practical use cases built in

3DPresso is aimed at **fashion, furniture, e-commerce, and product digitization** — the industries that most need real objects as digital assets.

### No learning curve

The whole workflow is **upload-and-wait**, so someone who has never opened 3D software can produce a usable model on the first try.

**3DPresso is the right tool when you have a real object** — text-to-3D invents objects, while 3DPresso digitizes the ones you actually have. Professional photogrammetry is more precise but far more demanding; 3DPresso trades a bit of precision for a workflow anyone can do.

## Who is 3DPresso for?

- **E-commerce teams** digitizing products for catalogs and AR previews.
- **Fashion and furniture designers** capturing physical pieces.
- **Creators** building 3D scenes from real-world objects.
- **Designers** who need 3D assets without learning modeling software.

It's **not** for generating imagined 3D from prompts (use Meshy or Tripo), and not for engineering-grade scans — it's the fast, accessible middle ground.

## How to get started with 3DPresso

1. **Open 3dpresso.ai** — create a free account.
2. **Film your object** — 1–2 minutes, slow circle, good light.
3. **Upload and wait** — the AI reconstructs the model.
4. **Edit the result** — rotate, refine, and clean up.
5. **Export** — for product pages, AR, or your 3D pipeline.

Start with a simple, well-lit object — the quality of your capture determines the quality of the model.
