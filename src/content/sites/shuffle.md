## What is Shuffle?

Shuffle is an AI‑powered chat builder that creates responsive UIs with Tailwind, Bootstrap, Material‑UI, or Bulma. It offers a 13k‑component library, 80+ front‑end frameworks, SVG generation, VS Code integration, and JSX/TSX export for React, supporting team collaboration and version control.

In short, Shuffle is built around one clear promise: take your input — a prompt, a file, or a task — and return a usable result for AI web development without the manual grind. For plenty of users, that one capability of Shuffle replaces several steps they used to do by hand.
