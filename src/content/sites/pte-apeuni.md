## What is PTE APEUni?

PTE APEUni is an **AI-powered preparation platform for the Pearson Test of English (PTE)**. It combines realistic practice tests with instant AI scoring — the same kind of automated scoring the real exam uses — to help candidates improve efficiently.

Core features:

- **Realistic practice tests** — mirroring the real exam
- **Instant AI scoring** — speaking, writing, and more
- **Detailed feedback** — what to improve and how
- **Exam strategy** — question-type mastery

For the thousands who take PTE annually for study and migration, it's a practical way to prepare.

## How PTE APEUni works

The practice loop is immediate:

1. **Choose a practice test or question type**.
2. **Complete it** — in the real exam format.
3. **Get instant AI scores** — speaking, writing, and listening.
4. **Read the feedback** — specific improvement tips.
5. **Practice the weak areas** — and retest.

Because scoring is instant, you can iterate rapidly — practice, learn, improve, repeat.
