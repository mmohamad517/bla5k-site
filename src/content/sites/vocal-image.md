## What is Vocal Image?

Vocal Image is an **AI voice coach** that analyzes spoken audio and provides feedback on vocal delivery. It's built for people who speak professionally — presenters, interviewees, podcasters — and want objective feedback without hiring a coach.

Core analysis areas:

- **Pacing** — speed and rhythm of speech
- **Clarity** — diction and articulation
- **Energy** — vocal presence and dynamism
- **Confidence** — delivery and conviction

The feedback is immediate, objective, and actionable — you can practice, record, and improve in one session.

## How Vocal Image works

The workflow is record → feedback:

1. **Record yourself** — a presentation, answer, or passage.
2. **AI analyzes** your vocal qualities.
3. **Get feedback** — scores and tips on pacing, clarity, energy.
4. **Practice** — apply the feedback.
5. **Re-record** — measure your improvement.

It's like having a coach listen to every practice session — without scheduling or fees.
