Homework gets hard, and the hardest part is usually *how* — not just what the answer is. **Solvely** is the AI homework solver built around explanations: photograph a math or science problem and get a detailed, step-by-step walkthrough that teaches you the method. This guide covers how Solvely works and how to use it well.

## What is Solvely?

Solvely is an **AI-powered homework solver for math and science** that prioritizes detailed explanations. Its design philosophy: an answer without the method teaches nothing — so every solution is a full walkthrough of the steps and reasoning.

Core features:

- **Photo problem input** — snap and solve
- **Step-by-step explanations** — the method, not just the result
- **STEM focus** — math and science coverage
- **Learning-first design** — built to teach, not to do your homework

It's aimed at students who want to understand their homework — and at parents helping kids with subjects they've forgotten.

## How Solvely works

The workflow is instant:

1. **Take a photo** of your problem.
2. **Solvely reads it** — equations and text recognized.
3. **It solves step by step** — with explanations of each step.
4. **You study the method** — understand the reasoning.
5. **You apply it** — solve similar problems yourself.

The explanation is the product. Solvely is designed so that after using it, you can solve the next problem without it.

## Key features of Solvely

### Photo-based input

**Snap a photo** of handwritten or printed problems — no typing equations.

### Detailed step-by-step explanations

Every solution breaks down into **clear steps with reasoning**, teaching the method behind the answer.

### Math and science coverage

Built for **STEM homework** — algebra, calculus, physics, chemistry, and related subjects.

### Learning-first design

Focused on **understanding the method** so you can apply it independently — a study tool, not an answer service.

### Mobile-friendly

Available where homework happens — **on your phone**, ready when you're stuck.

## Who is Solvely for?

- **Students** stuck on STEM homework.
- **Exam preparers** who need to understand methods.
- **Parents** helping with unfamiliar subjects.
- **Self-learners** working through textbooks.

It's **not** for general knowledge questions (use [ChatGPT](/tools/chatgpt/) or [DeepSeek](/tools/deepseek/)), or for users hunting answers to copy — the value is in the explanation, and skipping it defeats the purpose.

## How to use Solvely responsibly

1. **Attempt the problem first** — Solvely is for getting unstuck.
2. **Read every step** — understand the reasoning, not just the result.
3. **Close the app and retry** — solve a similar problem yourself.
4. **Use it to verify** — check your own work after solving.
5. **Know your school's policy** — AI use rules vary.

The step-by-step explanations are the point. Use them to learn the method, and the next problem gets easier on its own.
