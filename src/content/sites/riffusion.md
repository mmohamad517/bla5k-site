## What is Riffusion?

Riffusion is a free, open-source AI music generator that takes a unique approach to creating audio. Rather than generating sound directly, it uses stable diffusion to create spectrogram images — visual representations of audio — and then converts those images into playable music. This creative bridge between image AI and audio synthesis was one of the earliest demonstrations of using diffusion models for music.

The platform requires no login, no download, and no payment. Open the website, type a description, and hear music within seconds.

## Key features

**Instant generation** with no account required — the lowest-friction entry point to AI music generation available.

**Prompt interpolation** allows smooth transitions between different musical styles, creating gradual morphing effects between genres or moods.

**Open-source codebase** on GitHub means developers can inspect, modify, and build on the technology for their own projects.

**Visual feedback** through spectrograms lets you see the music as it's being generated, providing an educational and intuitive view of what the AI is creating.

## Limitations

Riffusion generates relatively short audio clips rather than full-length songs. It doesn't support vocal generation with lyrics. And while it was groundbreaking when it launched in 2022, newer models like [ACE-Step 1.5](/tools/ace-step-ui/) and [Stable Audio](/tools/stable-audio/) have surpassed it in audio quality and capability.

## Who is Riffusion for?

Riffusion is perfect for quick, free experimentation — trying out ideas, exploring AI music for the first time, or building on an open-source foundation. For serious music production with vocals, full-length tracks, and advanced controls, [ACE-Step UI](/tools/ace-step-ui/), [Suno](/tools/suno-ai/), or [Udio](/tools/udio/) are better choices.

## Conclusion

Riffusion remains a valuable entry point to AI music generation: free, instant, and open source. While it's been surpassed in raw capability by newer tools, its zero-friction approach and unique spectrogram-based technology make it worth trying for anyone curious about AI music.
