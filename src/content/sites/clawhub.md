## What is ClawHub AI?

ClawHub AI is an **AI agent marketplace and deployment platform**. It serves two roles:

- **Marketplace** — find AI agents built by developers for common tasks
- **Deployment** — run those agents without managing servers or infrastructure

The model mirrors app stores: instead of building every agent yourself, you browse existing ones, pick the closest match, customize it, and put it to work. For businesses that want AI automation without an engineering team, it lowers the barrier dramatically.

## How ClawHub works

The workflow is marketplace-style:

1. **Browse the marketplace** — agents for support, content, data, and more.
2. **Pick an agent** — find one matching your task.
3. **Customize** — configure it with your data and requirements.
4. **Deploy** — ClawHub runs it for you.
5. **Monitor** — watch it automate your workflows.

The platform handles the infrastructure — running, scaling, and maintaining the agent — so you focus on the task, not the plumbing.

## Who is ClawHub for?

- **Businesses** wanting AI automation without engineering overhead.
