## What is Hailuo AI?

Hailuo AI is the **creative generation suite from MiniMax** ([minimax.io](https://www.minimax.io/)), a major Chinese AI company. It offers:

- **Text-to-video** — describe a scene, get a cinematic clip
- **Image-to-video** — animate a static image into motion
- **Image generation** — create visuals from text prompts
- **Voice cloning and TTS** — studio-grade audio and voices

The video quality is the headline: Hailuo is consistently ranked among the best AI video generators for **motion realism** — how naturally objects move, how cameras follow prompts, and how physics behave in the frame.

## How Hailuo AI works

The workflow is simple enough for anyone:

1. **Open hailuoai.video** — no download needed, it runs in the browser.
2. **Describe a scene** — or upload an image to animate.
3. **Generate** — Hailuo renders a short clip (seconds long).
4. **Review and iterate** — refine the prompt for better results.
5. **For audio** — use the same account for TTS and voice cloning.

The free tier lets you experiment; paid plans unlock resolution, speed, and credits.

## Key features of Hailuo AI

### Cinematic text-to-video

The core feature: **describe a scene, get a cinematic 1080p clip** with strong motion physics and camera-movement control. It's built for the "wow" factor that short-form creators need.

### Image-to-video

Turn **static photos into animated clips** — give a still image motion, with the AI extrapolating natural movement.

### Voice cloning and TTS

**Studio-grade text-to-speech and voice cloning** from the same suite — a complete audio + video package for creators and marketers.

### One-click video creation

Generate polished clips from text or photos with **minimal manual editing** — ideal for social content, ads, and prototypes.
