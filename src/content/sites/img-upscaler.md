## How to get the best results

1. **Start with the highest quality original** you have.
2. **Upscale in reasonable steps** — 2x often looks better than jumping to 4x.
3. **Check the result at actual size** — not zoomed out.
4. **For critical work** — compare against the original before replacing it.
5. **Use for print and display** — where size matters most.

The better your source image, the better the upscale — garbage in, slightly-less-garbage out is the honest rule for any upscaler.
