Search has a new shape: instead of ten blue links, you get **one answer with sources**. **Perplexity AI** is the most famous version of this — an answer engine that synthesizes real-time web results into clear, cited responses, free for unlimited basic searches. If research is part of your day, this guide explains why Perplexity might replace half your tab-switching.

## What is Perplexity?

Perplexity is an **AI-powered answer engine** — a conversational search tool that answers your question with a synthesized summary drawn from live web results, with sources cited inline. Launched in 2022, it has become one of the most successful AI startups, positioning itself as the "answer engine" alternative to traditional search.

The core loop is simple:

- You **ask a question** in natural language.
- Perplexity **searches the web in real time**.
- It **writes an answer** with the relevant sources cited.
- You **verify** — each claim links to its source.

It's search for people who want answers, not lists.

## How Perplexity works

1. **Open perplexity.ai** — free, unlimited basic searches.
2. **Ask anything** — a question, a comparison, a research topic.
3. **Read the sourced answer** — synthesized from live web results.
4. **Click the citations** — verify claims directly.
5. **For deeper work** — upload files, use Pro Mode, or run Deep Research.

The answers come with **transparent footnotes and source links**, which is the feature that sets it apart: you don't have to take the AI's word for anything.

## Key features of Perplexity

### Real-time sourced search

The core feature: **instant answers with citations**. Every claim is backed by a source link you can click, making it dramatically more trustworthy than a bare chatbot answer.

### Pro Mode and Deep Research

**Multi-step autonomous research agents** — break a complex question into parts, run follow-up searches, and compile a comprehensive report. A powerful tool for serious research and analysis.

### File uploads and Collections

Upload **PDFs, CSVs, and images** for analysis, and organize research threads into **Collections** for persistent workspace management.

### Model choice on Pro

Pro users can **toggle between frontier models** (Claude, GPT, and more) alongside Perplexity's own Sonar models — pick the best model for each job.

### Free unlimited searches

The **free tier has unlimited basic searches**, which covers the daily research needs of most users.

## Perplexity vs. ChatGPT vs. Google

| | Perplexity | ChatGPT | Google |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Real-time sources | ✅ Always cited | Limited | Links only |
| Answer format | Synthesized + sources | Conversational | List of links |
| Free tier | ✅ Unlimited search | Limited | ✅ |
| Deep research | ✅ Pro | ✅ Pro | ❌ |

**Perplexity** wins when you need *verified* answers from the live web — research, comparisons, current events. **[ChatGPT](/tools/chatgpt/)** is stronger for creative writing and general conversation. **Google** remains the place for browsing and discovery. For "just answer me, with sources" — Perplexity is the specialist.

## Who is Perplexity for?

- **Researchers and analysts** — sourced answers and deep research.
- **Journalists** — quick verification and fact-checking.
- **Students** — study help with citations.
- **Anyone** tired of clicking through search results.

It's **not** a creative writing tool, not a coding IDE (use [Claude Code](/tools/claude-code/) or [Aider](/tools/aider/) for that), and privacy-conscious users should weigh the fact that queries are processed by an AI platform.
