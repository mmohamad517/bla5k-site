# FreedomGPT: Open-Source Private AI That Runs Locally

FreedomGPT is for users who want AI without compromise: no tracking, no data collection, no corporate servers, no censorship. It's an open-source chatbot that runs entirely on your own device, giving you access to 400+ models while keeping everything private.

## What is FreedomGPT?

An open-source AI platform that runs locally on Windows, Mac, Linux, or mobile. Access hundreds of uncensored and open-source AI models without sending data to external servers. Your conversations never leave your device.

## Key Features

**Local Execution**: Download and run on your computer. No internet required (after initial model download).

**400+ Models**: Uncensored variants of major models, open-source options, and proprietary models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and others.

**Complete Privacy**: No tracking, no account required, no data collection. What you ask stays on your device.

**Model Comparison**: Side-by-side comparison with voting to evaluate model quality.

**Multi-Platform**: Windows, Mac, Linux, iOS, Android, and web access.

**No Censorship**: Access to uncensored model variants without content filters (use responsibly).

## Considerations

- **No moderation**: Content filters are disabled, which means models can produce harmful outputs
- **Security responsibility**: Running local models requires understanding security implications
- **Hardware requirements**: Larger models need significant GPU memory
- **Maintenance**: You're responsible for keeping models updated and secure

## Best For

Privacy-first users, researchers, developers, and anyone uncomfortable with corporate data collection.
