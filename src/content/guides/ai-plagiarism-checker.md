## What is an AI plagiarism checker?

An AI plagiarism checker is a tool that combines traditional plagiarism detection with AI content detection. It scans text against existing content databases to find matches, while also analyzing writing patterns to determine if AI generated the text. The best AI plagiarism checker tools handle both tasks in one scan, making them essential for educators, publishers, and content teams.

## How AI detection works

AI content detectors analyze text patterns that differ between human and machine writing. Human writing has varied sentence lengths, unexpected word choices, and natural rhythm. AI-generated text tends toward predictable sentence structures, common transitions, and uniform paragraph lengths.

The best AI plagiarism checker tools combine multiple detection methods:

- **Perplexity analysis** — measures how predictable the text is. AI text has lower perplexity (it is more predictable).
- **Burstiness analysis** — measures sentence length variation. AI text has more uniform lengths.
- **Pattern recognition** — identifies characteristic patterns from specific AI models like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini.
- **Database matching** — for plagiarism detection, compares text against indexed content to find matches.

### Limitations of AI detectors

No AI content detector is 100% accurate. False positives occur with:
- Non-native English writing that follows formulaic patterns
- Highly structured content like technical documentation
- Short text samples below 100 words
- AI text that has been heavily edited by a human

For important decisions, test suspected text on multiple AI detectors and consider other evidence before concluding.
