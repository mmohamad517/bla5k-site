# Duck.ai by DuckDuckGo: The Privacy-Focused AI Assistant in 2026

If you care about privacy but want access to the world's best AI models, Duck.ai solves a problem that shouldn't exist but does: How do you use powerful AI without your conversations being mined for training data, stored for advertising, or handled by companies with questionable privacy practices?

Duck.ai is DuckDuckGo's answer. It's a front-end that lets you access multiple AI models — ChatGPT, Claude, Llama — while keeping your prompts anonymous and your conversations completely private. No sign-up, no tracking, no account needed.

## What is Duck.ai?

Duck.ai is a privacy-focused AI chat interface built by DuckDuckGo. It acts as an anonymizing layer between you and various AI model providers. When you ask Duck.ai a question, your prompt is anonymized before being sent to the actual model (Claude, ChatGPT, Llama, Mistral). The model responds to DuckDuckGo (not you), DuckDuckGo sends the response to you, and no permanent record of "you" exists anywhere.

It's the AI equivalent of DuckDuckGo Search — you get the service without the surveillance.

## Key Features

**No Sign-Up Required**: Just visit duck.ai and start typing. No account, no email, no form. Open your browser and use immediately.

**Model Selection**: Choose which AI model to use for each conversation:
- **Free tier**: Claude 3.5 Haiku, Llama 4 Scout, Mistral Small 3, GPT-4o mini
- **Paid tier**: GPT-5.2, GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Llama 4 Maverick

**Encrypted Voice Chat**: DuckDuckGo added encrypted real-time voice chat in February 2026. Speak naturally, and the AI responds via audio. Audio streams through encrypted WebRTC relay, is processed ephemerally, and never stored or used for training.

**Anonymized Prompts**: Your questions are anonymized before reaching the AI provider, ensuring that no company building your profile connects "this is what person X asked" to you.

**Works in Any Browser**: No app to install, no software to download. Uses your existing browser, whether that's Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or anything else.

**Mobile Friendly**: Works perfectly on phones. Type or use voice and get responses immediately.

## What Duck.ai Doesn't Do

**No Web Search**: Duck.ai can't browse the web. It can't look up current events, fetch URLs, or access live information.

**No File Uploads**: You can't upload PDFs, documents, or images for analysis.

**No Image Generation**: It's pure text-to-text and voice-to-voice chat. No image generation.

**No Persistent Memory**: Each conversation is isolated. The AI doesn't remember previous chats or build long-term context about you.

**No Integrations**: It doesn't connect to other tools or services. It's standalone chat only.

These limitations are intentional. DuckDuckGo's philosophy is: do privacy well, keep the scope narrow, don't collect data.

## Privacy Deep Dive

**How the Anonymization Works:**

When you send a prompt to Duck.ai:
1. Your browser connects to DuckDuckGo (encrypted)
2. DuckDuckGo strips identifying information (IP address, device ID, etc.)
3. DuckDuckGo sends your anonymized prompt to the AI provider
4. The AI responds to DuckDuckGo's server (not to your IP)
5. DuckDuckGo forwards the response to you
6. No database entry exists linking "you" to "this prompt"

**What DuckDuckGo Collects:**
- Minimal analytics (how many prompts sent, broad usage patterns)
- Not your prompts, not your responses
- No IP logging, no device tracking
- No advertising correlation

**What Stays Private:**
- Your questions and requests
- Your voice (in voice chat mode)
- Your identity
- Your browsing/AI history
- Any personal information in your prompts

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Duck.ai secure?

Yes. All connections are encrypted, prompts are anonymized, and voice is encrypted end-to-end. DuckDuckGo has no structural incentive to collect data.

### Can DuckDuckGo see my prompts?

No. The anonymization strips identifying information before the prompt reaches the AI model provider. DuckDuckGo's servers facilitate the connection but don't store your actual questions.

### Is the free tier really unlimited?

Yes. No message limits, no daily restrictions, no usage caps. The only difference is which AI models you can access.

### What if I want to save conversations?

Duck.ai doesn't offer this natively. You can screenshot or copy-paste conversations, but there's no built-in save or export feature.

### How is Duck.ai different from just using ChatGPT in Incognito Mode?

Incognito mode only hides from your browser history. ChatGPT still stores conversations on their servers, tracks your IP, and can identify patterns about you. Duck.ai doesn't store anything or have any ability to identify you.

### Can I use Duck.ai for commercial purposes?

Yes. The terms of service allow commercial use.

### Why would I pay for Duck.ai when ChatGPT is free?

For access to stronger models (GPT-5.2, Claude Sonnet) while maintaining anonymity. You're paying for privacy + model access, not for the chat interface itself.

## The Verdict

Duck.ai is the best option if privacy is your non-negotiable requirement. It's not the most feature-rich — it lacks file uploads, web search, and conversation history. But if your primary concern is having powerful AI conversations without being tracked, profiled, or mined for training data, Duck.ai is unmatched.

It's DuckDuckGo's philosophy applied to AI: you deserve powerful tools without surveillance. In 2026, that's increasingly rare.

For sensitive work, confidential research, or anyone uncomfortable with corporate data collection, Duck.ai is worth your time.
