# Monica: ChatGPT Chrome Extension and All-in-One AI Assistant

Monica is a Chrome extension that gives you instant access to GPT-5, Claude 4.7 Opus, Gemini 3.1 Pro, and more AI models with a smart toolbar that appears when you highlight text anywhere on the web.

## What is Monica?

A Chrome extension that brings multi-model AI assistance directly into your browser. Highlight text and get instant AI suggestions without leaving the page.

## Key Features

**Smart Toolbar**: Highlight text anywhere, and Monica's toolbar appears with AI actions.

**Multi-Model Chat**: Switch between GPT-5, Claude 4.5, Gemini 3 Pro, and others for different perspectives.

**Webpage Summarization**: Condense long articles into key points instantly.

**Translation**: Translate full websites or selected text between dozens of languages.

**Content Rewriting**: Adjust tone, length, formality, and style with one click.

**Image Generation**: Create images directly from the chat interface.

**Sora Video Generation**: Generate videos (Pro tier).

**Chat Interface**: Full conversational AI chat within the extension.

## Pricing

**Free**: 40 basic queries/day, unlimited basic queries, page summarization, website translation.

**Pro** ($9.90/month): 300 premium queries/day, access to all premium models.

**Pro+** ($19.90/month): Unlimited queries, priority processing, all features.

## Best For

- Researchers reading lots of content
- Writers needing AI assistance
- Multi-language work
- Getting multiple AI perspectives

## How It Works

1. Install Monica from Chrome Web Store
2. Browse any website
3. Highlight text → Monica toolbar appears
4. Choose an AI action (summarize, rewrite, translate, etc.)
5. Get instant results

## Why Monica vs Standalone ChatGPT?

- **No tab switching**: AI is right there in your sidebar
- **Context from webpage**: Ask about the page you're reading
- **Multiple models**: Compare responses from different AIs
- **Cheaper**: Pro tier ($9.90) vs ChatGPT Plus ($20)
- **More tools**: Image generation, translation, summarization all included

## Limitations

- **Chrome-only**: Works on Chrome and Edge, not Firefox or Safari
- **Limited free tier**: 40 queries/day may not be enough for heavy users
- **Requires extension**: Can't use on all websites due to extension restrictions
