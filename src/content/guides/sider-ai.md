# Sider: The AI Sidebar for Your Browser Productivity

Sider puts a persistent AI assistant sidebar in your browser. Highlight text anywhere on the web, and instantly get AI-powered summaries, translations, rewrites, or explanations without opening new tabs or switching tools.

## What is Sider?

A browser extension (Chrome, Edge) with 30+ AI models in a sidebar that stays with you across any website, document, or video.

## Key Features

**Persistent Sidebar**: Access AI instantly on any webpage without opening new tabs.

**30+ AI Models**: GPT-4o, Claude Sonnet, Gemini Pro, DeepSeek-R1, and 20+ others.

**Instant Summarization**: Collapse long articles into key points.

**Smart Actions**: Rewrite, explain, translate, extract, and more with one click.

**Video Support**: Summarize YouTube videos by URL.

**Web Context**: Ask questions about the page you're on.

**Multi-Language**: Supports dozens of languages.

**No Context Switching**: Everything happens in the sidebar.

## Pricing

**Free**: Limited daily queries, basic models.

**Unlimited** ($16.70/month): Unlimited queries, access to all 30+ models, priority processing.

Users report saving 3.5+ hours per week.

## Best For

Researchers, content creators, writers, and anyone spending time reading and processing web content.

## Key Benefit

Never leave the page you're reading. Get AI assistance right there in the sidebar.
