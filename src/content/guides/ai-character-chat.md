## How to create a great AI character

1. **Define a clear personality** — specific traits create more engaging conversations than vague descriptions. "A cynical detective who speaks in short sentences" is better than "a detective."
2. **Write a strong greeting** — the first message sets the tone. Show the character's personality through their opening words, not just their description.
3. **Add example conversations** — showing the AI how the character should respond to different situations improves consistency dramatically.
4. **Give them knowledge boundaries** — a historical character should not know about modern technology unless you want that. Define what the character knows and does not know.
5. **Iterate based on conversations** — the first version of a character is rarely perfect. Chat with it, note where the personality breaks, and refine the definition.
