# HotBot: Free AI Assistant with Multiple AI Models

HotBot brings together major AI models (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, Llama) in one interface without requiring sign-up or payment.

## What is HotBot?

A free browser-based AI platform providing instant access to Claude 3.0 (Haiku, Opus, Sonnet), ChatGPT 4 and o1, DeepSeek V3 and R1, Google Gemini 1.0 Pro and 1.5 Pro, and Meta Llama 3 models — all without registration.

## Key Features

**Multi-Model Access**: Choose from 6+ major AI models for each conversation.

**No Signup Required**: Use immediately without account creation or email.

**Private Chats**: Data privacy features keep conversations confidential.

**Multi-Modal**: Text, images, and voice input support.

**Image Generation**: Create images directly in chat.

**Context Memory**: Maintains conversation history for nuanced discussions.

**Browser Extension**: Chrome extension for access anywhere.

## Pricing

**Free**: Generous limits on AI model access.

**Premium**: Unlimited messaging and advanced features.

## Best For

Users wanting free access to multiple AI models without account fragmentation.
